Most roofs give you warning before they fail. The trouble is that the early
warnings are easy to miss from the ground, and the obvious ones usually arrive
after water has already found its way inside.

## Start in the attic, not on the roof

Daylight visible through the roof boards, damp insulation, or dark staining
along the rafters all point to an active leak. This is the single most reliable
check a homeowner can do without a ladder.

## Look at the shingles from the driveway

Curling or cupping edges, bald patches where granules have washed away, and
shingles that have gone visibly darker in patches are all end-of-life signals.
Granules collecting in the gutters mean the same thing.

## Age matters more than appearance

An asphalt roof past twenty years is near the end of its service life even if it
still looks sound. Replacing on your schedule is consistently cheaper than
replacing on the weather's schedule.
