Our approach starts with the cause — impact, moisture intrusion, freeze-thaw, or overload — and then ensures the repair is properly bonded, reinforced and finished so it performs under heavy traffic and operational demand. Where full replacement is not necessary but structural integrity has to be maintained, a properly executed traditional repair improves safety, extends slab and pavement life, and lets a facility avoid the cost and disruption of premature replacement."

faqs:
  - q: "How do you decide between this and Fibrecrete-G?"
    a: "By what failed. If the problem is edge, spall or crack damage in a slab that still carries its load, a flexible system usually outperforms rigid patching. If the element has lost section and needs load-bearing restoration, rigid repair is correct and we specify it — that honesty is the point of the assessment."
  - q: "Do you handle municipal and exterior work?"
    a: "Yes. Pavements, yards, dock aprons and structural elements outside the building are all in scope alongside interior slabs."
  - q: "Will the repair match the surrounding slab?"
    a: "We profile, bond and finish so the repair is compatible with and flush to the surrounding concrete. A repair standing proud of the slab just becomes the next impact point."
  - q: "How long before it takes traffic?"
    a: "Longer than Fibrecrete-G — traditional materials cure on their own schedule. The specific return-to-service window is in the written scope so you can plan the shutdown around it."
