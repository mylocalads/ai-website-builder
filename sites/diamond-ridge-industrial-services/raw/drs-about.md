# About Us

Traditional concrete repair methods weren’t built for today’s industrial demands. Rigid, cement-based fixes are expensive, disruptive, and prone to cracking—often leading to repeated repairs, extended downtime, and rising maintenance costs. At Diamond Ridge Industrial Services, we provide a smarter, longer-lasting alternative.

# A Modern Approach to Concrete & Industrial Surface Repair

#### Diamond Ridge Industrial Services is a **women-owned industrial services company based in the Mid-Atlantic region,** delivering advanced concrete and surface protection solutions for facilities that can’t afford failure. Our work is centered on preventative maintenance strategies that reduce disruption, extend surface life, and protect long-term investments.

#### Our flagship service, **Fibrecrete-G®**, is the foundation of what sets us apart. Engineered to outperform traditional concrete, Fibrecrete-G® combines flexibility and strength to handle heavy traffic, structural movement, and extreme temperature changes—without cracking or separating like rigid repairs.

#### Our team brings hands-on experience, technical knowledge, and disciplined execution to every project.

#### We understand the demands of warehouses, manufacturing facilities, logistics centers, and commercial operations—and we deliver solutions designed to perform under real-world conditions.
