# How It Works

Fibrecrete-G is a high-performance concrete repair solution engineered to restore damaged loading docks, pavements, and industrial concrete surfaces. It effectively repairs corner breaks, spalls, and both transverse and longitudinal cracks—without the failures commonly associated with traditional cementitious repairs.

Formulated with polymer-modified resins, fiberglass reinforcement, mineral fillers, and high-quality aggregate, Fibrecrete G delivers an ideal balance of flexibility and strength. Unlike rigid concrete repairs that are prone to cracking, Fibrecrete G flexes with concrete movement, allowing it to withstand heavy traffic loads, temperature fluctuations, and seasonal expansion and contraction.

Designed for fast return to service, Fibrecrete G cures in one hour or less and can be installed year-round, even in low-temperature conditions. Its moisture-resistant composition prevents water intrusion and protects concrete from contaminants, extending pavement life while reducing maintenance costs. As a cost-effective alternative to full-depth concrete replacement or panel repairs, Fibrecrete G helps minimize downtime and keeps operations running efficiently and profitably.
