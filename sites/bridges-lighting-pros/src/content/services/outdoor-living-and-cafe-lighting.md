faqs:
  - q: "What parts of a yard do you light?"
    a: "Patios, porches and decks, outdoor kitchens and dining areas, fire pit and seating areas, walkways, steps and entry points, and landscapes, gardens and water features. Usually all on one system so the whole yard comes up together rather than in pieces."
  - q: "Will I see the fixtures?"
    a: "Not if it is done properly. Fixtures are set back and aimed so you see the effect rather than the lamp, and the wiring is hidden. Glare is the most common fault in landscape lighting and it comes from putting fixtures where they are easy to install rather than where they belong."
  - q: "Is it worth paying for professional fixtures?"
    a: "In our experience yes, and homeowners who have tried the alternative tend to agree. Big-box landscape fixtures commonly fail within a couple of seasons once moisture gets into them, and replacing them costs more over five years than installing commercial-grade in the first place."
  - q: "Do you work with restaurants and cafés?"
    a: "Yes. Café and patio lighting, accent lighting for architectural and landscape features, safety lighting for walkways and entrances, and ongoing maintenance. Your guests' comfort after dark is a business input, not a decoration."
  - q: "Can you add to a system I already have?"
    a: "Usually. We will look at the existing transformer capacity and wiring first and tell you honestly whether it can carry more before we quote extending it."
  - q: "Can landscape lighting tie into a permanent roofline system?"
    a: "Yes, and it is the cleanest way to do it — one controller and one app for the house and the yard rather than two systems and two remotes."
