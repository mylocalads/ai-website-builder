    a: "We own the lights rather than selling them to you. We design the display, install it, maintain it through the season, take it all down from January 1st and store it until next year. Because the same inventory has to go back up next season, we maintain it properly between seasons — so what arrives at your house in November is checked and matched rather than last year's strand with three dark bulbs in it."

    a: "You call us and we are back out within 24 hours — next business day — any time between installation and December 23rd. This is the single thing people wish they had asked about before hiring somebody else."

    a: "Warm white, classic red and green, red and white candy cane, or a custom scheme. We also install RGB colour-changing lights with full remote control if you want to change the display through the season rather than commit to one look in November."
