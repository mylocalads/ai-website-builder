We install both, so we have no stake in which one you choose. Here is the comparison we would give a neighbour.

## What each one actually is

**A permanent system** is a slim, colour-matched LED track fixed once along the roofline, eaves and architectural features, with the wiring concealed. It stays there. In daylight it reads as trim. At night you choose the colour from an app — warm white most of the year, red and green in December, whatever you want on game day.

**A seasonal holiday install on our lease programme** means we own the lights. We design the display, install it in the autumn, maintain it through the season, take it all down from January 1st and store it until next year. Nothing lives on your house between seasons and nothing lives in your attic either.

## Choose permanent if

- You want the house lit on ordinary nights, not just in December. This is the reason most people are actually buying, once they have had it a year.
- You like changing it. Colour by occasion is genuinely fun, and it is the feature that gets used far more than people expect at the quote.
- You want the roofline outlined precisely and identically every year, with no install crew on the property each November.
- You are staying in the house. It is a capital improvement, and it prices like one.

## Choose the seasonal lease if

- December is the only time you want the house lit, and the rest of the year you would rather look at a plain roofline.
- You want tree wraps, wreaths, garlands and shrub lighting. A permanent roofline system does not do any of those — this is the part people miss. A serious traditional display has elements no track can reproduce.
- You do not want to own equipment. On the lease programme the lights are ours, so their condition next season is our problem, and what arrives at your house has been maintained rather than pulled out of a bin.
- You want to try before committing to a permanent installation.

## The combination most of our repeat customers land on

A permanent roofline system for the architecture, plus a seasonal package for the trees, wreaths and garlands. The house outline is handled year-round from the app, and the crew comes out in November for the things that need hands on them. It is more display for less annual labour than a fully traditional install, and it is the setup we would choose ourselves.

## The question that matters either way

Whichever you pick, ask what happens when something stops working in the middle of December, because something occasionally does. Ours is 24 hours, next business day, from installation through December 23rd.
