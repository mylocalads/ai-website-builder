Lawrenceville is the Gwinnett county seat, and the spread of its housing is what shapes the lighting work here.

Around the historic downtown square there are homes with a hundred years on them — deep porches, decorative trim, fascia boards that were not detailed with an LED channel in mind. Those properties reward restraint. The right answer is usually warm white picking out the actual architecture, with the mounting worked out on site rather than assumed, because a track fixed the fast way to an older fascia will look exactly like a track fixed the fast way.

Then there are the subdivisions out toward Sugarloaf, Rock Springs and the Dacula line, many built in the last decade, with the clean gable geometry that permanent architectural lighting was more or less designed around. Those go quickly and they look sharp.

We install permanent roofline systems, holiday displays on the lease program, landscape and path lighting, and event lighting throughout Lawrenceville. Estimates are free and we will tell you which of the above your house actually wants.
