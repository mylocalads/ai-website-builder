Snellville is about ten minutes up Highway 78 from our shop, and its neighbourhoods have had thirty or forty years to grow into themselves.

That maturity is the whole story here. The hardwoods in Brookwood and Centerville are large now, and on a property like that the roofline is only half the picture — an uplit oak does more for the front of a house at night than another twenty feet of eave lighting would. So the plans we build in Snellville tend to split the budget differently than they would in a new subdivision, with real money going into tree and canopy work.

Mature trees have a second consequence that homeowners here already know about: they drop things. Branches come down in summer storms and take fixtures and wiring with them. It is worth asking any lighting company what happens the morning after that, and it is one of the reasons we keep the 24-hour service response.

Permanent roofline lighting, landscape and path lighting, holiday displays and event lighting, all installed across Snellville. The estimate is free.
