Suwanee's residential stock is newer than most of Gwinnett's, much of it master-planned, and it suits permanent architectural lighting well — clean gable lines, consistent fascia detail, and elevations designed to be looked at.

There is a second factor here that comes up on more Suwanee quotes than anywhere else we work: covenants. Plenty of these neighbourhoods have an active association, and the reasonable worry is what the installation looks like in daylight and whether anyone is going to write a letter about it. The honest answer depends entirely on how the track is fixed. In a colour-matched channel following the roofline with the wiring concealed, it reads as trim from the street at noon. Surface-run in whatever colour came in the box, it does not, and that is a decision made by the installer rather than the manufacturer.

We install permanent roofline lighting, landscape and outdoor living lighting, holiday displays and event lighting throughout Suwanee, from Old Town out to Settles Bridge and the Johns Creek line. The estimate is free and includes a straight answer about daylight appearance.
