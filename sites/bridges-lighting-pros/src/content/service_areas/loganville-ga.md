Loganville is home. Our shop is here, and a good share of the yards we light in this town are yards we built first.

That has a practical consequence people notice in December rather than in October. When a run goes dark two days before a party, the difference between a company based here and a company covering metro Atlanta from somewhere else is whether "within 24 hours" is a promise or an aspiration. Ours is a short drive.

The housing stock along the Highway 78 corridor and out toward Walnut Grove and Between is a mix — established homes on larger lots alongside newer subdivisions — and the two want different things from a lighting plan. Older properties usually have the mature trees, which means uplighting and canopy work carry as much of the effect as the roofline does. Newer builds have the clean gable lines that permanent architectural lighting was designed for. We quote both the same way: someone stands in the driveway, looks at the house, and prices what is actually there.
