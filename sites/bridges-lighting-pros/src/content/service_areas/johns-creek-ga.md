Johns Creek has the largest and most architecturally detailed homes in our service area, and on a property like that the lighting plan is mostly a set of decisions about what to leave dark.

This is the one place where more is reliably worse. A house with six gables, a turret and three separate roof planes, outlined edge to edge in a continuous run, stops reading as a house and starts reading as a diagram of one. The elevations that actually look expensive at night are the ones where a few structural lines carry the composition and the rest of the roof is allowed to recede. Getting that right takes someone standing at the kerb at dusk rather than tracing a satellite image.

The neighbourhoods off Medlock Bridge, through St Ives and Rivermont and down toward the Chattahoochee also tend to have serious landscaping, which means specimen trees and hardscape worth uplighting. On those properties the landscape lighting frequently does more for the front of the house than the roofline does, and we will say so if it is true of yours.

Permanent architectural lighting, landscape and outdoor living lighting, holiday displays and wedding and event lighting, installed throughout Johns Creek.
