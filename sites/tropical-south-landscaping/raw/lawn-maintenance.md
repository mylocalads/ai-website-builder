# Landscape Maintenance & Services

_**What is landscape maintenance service?**_

_**Landscaping maintenance, is all about keeping the landscape of your property clean, attractive, and safe. It typically consists of the upkeep of the yard and garden. This is why it helps to hire a reputable landscaping company that provides comprehensive services.**_

_**As a homeowner, you want your lawn to be healthy and lush. If you have a small lawn, you can likely take care of it yourself, but if you have a larger property, you probably need the help of a lawn care and lawn maintenance service.**_

_**What is included in landscaping services?**_

- _**Mowing**_
- _**Edging**_
- _**Trimming**_
- _**Blowing**_
- _**Landscape design**_
- _**Planting**_
- _**Mulching**_
- _**Weeding of flower beds**_
- _**Spring cleanup**_

_**Our residential lawn maintenance staff is fully trained and only use professional grade tools. We sharpen our blades every day to ensure the best cut possible every time.**_
