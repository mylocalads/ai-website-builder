# Tree Trimming

_**Why trim your trees?**_

- _**Proper trimming can improve the health of your trees by removing branches that are dead or dying or hanging to low.**_

- _**Tree limbs and branches touching your home offer direct access for pests to reach your home.**_

- _**Prepare for a storm by trimming off limbs that might cause damage, or simply might fall off.**_

- _**Open up your property and views to allow the sun light in.**_

- _**Bring back the attractiveness of any trees that were damaged during a storm.**_

_**All tree trimming and tree pruning services are performed by trained licensed and insured supervised crews. We strictly adhere to OSHA safety regulations and ISA standards for tree pruning, palm tree trimming, canopy reductions, tree plantings and tree removals.**_
