## Reasons to remove a tree

- The tree is dead or diseased
- Root damage to concrete or your foundation
- Storm preparation
- Property beautification
- The tree is too close to a structure

## How we take it down

All tree removal is performed by trained, licensed and insured supervised crews. We strictly adhere to OSHA safety regulations and ISA standards for tree pruning, palm tree trimming, canopy reductions, tree plantings and tree removals.

Large trees come down in sections rather than in one piece, roped and lowered so nothing swings into the roof, the pool cage, the hedge or the neighbouring property. Where a bucket truck cannot get in, we bring a tracked spider lift that fits through a side gate and spreads its weight across the turf.

## Chipper services

A wood chipper cuts tree limbs, trunks and branches into chips, reducing branches, twigs and leaves to mulch and composting material. It makes cleaning up after tree work far less arduous — a truck carries a much bigger load once the wood is broken down, so the time, manpower and budget a job requires drops significantly.

In practice that means the debris leaves with us the same day, instead of sitting in a pile at your curb waiting on a second trip. If you want the chips, we can leave them on site as mulch.
