## What is sod?

Sod is simply grass and a small part of the soil beneath it, held together by the root system or in some cases by a thin layer of biodegradable material. But that is only part of what sod is — there are other aspects worth looking at when exploring its benefits.

Landscaping is one of the fastest and easiest ways to increase the value of a home, but sod has many uses that go beyond appearance. For example:

- Sod is used to establish a lawn quickly, especially in areas where seed would be too expensive to use or would simply blow away.
- Sod can improve cooling and air and water quality in the immediate area.
- It can help with the prevention of flooding. Sod helps to drain the water and divert it to the ground, where it becomes part of the groundwater supply instead of overflowing overtaxed drain systems or already swollen local water supplies.

## How we install it

We measure the area and prepare the ground first — old turf and debris cleared, the grade corrected where it needs to be. Then the sod goes down tight, seam to seam, and gets rolled so it makes proper contact with the soil beneath.

The first few weeks are the ones that matter. We tell you exactly how much water it needs and how often, because a new lawn that dries out at the knitting stage does not recover. If you would rather not manage it yourself, we can put the property straight onto a maintenance schedule.
