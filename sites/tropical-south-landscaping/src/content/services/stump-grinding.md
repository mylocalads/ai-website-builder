## What is stump grinding?

A stump grinder chews away at the stump wood that is left after a tree has been cut down. All stump grinders use a powerful, rotating blade that rips into the wood as it turns. The blade's teeth cut the stump wood into small pieces. The operator guides the blade over the entire stump as the blade reduces the wood to chips, taking the stump down to well below ground level.

## What you are left with

Stump grinding produces a lot of chips of wood. You will make more of them than you think, but they can be used as mulch on site or added to your green waste can.

You will also have a hole, since you have just ground out all the stump volume that filled it. You can shovel the wood chips into the hole to decay, or you can import soil and fill the hole so that it is even with the surrounding ground — which is what you want if you plan to sod or replant over it.
