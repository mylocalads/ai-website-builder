Landscaping maintenance is all about keeping the landscape of your property clean, attractive and safe. It typically consists of the upkeep of the yard and garden — which is why it helps to hire a reputable landscaping company that provides comprehensive services rather than a different subcontractor for each piece.

As a homeowner, you want your lawn to be healthy and lush. If you have a small lawn you can likely take care of it yourself, but if you have a larger property you probably need the help of a lawn care and lawn maintenance service.

## What is included

- Mowing
- Edging
- Trimming
- Blowing
- Landscape design
- Planting
- Mulching
- Weeding of flower beds
- Clean-ups

## How we work

Our residential lawn maintenance staff is fully trained and only uses professional-grade tools. We sharpen our blades every day to ensure the best cut possible every time — a dull blade tears the grass instead of cutting it, and on St. Augustine turf you can see the difference within a week.

We trim your grass to the right height for the species and the season, and we leave your lawn impeccably clean by blowing the trimmings off your hardscape areas. Nothing gets blown into the beds or the pool.

We offer weekly or bi-weekly service — the choice is yours.
