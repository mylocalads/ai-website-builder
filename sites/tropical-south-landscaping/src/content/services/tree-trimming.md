## Why trim your trees?

- Proper trimming can improve the health of your trees by removing branches that are dead, dying, or hanging too low.
- Tree limbs and branches touching your home offer direct access for pests to reach your house.
- Prepare for a storm by trimming off limbs that might cause damage, or simply might fall off.
- Open up your property and views to allow the sunlight in.
- Bring back the attractiveness of any trees that were damaged during a storm.

## How the work is done

All tree trimming and tree pruning services are performed by trained, licensed and insured supervised crews. We strictly adhere to OSHA safety regulations and ISA standards for tree pruning, palm tree trimming, canopy reductions, tree plantings and tree removals.

We work from aerial lifts rather than off a ladder wherever access allows. That is not about speed — it is about being able to reach the correct cut point on the correct limb, instead of taking whatever happens to be within arm's reach. It is the difference between a tree that comes back healthy and one that comes back as a thicket of weak shoots.

## Before the storm, not after it

Miami-Dade hurricane season runs June through November, and the county's own guidance is to prune ahead of it. The trees that fail in a storm are usually the ones carrying deadwood, a dense unthinned canopy, or a limb that has grown out over the roof. All three are fixable in spring, and none of them are fixable in the 48 hours before landfall.
