Homestead and the Redland are a different kind of property to the rest of the county. Acre lots, agricultural land, groves and nurseries, and tree lines that run the length of a fence rather than sitting in a front yard.

That is a matter of scale and equipment. Bigger properties need more mowing capacity and far more chipping capacity, and a job here is usually a full day rather than a scheduled half hour. We are set up for it — our own chipper, our own chip truck, and lifts that can work on unpaved ground.

South Dade is also the part of the county a storm reaches first. We keep that equipment ready through the season instead of trying to rent it the week a system is named, which is the difference between clearing your property in days and waiting weeks.
