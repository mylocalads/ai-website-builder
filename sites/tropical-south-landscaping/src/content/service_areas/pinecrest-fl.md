Pinecrest is large lots, mature planting and a neighbourhood standard that does not tolerate a sloppy visit.

That cuts both ways for us. The lawns need consistency — the same day each week, cut to the right height, edges crisp, driveway and walkways blown clean before we leave. The trees need experience: banyans and live oaks of the kind lining the Old Cutler corridor are not trees to learn on, and they are not trees to top.

We work residential properties and commercial grounds across the village, and we are equally happy quoting a weekly maintenance account or a single large removal.
