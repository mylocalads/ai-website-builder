Kendall covers a lot of ground, and much of it sits under a homeowners association.

That matters more than people expect. In communities like The Hammocks, Kendall Lakes and Country Walk, the association often sets the cut height, the edge treatment, what gets planted and what has to be removed. A crew that ignores those rules creates a violation letter for the homeowner rather than solving anything.

We work with homeowners, commercial property owners and HOAs in accordance with your specifications — and we are used to being the crew that has to satisfy both the resident and the association.
