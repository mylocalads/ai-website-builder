Coral Gables was laid out around its trees, and the city has protected that canopy ever since. It is one of the few places in Miami-Dade where a homeowner is more likely to be told they cannot cut something than that they should.

We work to that standard. Pruning to ISA specification, no topping, licensed and insured crews, and an honest conversation up front when the work you are asking about is the kind the city needs to weigh in on first. It is a slower answer than "sure, we'll take it out Tuesday" — and it is the one that does not leave you holding a violation.

Alongside the tree work we run regular lawn and landscape maintenance on residential and commercial properties across the city.
