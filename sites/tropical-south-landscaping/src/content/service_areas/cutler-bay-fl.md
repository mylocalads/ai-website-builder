Cutler Bay is where we are based. The shop is on Marine Drive, and a good share of our maintenance accounts are within a few streets of it.

The village runs from the bay westward, and the yards change as you go. Waterfront lots near Lakes by the Bay and Saga Bay take salt and wind, which is hard on turf and harder on anything planted too close to the seawall. Inland, the older Cutler Ridge streets carry mature canopy planted after Andrew, now grown well over the rooflines and due for real structural pruning rather than another round of tipping.

We handle both sides of that: the weekly or bi-weekly lawn maintenance, and the tree work that keeps the canopy off the house before the next storm season.
