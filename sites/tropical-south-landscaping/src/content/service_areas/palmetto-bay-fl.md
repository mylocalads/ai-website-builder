Palmetto Bay is a short run north from our yard, and it is one of the areas where the difference between a real tree crew and a truck with a chainsaw shows up fastest.

The village is known for its canopy — the Old Cutler Road corridor, the streets around Coral Reef Park, the mature oaks and royal palms on the larger residential lots. Those trees are an asset, and they are also the thing most likely to put a limb through a roof in a September storm. The answer is proper structural pruning and deadwood removal well ahead of the season, not an emergency crew in the week the cone shifts.

We also run regular lawn maintenance across the village, on both residential lots and HOA-managed common areas.
