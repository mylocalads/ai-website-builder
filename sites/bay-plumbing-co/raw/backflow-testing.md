| ## BACKFLOW TESTING  ​​BAY PLUMBING CO.<br>Annual backflow testing helps ensure that only clean, potable water is flowing through your [plumbing](http://www.bayplumbingco.com/index.html) into your home or business. Most Miami-Dade County businesses are required by County ordinance to have an up to date backflow preventer installed by 2012. If you’re not sure if you have a backflow prevented (some older buildings don’t), contact Bay Plumbing Company, Miami-Dade’s most dependable plumbers for over 40 years.<br>There are a number of different types of backflow preventer devices used. Backflow Bob, Bay Plumbing Company’s backflow experts are familiar with all kinds of backflow preventers. No matter what type of device your home or business has installed, we can handle all of your backflow testing, maintenance, repair and replacement needs.<br>[​Commercial property](http://www.bayplumbingco.com/residential_and_commercial.html) in Miami-Dade County should have an annual backflow testing conducted by our trained and certified backflow testing experts. It’s also a good idea for [residential homes](http://www.bayplumbingco.com/residential_and_commercial.html) to periodically have backflow testing conducted. This is especially true of older homes which often do not have an adequate backflow preventer installed.<br>In many cases, the Water and Sewer Department will contact you and require you to have your backflow tested. This isn’t cause for alarm. Backflow testing is a routine procedure which is designed to make sure that no contaminated water is entering your water supply from outside sources.
