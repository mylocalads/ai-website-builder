Water and sewer lines are where a Miami plumbing job gets expensive, and where the difference between contractors shows up most clearly. A lot of the housing stock through Coconut Grove, Coral Gables and South Miami still runs on cast iron laid two generations ago. It scales, it cracks, and eventually roots find it — and once that starts, patching one break rarely buys much time.

We repair them or replace them, depending on the need — and we will tell you which, based on what the camera actually shows rather than on which job is larger. Where replacement is the honest answer, we say so; where a repair will genuinely hold, we do that instead. One customer wrote that our plumber "saved me a bundle by suggesting an easier fix." That is not an accident, it is the policy.

City sewer connections are the other half of this. Whether you are building a new home or commercial building in Dade County or finally taking an older property off septic, we make sure the connection is done right the first time so you will not have problems down the line.

All of it includes the digging. We do not sub-contract excavation or concrete removal, so the trench, the repair and the make-good are one crew's responsibility and one line on your estimate.
