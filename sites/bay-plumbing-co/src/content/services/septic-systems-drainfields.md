faqs:
  - q: How do I know my drainfield is failing?
    a: Soggy or unusually green ground over the field, slow drains throughout the house rather than at one fixture, gurgling, or odour outside after rain. In Miami's water table these signs escalate quickly, so a soggy yard is not a wait-and-see.
  - q: Do you sub-contract the excavation?
    a: No. We have a large fleet of equipment and do not need to sub-contract out septic tank work, excavation work or concrete removal work. We have all of it in house, which is why the dig, the repair and the make-good happen on one schedule and appear on one invoice.
  - q: How often should a septic tank be pumped?
    a: It depends on tank size and how many people are on the system, but most residential tanks want attention every three to five years. If yours has never been pumped, that is the first thing to check — and we can inspect the drainfield while the tank is open.
  - q: Can you inspect a septic system before I buy a property?
    a: Yes, and you should. Finding out after closing that the drainfield needs replacing is one of the most expensive surprises in Miami real estate. We inspect the tank, the field and the rest of the plumbing infrastructure and give you an accurate estimate of the costs before you buy.
  - q: Do you handle grease traps for restaurants?
    a: Yes. We pump, inspect and repair grease traps as well as septic tanks, on a scheduled or as-needed basis for commercial kitchens across Miami-Dade.

Septic is the work that separates a plumbing company from a plumber. A lot of Miami-Dade property — particularly south and west of the built-up core — still runs on a tank and a drainfield rather than city sewer, and the local water table gives those systems a harder life than they would have almost anywhere else in the country.

When a drainfield starts to fail, the window between "the yard seems damp" and "nothing in the house drains" is short. Our septic system professionals can diagnose any drainfield issue and perform the repairs or replacement needed. We also handle inspections, repairs and pump-outs for all grease traps and septic tanks, and we install complete septic systems and drainfields from scratch on new construction.

The part that matters commercially is that none of it gets handed off. We have a large fleet of equipment and do not need to sub-contract out septic tank work, excavation work or concrete removal work — we have all the equipment in house to provide these services quickly and efficiently. For you that means one company on the property, one schedule to keep track of, one crew responsible for putting your driveway and your yard back, and one bill at the end of it.
