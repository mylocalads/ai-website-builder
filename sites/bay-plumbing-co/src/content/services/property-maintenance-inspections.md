Timely property maintenance saves money over the years, and nowhere more so than in Miami, where the water table and the age of the housing stock conspire against anything buried. The problem is that plumbing infrastructure gives very little warning: it is fine, and then it is a bill.

Before you buy any property, have the plumbing infrastructure inspected. We check the condition of all of it — sewer, backflow, drainfield, septic systems, leak detection visible and concealed, water pressure, hot water service and water connections — and where there are problems we give you an accurate estimate of the costs *before* you buy rather than after you have signed.

For landlords and property managers the same logic runs continuously rather than once. Whether you own a home, a business, or multiple units, having the plumbing inspected and maintained on a regular basis by the same company is a cost-effective way to make sure you are not paying for major repairs down the line. We provide the inspections and certifications required across Miami-Dade, and we can suggest a schedule that keeps the system running rather than reacting to it.

Where repair or replacement is necessary, you get quality work for an honest price — and because we own our excavation and concrete-removal equipment, the difficult jobs do not turn into a second contractor and a second invoice.
