This is the everyday half of residential plumbing — the fixture that finally gave up, the water heater that started weeping at the base, the bathroom you have decided to gut. None of it is glamorous and all of it goes wrong if the connection behind the wall is done carelessly.

We handle new fixture installation and hook-up, and we replace what is past saving. Whether you need pipes, toilets, or any other part of your plumbing system replaced, you get a fair price for the work and a number before we start. Replacement is quoted work, so the estimate is free — that is the same rule that covers remodelling and water and sewer line replacement.

For kitchen and bathroom remodels we take the plumbing side: re-running supply and waste where the layout moves, setting the new fixtures, and hooking it all up so the finish carpenter is not waiting on us. And because our trucks carry a large inventory of common material, most of it happens without a supply-house run in the middle of your job.
