Water does not wait for business hours, and in Miami it rarely stops on its own. A supply line that lets go at midnight, a main drain that backs up into a ground-floor bathroom, a water heater that finally splits — every hour those go unattended is more floor, more drywall and more money.

We handle all sorts of leaks, whether they are concealed or visible, and we handle every type of pipe. Most leaks can be repaired on the spot. Where the fault is inside a sewer line rather than a supply line, we put a camera in and look before we start opening anything, so the repair happens in one place rather than three exploratory holes.

The reason we can finish is inventory and equipment. We stock materials at the shop and carry the common ones on the trucks, which means jobs get completed without ordering parts or driving to a supply house. And because we own our excavation and concrete-removal equipment outright, an emergency that turns out to be under a slab or out in the yard does not stall waiting for a sub-contractor to have a free morning.
