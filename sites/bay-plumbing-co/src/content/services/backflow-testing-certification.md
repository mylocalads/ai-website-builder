Backflow is the failure nobody sees until it has already happened: contaminated water from an outside source pulled backwards into a clean supply. The device that stops it is small, cheap relative to the consequences, and legally required across most of Miami-Dade's commercial property.

Most Miami-Dade County businesses are required by County ordinance to keep an up-to-date backflow preventer installed. There are a number of different preventer types in service around the county, and older buildings frequently have none at all. Backflow Bob, Inc. — Bay Plumbing Company's certified backflow experts — is familiar with all of them, and handles testing, maintenance, repair and replacement.

The practical version: we come out, test, and in most cases are finished in 15 to 20 minutes. If the device fails, we usually have the equipment and material on hand to handle simple repairs there and then. If a new preventer is needed — to replace a defective one, or because one was never installed — we handle that too.
