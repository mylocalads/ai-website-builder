New construction is where plumbing decisions get locked in. Everything is accessible for a few weeks and then it is behind concrete and drywall for the next fifty years, which is why "done right the first time" is not a slogan on this particular job — it is the whole economics of it.

If you are building a new home or commercial building in Dade County, we handle the plumbing: rough-in, fixture setting and hook-up, and the connection to city sewer. Whether you are building new or finally taking an older property off septic, we make sure the connection is done right so you will not have problems down the line. Where the site needs its own system instead, we install complete septic systems and drainfields.

The reason contractors keep us on programme is that we are one trade rather than three. We have a large fleet of equipment and do not need to sub-contract septic tank work, excavation work or concrete removal work — we have all of it in house. On a build, that is the difference between a delay you can manage and one that belongs to somebody else's calendar.

We also handle the inspections and certifications required across Miami-Dade, including fitting and certifying backflow preventers, which is considerably cheaper as part of the build than as a retrofit.
