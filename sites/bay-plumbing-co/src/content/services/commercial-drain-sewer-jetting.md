A blocked line in a house is an inconvenience. A blocked line in a restaurant, a strip of retail units or a multi-tenant building is lost trading, and often several tenants calling at once.

Commercial waste lines fail differently. They are larger, they carry far more volume, they are frequently shared, and in any kitchen they are coated in grease rather than blocked by a single object. That combination is why a cable is the wrong tool: it punches a hole through the blockage, water moves, everyone relaxes, and the line closes up again a few weeks later. Jetting scours the pipe wall properly, which is what actually removes grease and root intrusion.

We put a camera in first where the line has backed up more than once, because a repeat stoppage usually means something structural — a belly, a crack, root intrusion, a collapsed section — and video equipment finds that far more cheaply than an exploratory dig. If the line does need repair or replacement, we handle that too, excavation and concrete removal included, without bringing in another company.

The other half of this is timing. We have run 24-hour service since 1968, so the work can happen before you open or after you close rather than during service. For kitchens and multi-tenant buildings we will also put you on a preventive interval, which is almost always cheaper than the reactive version.
