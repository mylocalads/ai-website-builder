## Scheduled Tree Maintenance for HOAs and Property Managers in Miami-Dade

Reactive tree care — calling a crew after a limb falls or a tree becomes a hazard — is almost always more expensive, more disruptive, and more dangerous than a planned maintenance schedule. For HOA communities, commercial properties, and property managers overseeing multiple sites throughout Miami-Dade County, a tree maintenance contract turns unpredictable emergency costs into a manageable, budgeted line item.

Tropical South Tree Services offers ongoing tree maintenance contracts for residential communities, HOA common areas, commercial properties, and multi-site portfolios throughout Miami-Dade County. We work directly with property managers and HOA boards to build a maintenance schedule that fits the property's canopy, budget, and seasonal priorities — with pre-hurricane season pruning, regular palm maintenance, hazard inspections, and emergency response coverage built into every agreement.

The [International Society of Arboriculture](https://www.isa-arbor.com/) recommends regular structural pruning cycles for trees in managed landscapes — typically every 2 to 3 years for large shade trees and annually for palms. A maintenance contract ensures your property stays on that cycle rather than falling behind until a storm makes the decision for you.

Our full range of [tree services in Miami-Dade County](https://tropicalsouthtreeservices.com/tree-services) is available on a contract basis for properties of all sizes throughout the county.

## What's Included in a Tree Maintenance Contract

### Scheduled Pruning and Palm Care

We build a pruning calendar based on your property's species mix, canopy size, and HOA or municipal requirements. Large shade trees are typically pruned on a 2 to 3 year structural cycle. Palms — coconut, royal, queen, foxtail, and others — are maintained annually with frond removal, seed pod cleaning, and boot removal. All pruning follows ANSI A300 standards.

### Pre-Hurricane Season Inspection and Prep

Every contract includes a pre-season walkthrough before June 1. We identify high-risk trees — those with structural defects, overextended limbs over structures, or root problems — and prioritize them for pruning or removal before hurricane season begins. A written summary of findings is provided to the property manager or board after every inspection.

### Hazard Tree Monitoring and Reporting

Between scheduled maintenance visits, conditions change. We flag newly identified hazards between visits and provide written documentation that HOA boards and property managers can use for insurance purposes, resident communications, and permit applications when removal is needed.
