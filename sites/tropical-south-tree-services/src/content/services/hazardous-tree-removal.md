## Warning signs worth a phone call

Most tree failures announce themselves first. Look for:

- **A lean that is new**, or soil cracked and lifting on the opposite side of the trunk — that is the root plate moving.
- **Cracks or splits** in the trunk or where two major limbs meet.
- **Mushrooms or fungal brackets** at the base or on the trunk. They are the visible part of decay that is already well advanced inside.
- **Deadwood in the upper canopy**, especially over a roof, a driveway or a pool deck.
- **Roots cut or damaged** by recent trenching, driveway work or a pool installation — the canopy stays the same size while its anchorage has been reduced.
- **A hollow trunk**, or bark falling away in sheets.

## An honest assessment first

Not every tree showing a warning sign has to be removed. Sometimes the answer is a crown reduction to take weight off a compromised limb, or a deadwood clean-out, and that is both cheaper for you and better for the tree.

We will tell you which situation you are in. Where the call is genuinely borderline — or where an HOA, an insurer or the county wants it in writing — we can arrange a formal assessment through our ISA certified arborist partners.

## Taking one down safely

Hazardous trees are removed differently from healthy ones, because the assumption that the wood will hold no longer applies. Compromised trees are dismantled in small sections and roped down rather than felled, and we set up so that no one is working under a suspect limb.

Where a bucket truck cannot reach — a back garden, a narrow side return, a courtyard — we bring a tracked spider lift that fits through a side gate and spreads its weight across the lawn instead of rutting it.
