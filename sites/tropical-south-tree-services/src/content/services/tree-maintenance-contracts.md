## What a contract covers

Scope is built around the property rather than sold as a package, but most include:

- **Scheduled pruning** on a set cycle, so trees are maintained rather than rescued.
- **Annual palm care** — fronds and seed pods, timed so the property is not covered in debris before an event or a busy season.
- **Hazard inspection**, with anything urgent flagged in writing.
- **Pre-season storm prep** completed before June rather than during it.
- **Priority storm response** for contracted properties.
- **Documented records** of what was inspected, recommended and done.

## Why HOAs run this way

**One number instead of three.** The same crew handles the entrance, the common areas, the perimeter tree line and the resident requests.

**A budget you can actually set.** An agreed annual figure is something a board can approve in advance. Emergency invoices are not.

**Compliance.** Communities with landscape standards need work that meets them, and we work to your specifications rather than around them.

**Documentation for the board.** Records of inspections and completed work, which matters both for the annual meeting and for the insurer.

**A defensible position.** If something does fail, an owner with a documented maintenance programme is in a very different place to one without.

## Priority when it counts

The fortnight after a hurricane crosses Miami-Dade is when every tree company in the county is committed and nobody new is getting scheduled.

Contract properties are triaged first. That is not a marketing line — it is the practical reason most of our commercial and HOA accounts signed one in the first place.
