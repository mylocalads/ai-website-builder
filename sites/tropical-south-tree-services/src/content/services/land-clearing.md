## What land clearing covers

**Tree and stump removal** across the parcel, including the root systems where the ground needs to take footings or hardscape.

**Heavy brush and undergrowth**, cleared back to open ground.

**Invasive exotics** — Brazilian pepper and melaleuca in particular, which take over cleared and disturbed land in South Florida faster than anything native will.

**Debris haul-off.** Material is chipped on site where it makes sense and hauled out. Nothing is left stacked in a corner for you to deal with later.

**Grading to finish**, so the site is workable rather than simply cut.

## South Dade acreage

The bigger parcels in Redland, Goulds and the agricultural south of the county are a different job from a suburban lot. Fence lines that run for hundreds of feet, old groves, and ground that has not been touched in years.

That is a question of equipment and capacity more than technique — more chipping capacity, more haul-off, and full days rather than half-hour visits. We are set up for it.

## Permits still apply

Clearing a lot does not exempt you from the county's tree ordinance. Protected and heritage species on the parcel still require permits before removal, and that is exactly the kind of thing that stops a build.

We identify what is protected before we start and coordinate the permitting with Miami-Dade, including arranging a written arborist assessment through our ISA certified partners where the county wants one.
