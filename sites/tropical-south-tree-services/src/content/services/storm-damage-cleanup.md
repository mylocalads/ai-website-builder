## What we clear

**Downed and uprooted trees**, sectioned out and removed, including trees that have come down across fences, sheds, pool cages and driveways.

**Hangers and widowmakers** — limbs broken but still lodged in the canopy. These are the most dangerous thing left on a property after a storm and the easiest to miss from the ground.

**Split and damaged trees** that are still standing. Some can be reduced and saved; some are now a hazard. We will tell you which.

**Brush and debris**, chipped on site and hauled away rather than dragged to the kerb.

**Access** — driveways, walkways, gates and shared community roads cleared first so people can get in and out.

## Why the chip truck matters

We own our chipper and chip truck rather than renting when a storm is forecast. That sounds like a detail, and after a hurricane it is the whole difference.

When every crew in the county is trying to hire equipment at once, rented kit is simply not available. Ours is already on the trailer. It means debris leaves your property with us on the day we do the work, instead of forming a pile at the kerb that waits weeks on the county's storm-debris rounds.

## For HOAs and property managers

Multi-property storm response needs someone who can walk a whole community, triage it, and give the board one written scope rather than a running series of surprises.

We handle that — common areas, entrances, perimeter tree lines and individual units — and we can put a maintenance contract in place ahead of the season so the response is already arranged when a storm is named.
