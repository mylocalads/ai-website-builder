## What we clear

- Overgrown shrub beds and hedge lines that have outgrown any hope of a trim.
- Fence lines and property boundaries buried under vegetation.
- Vacant and neglected lots, including code-enforcement clean-ups.
- Undergrowth beneath tree canopies.
- Vegetation crowding structures, air-conditioning units, pool equipment and utility access.

## Why it is worth doing before the season

Dense brush against a structure is a genuine liability in a storm — it becomes airborne debris, and it holds water against walls and foundations afterwards.

It is also where the county's least welcome wildlife lives. Overgrowth along a fence line is prime habitat for rats and iguanas, and standing water trapped in dense vegetation is a mosquito problem you cannot spray your way out of.

## Clearing versus removal

If you want the plants gone permanently, we take out the root systems rather than cutting to the ground — otherwise most South Florida shrubs are back within a season.

If you want the bed kept but reclaimed, we cut back hard to a shape that can be maintained, and tell you honestly which plants will recover from that and which are past it.
