## The permitting matters more here than anywhere

The Village of Pinecrest administers and enforces its own tree removal permits, and it does so actively.

Protected species — live oak, gumbo limbo, mahogany and certain palms and large-diameter trees — require a permit **before any removal work begins**. Replacement trees are frequently a condition of approval, and unpermitted removal can result in substantial fines plus mandatory replacement, occasionally at a ratio of several new trees for each one removed.

We have been called in after the fact often enough to be blunt about it: the cheap quote that does not mention permits is the expensive option in Pinecrest.

## How we handle it

We identify which trees on your property are protected during the quote, tell you plainly whether a permit is needed, prepare the application, and coordinate with the village and with DERM.

Where the application requires a written assessment, we arrange it through our ISA certified arborist partners. Those reports also meet Miami-Dade DERM requirements, so one document generally serves both.

## Large lots, mature specimens

Pinecrest properties are big and heavily planted, and maintained to a standard the neighbours notice.

The banyans and live oaks along the Old Cutler corridor are not trees to learn on. Most of our work here is structural pruning and canopy thinning on mature specimens, deadwood clean-outs over houses and pool decks, and royal palm maintenance.

## HOAs within the village

Pinecrest itself has no single HOA governing the whole village, but many subdivisions and gated communities inside it maintain their own landscape standards.

We confirm what applies before starting, and provide full documentation of the work for homeowners who need it for property records or for a disagreement with a neighbour.
