## Exposed from two directions

The Hammocks catches weather from both sides.

Storm cells develop over the Everglades to the west and track east across this part of the county through the summer, and the Atlantic hurricane tracks that hit southern Miami-Dade come in from the other direction. Between the two, this is not a sheltered corner of the county.

The community sustained damage in Hurricane Irma, and — as with Kendall generally — the more important legacy is what was left behind rather than what came down. Cracked unions closed over by regrowth, hidden stub breaks, and root plates that were loosened in saturated ground and never properly re-anchored.

## Association-governed streets

Much of The Hammocks operates under association governance with landscape standards attached.

We work to those specifications, coordinate with property managers, and will tell you plainly what needs board sign-off before we start. County approval and HOA approval are separate processes and you generally need both.

## Permits in The Hammocks

The Hammocks is in unincorporated Miami-Dade County, so permits go **through the county** rather than a city office.

Protected and heritage species require a permit before removal. We advise on whether your specific tree needs one, prepare the application, and coordinate with DERM.
