## Our home town

Tropical South Tree Services operates out of Cutler Bay, and a good share of our work is within a few streets of the yard.

That proximity is the whole value proposition here. When a tree splits on a Sunday afternoon, the difference between a crew twenty minutes away and one coming down from north Miami-Dade is the difference between a same-day make-safe and waiting until Tuesday.

## The trees on these streets

Cutler Bay incorporated in 2005, but the housing stock and the canopy are much older than the town.

Whispering Pines and Lake Lorene are established communities where the trees planted decades ago have now outgrown where they were put. That is the most common job we do here — mature live oaks and ficus that have reached the roofline and need genuine structural pruning, or occasionally removal because they were planted eight feet from a house that was never going to accommodate them.

Lakes by the Bay and Saga Bay are a different problem. Proximity to Biscayne Bay means salt exposure and unobstructed wind, and species that do fine two miles inland struggle on an exposed waterfront lot.

## Hurricane Irma, and what it taught

Cutler Bay took a direct hit from Irma in September 2017, with sustained winds over 100 mph — one of the strongest storms to reach Miami-Dade in decades. Mature trees came down across residential streets throughout the town.

The lesson from that week, repeated on almost every property we worked afterwards, was that the trees which failed were rarely the ones that looked unhealthy. They were the big dense-canopied specimens that had never been thinned, and the ones carrying deadwood nobody had looked at from thirty feet up.

## Permits in Cutler Bay

The Town of Cutler Bay does not run its own separate tree ordinance. Removals fall under **Miami-Dade County DERM**, and the countywide rules apply.

Protected species — live oak, gumbo limbo, mahogany among them — need a permit before removal. We identify what is protected during the quote and handle the county coordination, and where DERM wants a written assessment we arrange it through our ISA certified arborist partners.
