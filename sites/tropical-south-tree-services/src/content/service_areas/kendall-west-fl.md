## Two sets of rules

Kendall West sits at the western edge of developed Miami-Dade, and a large proportion of its housing is townhomes and villas under association governance.

That means almost every removal here has two approval paths running in parallel: **Miami-Dade County**, because the community is unincorporated and there is no city permit office, and **your association**, which very likely has its own landscape rules covering what can be removed, what has to be planted back and who signs it off.

We handle the county side and tell you clearly what your association still needs to approve. Getting one without the other is the most common way a straightforward removal turns into a problem.

## Townhome tree work

Shared and semi-shared planting brings its own complications — a tree in a common area, a canopy overhanging two units, or roots crossing a boundary that exists on paper but not in the ground.

We are used to working these situations with property managers and boards rather than being caught between neighbours, and we document what was done so there is a record afterwards.

## Access

Townhome and villa developments generally mean tight access: narrow side returns, shared driveways, limited parking for equipment.

Our tracked spider lift fits through a standard gate and spreads its weight across turf rather than rutting it, which is what lets us reach trees behind these units without a truck on the lawn.
