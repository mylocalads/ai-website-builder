## An old South Dade community

Goulds runs along the US-1 corridor south of Perrine and is one of the oldest communities in this part of the county.

That history is visible in the trees. Mature live oaks shading older yards, large specimens that predate most of the surrounding development, and trees that have taken decades of South Florida weather and carry the accumulated damage of it.

We are on these streets regularly and most of the work falls into two categories: keeping the old trees safe and healthy, and dealing with the ones where decades of storms have finally made removal the honest answer.

## Storm-worn trees

A tree that has stood through Andrew, Irma and everything between is not the same as a tree of the same age that has not.

Repeated storm loading leaves cracked unions, healed-over stub breaks and root plates that have been worked loose and re-anchored more than once. From the ground these trees often look magnificent. From a lift, thirty feet up, they frequently tell a different story.

That gap is the whole argument for having mature trees properly inspected rather than judged from the driveway.

## Permits in Goulds

Goulds is unincorporated, so removals fall **directly under Miami-Dade County's tree ordinance** with no separate city permit.

Whether a permit is needed depends on the tree's size and species. We determine that before any work starts and handle the paperwork for you.
