  Carbondale gets the weather first. It sits at the top of the valley where the elevation is
  higher and the snow load runs heavier than it does down in Scranton, and ice damming at the
  eaves is a genuinely common call here rather than an occasional one. That makes ventilation
  and the detail at the eave edge worth more attention than they usually get — a roof that is
  perfect in the field and wrong at the drip edge will still put water into a Carbondale ceiling
  in February. We have worked throughout the Upper Valley and into Forest City. One Carbondale
  family summed up their job simply: excellent work done very quickly, from the estimator down
  to the roofers and the cleanup crew.
