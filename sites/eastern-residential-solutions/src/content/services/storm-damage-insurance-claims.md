faqs:
  - q: Someone knocked on my door and said I have storm damage. Should I trust them?
    a: >-
      Be careful. After hail or high wind, crews from well outside the area work NEPA
      neighborhoods, and the pattern homeowners describe is always the same — pressure to sign
      on the spot, a promise of a free roof, and a request to endorse the first insurance check.
      Get a second opinion from a company with a local address you can drive to. Ours is
      1 Keystone Place in Jessup, and the second opinion is free.
  - q: Do you handle the claim, or do I?
    a: >-
      You file it; we do the heavy lifting after that. We inspect and document the damage so
      you go to your carrier with a photo record and a clear date of loss rather than a guess,
      and then we work directly with the insurance company on the scope. That is the part
      homeowners tell us saved them the most aggravation.
  - q: If you help with the claim, are you incentivized to inflate it?
    a: >-
      No, and this is the part worth understanding. Our fee does not change based on what your
      insurer pays out. One of our Scranton customers put it better than we could: they do not
      have to help with the claim, since they are getting paid the same amount regardless of
      the insurance payment. Our only interest in that conversation is making sure the damage
      that is genuinely there ends up on the paperwork.
  - q: My roof is leaking right now. How fast can you get here?
    a: >-
      Call (570) 382-8629. We are reachable around the clock, which matters when water is
      already coming in. We will get out to stop active intrusion and get a permanent repair
      scheduled — tree and limb impact, major leaks, and wind or hail damage are all things we
      respond to on an emergency basis.
