faqs:
  - q: Why vinyl instead of wood or fiber cement?
    a: >-
      For most NEPA homes it comes down to what the exterior costs you over the next twenty
      winters. Vinyl does not need repainting, resists fading and discoloring, and does not
      take on water at the seams the way wood does through a freeze-thaw cycle. The modern
      profiles also read far closer to rough sawn cedar than the vinyl most people picture.
  - q: Can you match my existing siding for a partial replacement?
    a: >-
      Sometimes, and we will tell you honestly when the answer is no. Vinyl fades over time and
      manufacturers discontinue colors, so a patch on a sun-facing elevation can end up more
      noticeable than the damage it replaced. Where a partial repair will read cleanly we will
      do it; where it will not, replacing a full elevation is usually the better money.
  - q: Do you do soffit, fascia and gutters at the same time?
    a: >-
      Yes, and it is normally the right call. Soffit, fascia and gutters all tie into the same
      roofline, so doing them together means one set of staging, one color match, and no seam
      where two crews met. A number of our siding jobs go out as roof, siding, soffit, fascia
      and seamless gutters in a single project.
  - q: How long does a siding job take?
    a: >-
      Longer than a roof — typically several days to a week for a full house, depending on
      square footage, the number of windows and openings to wrap, and whether any sheathing
      underneath needs repair. Your project manager gives you the schedule in the in-home
      estimate rather than after the first day.
