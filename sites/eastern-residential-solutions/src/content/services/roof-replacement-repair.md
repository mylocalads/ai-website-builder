faqs:
  - q: How long does a roof replacement take?
    a: >-
      Most of our residential replacements are finished in one to two days once materials are on
      site. A 28-square roof in Clarks Green went start to finish in a single day; a 53-square
      replacement in Dickson City took two. Steep pitch, multiple existing layers, or decking
      that needs re-sheeting can add time, and we tell you that up front rather than after
      the tear-off starts.
  - q: Do I need a full replacement or will a repair hold?
    a: >-
      It depends on how much life is left in the field of the roof, not just on the spot that is
      leaking. If the shingles still have years in them and the failure is localized — flashing,
      a valley, a few wind-lifted courses — a repair is the honest answer and we will say so.
      When granule loss and brittleness are widespread, a repair only buys months. The in-home
      estimate is where we show you which one you are looking at.
  - q: What happens if you find bad decking under the old shingles?
    a: >-
      We show you. Rotted or delaminated sheathing has to be replaced before new shingles go
      down, or the fasteners have nothing sound to hold. Re-sheeting is priced separately
      because nobody can see it until the tear-off, and on jobs where we have found it — a
      Monroe Township home where most of the house needed re-sheeting alongside two new
      skylights — the whole thing still came in inside two days.
  - q: What shingles do you install?
    a: >-
      Owens Corning is our primary line, most often TruDefinition Duration. Recent NEPA jobs
      have gone out in Onyx Black, Colonial Slate, Driftwood, Slatestone Gray and Harbor Blue.
      Our Preferred Contractor status is with Owens Corning specifically, which is what carries
      the extended manufacturer warranty on the materials.
