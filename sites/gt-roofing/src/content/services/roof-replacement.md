faqs:
  - q: How long does a roof replacement take?
    a: >-
      Most single-family homes in this area are a one to two day job once we are on
      site, weather permitting. Tear-off and dry-in happen the same day so your home
      is never left open overnight. Larger or steeper roofs, and roofs where we find
      significant deck rot underneath, can run longer — we will tell you as soon as
      we see it rather than at the end.
  - q: Do I need a full replacement or will a repair do?
    a: >-
      Often a repair will do, and we will say so. Age, the condition of the granules,
      whether the damage is isolated to one slope, and how many layers are already up
      there all factor in. The estimate is free and carries no obligation, so there is
      no cost to finding out that your roof has several seasons left.
  - q: What about the flat section over my porch?
    a: >-
      Shingles need pitch to shed water and will fail on a near-flat surface. Those
      sections get an EPDM rubber membrane instead, which is a single continuous sheet
      with no seams for water to work into. We do both on the same job routinely.
