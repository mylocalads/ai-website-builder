faqs:
  - q: Do I have to replace all the siding or can you patch a section?
    a: >-
      If the existing siding is still available and has not weathered too far from the
      original color, a section repair is often the right call and we will quote it that
      way. Where the product is discontinued or the color difference would be obvious,
      we will say that too and let you decide.
  - q: Why does soffit matter?
    a: >-
      Soffit vents let outside air into the attic. Without that airflow, warm air
      collects against the underside of the roof deck, melts the snow above it, and the
      meltwater refreezes at the cold eave as an ice dam. Blocked or damaged soffit is
      a roofing problem as much as a trim problem.
  - q: Do you paint interiors too?
    a: >-
      Yes. Interior and exterior both. The finish lasts as long as the preparation is
      good, which is where most of the labor on a paint job actually goes.
