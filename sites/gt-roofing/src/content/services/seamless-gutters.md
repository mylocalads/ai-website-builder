long_description: >-
  Seamless gutters are formed on site to the exact length of your run, so the only
  joints are at the corners and downspouts rather than every ten feet. That matters
  here more than it does in a mild climate: every seam is a place where freeze-thaw
  works the joint open and water starts finding the fascia behind it. We size the
  gutter and the number of downspouts to the roof area actually draining into them,
  add leaf guards where trees make that worthwhile, and pitch the run so it empties
  instead of holding standing water that freezes solid in January. We also clean and
  repair existing gutters. Clogged or undersized gutters are one of the main reasons
  ice dams form at the eave in the first place.

faqs:
  - q: Are leaf guards actually worth it?
    a: >-
      It depends on what is over your roof. Under mature oaks and maples, yes —
      they turn a twice-a-year cleaning into an occasional check. On an open lot with
      no overhang, the payback is much weaker and we will tell you so rather than sell
      you the upgrade.
  - q: Can you match the color to my house?
    a: >-
      Seamless gutter stock comes in a range of colors, so we can match or complement
      your trim and fascia. We will bring options when we come out to measure.
  - q: What do gutters have to do with ice dams?
    a: >-
      A great deal. When meltwater from the upper roof reaches the cold eave and has
      nowhere to drain, it refreezes and dams up. Water then backs up under the
      shingles. Correctly sized, correctly pitched, unclogged gutters are part of how
      you keep that from happening — along with proper attic ventilation.
