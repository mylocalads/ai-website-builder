faqs:
  - q: Will removing the tub hurt my resale value?
    a: In this market, rarely — most buyers looking at a master bath want a walk-in shower, and homes here usually keep a tub in a second bathroom anyway. If yours is the only tub in the house, Vince will say so during the consultation rather than after.
  - q: How long does a conversion take?
    a: An acrylic or composite conversion is typically about two days. A custom tiled conversion takes longer because the pan, membrane and tile each need their own stages — usually a week or more depending on size and materials.
  - q: Can you make it safe for someone with mobility issues?
    a: Yes. Low-entry and curbless bases, grab bars anchored into blocking rather than drywall, built-in bench seating, handheld fixtures and slip-resistant pan tile are all standard options we design in from the start.
  - q: Do I need to move plumbing?
    a: Sometimes. A straight swap in the same footprint often does not, but changing the drain position or moving the valve does. That is one of the biggest variables in the quote, so it gets checked at the consultation.
