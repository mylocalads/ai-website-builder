faqs:
  - q: LVP or laminate — which should I pick?
    a: Both hold up well in high-traffic areas. LVP is waterproof, which makes it the right call for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms and basements. Laminate gives a very convincing wood look and cleans easily, but wants more care around standing water.
  - q: Can it go over my existing floor?
    a: Sometimes. It depends on what is down now, how flat it is, and how much height you can add at doorways and transitions. We check the subfloor at the consultation rather than guessing.
  - q: Can I see samples in my own house first?
    a: Yes, and you should. Both LVP and laminate read completely differently under your own lighting than they do in a showroom, so we bring samples out before anything is ordered.
  - q: Is waterproof LVP really waterproof?
    a: The planks themselves are, which is why it can go in every room of the house. A thick wear layer also handles pets, kids and furniture better than most laminate, and many products have acoustic backing that cuts down the hollow sound underfoot.
