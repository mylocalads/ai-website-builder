faqs:
  - q: How long will I be without the bathroom?
    a: Most full remodels run a few weeks depending on size, material lead times and whether plumbing or electrical has to move. If it is the only bathroom in the house, tell us at the consultation and we will sequence the work around that.
  - q: Can I change my mind on tile or fixtures once you've started?
    a: You can, but it usually costs time and money — material lead times are the main reason. We lock selections before demo precisely so that does not have to happen, and we will always tell you the impact before you commit to a change.
  - q: Do you handle plumbing and electrical relocation?
    a: Yes. Vince is a fully licensed general contractor in both North and South Carolina, and jobs that need drains, valves or circuits moved are handled as part of the scope rather than handed off to you to coordinate.
  - q: What should I do before you start?
    a: Clear personal items out of the bathroom and give us as much room around it as you reasonably can. We will walk you through anything else specific to your project before the first day.
