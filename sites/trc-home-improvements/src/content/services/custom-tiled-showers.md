faqs:
  - q: Why choose a custom-tiled shower over a prefabricated system?
    a: A custom-tiled shower gives you control over the size, layout and finished look, so it fits the room rather than the room working around it. A prefabricated system is quicker and simpler to install. We price both and let you compare against your space, budget and how much upkeep you want.
  - q: Should I go with a shower base or a fully tiled pan?
    a: A pre-formed base is simpler and faster to install; a tiled pan gives you design freedom and a fully custom finish, including curbless and low-entry layouts. Vince will walk you through both against your bathroom's framing and drain location.
  - q: Which tile actually belongs in a shower?
    a: Porcelain and ceramic with low water absorption. Not every tile is rated for a wet space, and the wrong choice fails long before the workmanship does. We specify the tile, seal the grout properly, and show you how to keep it that way.
  - q: Can I add a bench, niches or grab bars?
    a: Yes — and they need to be planned before framing closes up, not added afterward. Bench seats, recessed niches and blocked-in grab bars all get designed into the layout at the consultation stage.
