  Electrically charged powder particles — finely ground pigment and resin — are sprayed onto a
  grounded part, where they adhere until heat fuses them into a smooth coating in a curing oven. The
  result is a uniform, durable, high-quality finish that meets government specifications for
  performance and durability. Our line accommodates components up to 22 feet long by 5 feet high by
  47 inches wide, with a weight capacity up to 2,000 pounds, and includes a six-stage iron phosphate
  washer that assures clean parts and excellent powder adhesion. The spray booth carries sixteen
  robotic guns that can also be operated manually when a part calls for it. The line is best suited
  to small parts at high production, but we process orders ranging from one piece to entire truckloads
  daily — motorcycle and sprint car frames, automotive parts, agricultural equipment, pipes and
  fittings, and indoor and outdoor furniture.
