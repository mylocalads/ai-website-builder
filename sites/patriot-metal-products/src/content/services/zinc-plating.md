  to 10 feet long, while barrel plating is best suited to small and medium size components. We offer
  clear and yellow chromate zinc coating, which provides corrosion resistance and is cosmetically
  pleasing. Our zinc plating operation processes automotive parts, screws, nuts, bolts, component
  parts and full assemblies for an array of different industries.

faqs:
  - q: Should my parts go on a rack or in a barrel?
    a: It comes down to size and handling. Rack plating accommodates parts up to 10 feet long and suits larger or appearance-sensitive components. Barrel plating is best for small to medium parts running in volume — fasteners, screws, nuts and bolts.
  - q: What chromate finishes do you offer?
    a: Clear and yellow chromate.
  - q: What kind of corrosion protection does zinc give?
    a: Zinc provides corrosion resistance and is cosmetically pleasing. For demanding corrosion applications such as vehicle frames and chassis components, autophoretic coating delivers materially higher performance — tell us the service environment and we will point you to the right one.
  - q: Can you plate full assemblies, not just loose hardware?
    a: Yes. We process component parts and full assemblies across a range of industries.
