faqs:
  - q: What colors is autophoretic coating available in?
    a: The coating is available in black. It also works as an excellent primer for top-coating with powder or wet paint when you need a different finish color.
  - q: Will it coat the inside of a tube or a blind recess?
    a: Yes. Because deposition is driven by a chemical reaction wherever the solution wets the ferrous surface, the process covers interiors, edges and complex geometry at the same film build as flat faces — which is the main reason it is chosen over line-of-sight processes.
  - q: Which industries approve A-coat?
    a: The process is approved by the automotive, agricultural equipment and furniture industries, and is widely used on vehicle frames and chassis components.
  - q: How does it compare environmentally to conventional coating?
    a: Compared with powder, E-coat and wet paint, autophoretic is environmentally friendly with very low VOCs and no HAPS, and it achieves better corrosion resistance and adhesion at minimal film build.
