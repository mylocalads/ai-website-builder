  system accommodates parts up to 15 feet long. Typical batch work includes individual motorcycle
  frames, sprint car frames, automotive parts, tractor trailer parts, combines for the agricultural
  industry, and government components requiring CARC finishes — high temperature, high chemical
  resistance — that meet MIL, OMAH and ANSI specifications. Those specialty coatings are always
  applied in the batch process, and batch coatings can be custom color matched to your requirements.

faqs:
  - q: How large a part can the batch booth handle?
    a: The room-sized spray booth and oven process parts as large as 15 feet wide by 20 feet long by 6 feet high, and parts up to 15 feet long are accommodated in the batch booth system.
  - q: Do you apply CARC and other military specifications?
    a: Yes. CARC finishes — high temperature, high chemical resistance — meeting MIL, OMAH and ANSI specifications are applied through the batch process. Specialty coatings of this type are always run in batch rather than on the automated line.
  - q: Will you take a single item?
    a: Yes. Batch exists precisely for individual and low-quantity work — a single motorcycle frame or sprint car frame is a normal job here.
  - q: Powder or wet paint?
    a: Either. Large items in the batch booth can be coated using powder or wet paint depending on the finish and performance you need.
