The single most common question we get in the showroom is how long the kitchen
will be out of commission. The honest answer is six to eight weeks from the day
the order is placed, and that figure already includes waiting on the cabinets.

## Why the number is a range

Two things move it. The first is the cabinet line. HomeCrest normally runs about
a three-week lead time — our weekly cut-off is Tuesday afternoon, the order
ships three weeks later on a Friday, and it usually lands the following Monday.
The semi-custom and custom Omega line takes five to six weeks because the doors
and finishes are built to your order rather than pulled from stock.

The second is how much of the room is moving. Swapping cabinetry and counters in
the same footprint is a different job from relocating a sink, opening a wall, or
running new circuits for an island.

## The sequence that keeps you out of a torn-out kitchen

A lot of remodels go wrong at the very start, because demolition happens the
moment the contract is signed and the homeowner then lives without a kitchen for
a month while product is in transit. We run it the other way around.

1. **Design.** We tweak the layout until you are genuinely happy with it. There
   is no charge for this part.
2. **Final measure.** Before anything is ordered we re-measure the space to
   confirm the design fits as drawn. This is the step that prevents the
   filler-strip surprises on install day.
3. **Order.** The cabinets and countertops go on order, and you now have real
   dates rather than an estimate.
4. **Demolition.** Demo starts as the products are about to arrive — not weeks
   before.
5. **Install.** Cabinets first, then countertop templating, then the counters
   themselves one to two weeks later, then trim, backsplash, and paint.

## What countertops do to the schedule

Countertops cannot be templated until the cabinets are set, and they take one to
two weeks after templating. That is why on nearly every full kitchen there is a
short stretch where you have cabinets and no counters. Plan for it — a temporary
plywood top and a working sink makes that fortnight far more livable.

## If you already have a contractor

Plenty of our work comes through builders and remodelers who use our showroom as
their product and design department. If you have your own contractor, tell us
their schedule and we will time delivery around it, which is usually the
difference between a smooth week and a crew standing around waiting on a truck.
