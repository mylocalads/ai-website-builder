  - q: How long does it take for cabinets to come in?
    a: HomeCrest normally runs a three-week lead time, subject to holidays and truck schedules. Our weekly cut-off is Tuesday afternoon; the order ships three weeks later on a Friday and usually arrives the following Monday. The semi-custom and custom Omega line is normally five to six weeks.

    a: Yes. Plenty of customers have bought cabinets from us for several kitchens and bathrooms over the years and installed them themselves or through their own contractor. We will still do the design and the measuring.

    a: We require a 75% deposit on cabinets or countertops being ordered, with the balance due at completion.
