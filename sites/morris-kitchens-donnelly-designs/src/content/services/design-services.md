faqs:
  - q: Is the design consultation really free?
    a: Yes. We offer free design services — there is no charge to sit down, look at your space, and work out a layout. Detailed floor plans are provided once a contract for purchase is signed, unless you would prefer to purchase the design itself from us.
  - q: Who actually does the design?
    a: Celeste Donnelly, who holds a bachelor's of fine arts in interior design from Marywood University and spent twelve years in the local tile trade before that. She measures the room and draws the layout herself.
  - q: Can you help with rooms other than kitchens and bathrooms?
    a: Yes. Any space that benefits from an efficient layout, stylish design, practical storage, and skilled craftsmanship is fair game — laundry rooms, mudrooms, closets, home offices, and basement wet bars are all regular work for us.
  - q: I am a builder or remodeler. Can I use your showroom?
    a: Please do. We invite builders, remodelers, and designers to treat our team as an extension of their business. If you do not have a showroom, use ours — we provide the space, the products, the installation, and more.
