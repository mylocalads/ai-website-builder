faqs:
  - q: How long will my kitchen be out of commission?
    a: Plan on six to eight weeks from order to completion for a full kitchen, and that figure already includes cabinet delivery time. We deliberately do not start demolition until the products are about to land, so you are not living in a torn-out kitchen waiting on a truck.
  - q: Can you work around a contractor I have already hired?
    a: Yes. Tell us who they are and what their schedule looks like and we will time delivery of the cabinets and countertops around them. Plenty of our work comes in through builders and remodelers who use our showroom as their product and design department.
  - q: Do you handle the plumbing and electrical?
    a: We coordinate it as part of the project, along with trim and painting. The point of buying the kitchen and the installation from the same company is that nobody is left arguing over whose scope a detail belonged to.
