faqs:
  - q: Should I choose granite or quartz?
    a: It depends on how you live. Granite is natural, so every slab is one of a kind, and it is extremely hard-wearing — but it is porous and should be resealed once a year. Quartz is engineered and nonporous, which makes it essentially maintenance-free and better in high-moisture areas. Come see both in the showroom before deciding.
  - q: How long do countertops take to arrive?
    a: Usually one to two weeks after templating. That is why on a full kitchen the counters come after the cabinets are set rather than at the same time.
  - q: Can I see full slabs rather than samples?
    a: You can view dozens of countertops on display in our showroom. Come in to see, feel, and find the exact surface you want — deciding off a three-inch chip is how people end up disappointed.
