## Get the Kitchen of Your Dreams Today

Remodeling and updating your kitchen is one of the most beneficial projects you can do to improve the look, value, and functionality of your home. Whether you’re looking to spruce up an existing kitchen, complete a total renovation, or add an addition to provide more space, we are here to help meet your goals, needs, and tastes.



## Kitchen Remodeling Process

Today’s kitchens are a reflection of our lifestyles. Kitchens should be designed for functionality, and versatility, well equipped with modern technology, and feel warm and inviting. Every element in your kitchen should serve a functional purpose and fit your overall aesthetic. At Morris Kitchens &amp; Donnelly Designs, LLC, our goal is to give you the kitchen remodel of your dreams.

As highly experienced, award-winning designers, we will guide you with all appropriate selections to achieve an established design concept. We’ll listen to your needs and dreams and respect your budget while guiding you through every step of the process during your remodel project.

## Award-Winning Design

The design of your kitchen is personal and should be reflective of your style. We believe that the kitchen remodeling process should be too. You appreciate what others have done but you want your home to be totally yours, not a cookie cutter design like is common in so many houses these days. Morris Kitchens & Donnelly Designs, LLC, understands, which is why “We Take Your Home Personally” is more than a tag line for us.

We begin by asking lots of questions and taking the time to listen. Because you already have the thoughts and ideas, you simply need someone to hear what you’re saying and put it together so that it’s all about you. The most important member of your Project Development team is you.
