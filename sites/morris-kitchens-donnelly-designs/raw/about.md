## About

## Experience the Morris Kitchens & Donnelly  Designs Difference

Morris Kitchens has been in business since 1995 – all started by local proprietor John Morris. Originally John’s showroom was in Plymouth and moved to Hanover Township in 2008. We are located right next to Colarusso’s Pizza &amp; Pasta Restaurant in the West End Plaza – come for lunch and stay to shop for your new kitchen or bathroom!

In January of 2016 John Morris decided it was time to retire and spend more time with his family. Patrick Healey, purchased the business from John. Pat graduated from Johnson College and was a home builder in the area for almost 30 years. Even though Pat had a very successful and thriving business in building he wanted to change his career path and have more time with his family and to enjoy life more. Owning the kitchen business has given him that freedom and still allows for the use of his monumental creativity with installations of kitchens, bathroom, entertainment areas, bar areas – and so much more!

In October of 2016 Celeste Donnelly joined Morris Kitchens to help design kitchens and baths and also day to day office work. Celeste is a graduate from Marywood University with a bachelor’s of fine arts degree in interior design. She previously had design experience during and after college at another kitchen design center then went to work at a local tile store for 12 years. Celeste is ready to help you plan the interior of your dreams! No job is too small or too big – we want you to love your space!

![](https://morriskitchensllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/IMG953144.jpg)

Morris Kitchens & Donnelly Designs, LLC provides exceptional service to both homeowners looking to update their kitchen or a business owner who wants a bathroom that will impress clients. We’ve listened to some unique people who had out-of-the-ordinary ideas about how they wanted a bathroom or kitchen to look.

With Morris Kitchens & Donnelly Designs you are only limited by your imagination. We cater to any size project large or small and provide the highest quality service our professional staff can offer.

Whether you’re interested in remodeling your kitchen, bathroom, office, or any other room, it pays to have experienced remodeling professionals by your side who can meet all your needs, from start to finish.
