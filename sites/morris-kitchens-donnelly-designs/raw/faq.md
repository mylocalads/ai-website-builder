## FAQ

Do you help design our kitchen (or other area) or is that up to us?

We definitely help to design your kitchen or other area that you are working on. Celeste is ready to woo you with her interior design skills! We offer free design services. Detailed floor plans are not available until a contract is signed for purchase unless you would like the purchase the design itself from Morris Kitchens LLC.

How long does it take for cabinets to come in?

Our HomeCrest cabinet line is normally a three week lead time (dependent upon holidays or truck schedules). Our weekly cut-off is Tuesday afternoon, cabinets are then shipped in three weeks on a Friday and usually arrive on Monday. Our semi-custom / custom line from Omega is normally a 5 to 6 week lead time.

What is the ordering process and what forms of payment do you accept?

We require a 75% down payment of the cabinets or countertops that are being ordered.

How long will it take to complete my project?

This is a hard question to answer because everyone’s project is different. But for an average you can figure that your kitchen will take approximately 6-8 weeks to complete (this includes delivery time on the cabinets). Countertops usually take about 1-2 weeks for arrival. Other projects like bathrooms or laundry areas may only be a week to complete.

What is the normal process of construction?

First we tweak your design until you are totally happy with it. Next comes a final measure of your space to make sure everything will fit as planned. After that products will get ordered and just before they arrive or thereabouts the demolition of your space will begin! If you have your own contractor available it is best to ask that person what their schedule is that way we can accommodate them on delivery of the products you have ordered.
