## Countertops

![](https://morriskitchensllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/countertops-gallery-7.jpg)

Your countertops play a starring role in your kitchen design. They set the style and tone for the entire kitchen or bathroom with their material, color, veining, edge, and more. There is a vast range of countertop options available to enhance the style and functionality of any home. These range from granite to engineered quartz, marble, natural wood tops, and plastic laminates like Formica and Wilsonart.

With so many options available, choosing a countertop may seem like a daunting task, but it doesn’t have to be difficult. Start by asking yourself questions to better understand your requirements, like:

- What is my preferred style?
- How much can I spend?
- What is my lifestyle?
- Am I willing to follow a specialized cleaning and maintenance routine?

The key to finding the perfect countertop choice for your kitchen design is to select an option that balances your style and functional needs. Some materials like marble or wood have a natural beauty that can’t be beaten, but they require specialized cleaning and ongoing maintenance. Make sure you are happy to keep up with these requirements before committing to a material like this. Other surfaces are more durable, stain-resistant, and virtually maintenance-free, which might suit a busy household with young kids better. If you love to entertain, you will look for a countertop that is the perfect marriage of style and convenience. For avid chefs or bakers, choose a surface that is hard-working, durable, and won’t stand in the way of whipping up your favorite dishes.

## Granite

Granite has long been a top choice for kitchen countertops and is a luxurious and sophisticated choice for any style kitchen. It is a beautiful, natural material, so every slab of granite is unique. Granite is one of the hardest materials in the world, making it extremely hard-wearing, and resistant to scratches, heat, and stains. It is a porous material, so it must be resealed annually to maintain its appearance.



![](https://morriskitchensllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Friesen-kitchen-design-9-website-1-2048x1365.jpg.webp)

## Quartz

Quartz is another highly durable surface, and it is nonporous, which means it can stand up to high-moisture areas, as well as spills and splatters. It also can withstand heat, impact, and sharp objects making it one of the most durable materials available for countertops. Best of all, quartz has nearly unlimited options for colors and patterns and can imitate other natural materials. It is even virtually maintenance free as it requires no sealing or special care, just a simple regular cleaning routine.



![](https://morriskitchensllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Krohn-kitchen-design-3-website-2048x1365.jpg.webp)

## Wood

Wood countertops bring a natural, unparalleled beauty and warmth to any kitchen design. There are many varieties to choose from, and each wood countertop will be unique depending on the wood species, finish, and the natural variations in the wood grain. It does require more careful maintenance and cleaning as it can be damaged. It is not nonporous so it must be resealed regularly, and it may need to be refinished over time.



![](https://morriskitchensllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Laminate-display-revisedc-2048x1363.jpg.webp)

## Laminate

Laminate countertops are a more affordable option and come in a limitless variety of style options, colors, and patterns. It is also a lightweight material, which means it is easier and less expensive to install. Laminate is stain-resistant and easy-to-maintain, though it can be scratched, dented, and damaged by hot objects.
