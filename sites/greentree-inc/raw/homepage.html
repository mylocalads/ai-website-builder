Welcome To GreenTree, Inc

Since 1984, GreenTree Inc., has been providing high-quality landscape maintenance services to some of the most well-known properties throughout South Florida.  We are a local Minority Certified family owned and run business dedicated solely to landscape maintenance. Our unwavering commitment to excellence is evident in every project we undertake and has helped us build a strong reputation as one of South Florida’s premiere commercial landscape maintenance companies.

With a specific focus on commercial clients, we bring landscapes to life, nurturing their environment and enhancing the natural beauty of their surroundings. Our diligent approach, emphasis on safety, and meticulous care for your property set us apart as an industry leader
