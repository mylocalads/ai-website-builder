faqs:
  - q: How often should commercial hedges be pruned?
    a: It depends on the species and the look the property needs, but South Florida's growing season means most formal hedges need attention considerably more often than an annual visit. We set the cycle when we walk the property and hold to it.
  - q: Why does our hedge look bad again a month after it is cut?
    a: Usually because it was sheared rather than pruned. Shearing the outer surface forces dense new growth at the tips and bare wood underneath, so the shape collapses quickly. Cutting to the plant's growth habit takes longer and holds far longer.
  - q: Do you handle tree work?
    a: GreenTree is dedicated solely to landscape maintenance — hedge, shrub and ornamental pruning within a maintenance scope. Large tree work is a different trade with different equipment and certification, and we will tell you when a job falls outside what we do.
