faqs:
  - q: Do you repair irrigation systems or only inspect them?
    a: Inspection is what sits inside the maintenance scope, on a schedule. When an inspection turns up a fault we report it and quote the repair separately, so the recurring cost stays predictable and you can see exactly what you are approving.
  - q: How would we know a zone has stopped running?
    a: Usually you would not, until the turf shows it — which is the entire argument for inspecting on a cycle. A zone that stops in May can cost a replanting by August.
  - q: Can bad irrigation be mistaken for a lawn problem?
    a: Constantly. Dry patches, thinning turf and edge dieback get blamed on the landscape contractor when the actual cause is a broken head or an arc pointing at a pavement. Checking coverage first is what stops a property treating the wrong problem for a season.
