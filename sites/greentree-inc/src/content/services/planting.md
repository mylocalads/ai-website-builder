faqs:
  - q: Is planting included in a maintenance contract?
    a: It is quoted as a separate line item rather than folded into the recurring figure, so you can see what you are approving. Once installed, the new plant material comes under the maintenance scope like everything else.
  - q: How do you choose what to plant?
    a: By the conditions in the specific bed — light, drainage, salt and wind exposure, irrigation coverage, and how much foot traffic it takes. Forty-two years in South Florida is mostly a long record of which plants survive where.
  - q: Can you work around our tenants and visitors?
    a: Yes. Commercial properties are occupied, and installation is scheduled around that. It is one of the practical differences between crews built for commercial sites and crews built for driveways.
