faqs:
  - q: How often will your crews be on our property?
    a: On a fixed schedule agreed before the contract begins. Frequency depends on turf area, plant material and the season — South Florida's growing season is long, so most commercial properties need more visits here than the same property would need further north.
  - q: Is Integrated Pest Management included in a maintenance contract?
    a: Yes. IPM is part of the standard maintenance scope rather than an add-on. It means identifying what is actually affecting the turf or plant material and treating that, instead of running a blanket application schedule.
  - q: What happens if the turf develops brown patches?
    a: We diagnose before we treat. Brown patches in South Florida are usually mowing height, chinch bug pressure, thatch or an irrigation fault — and they need completely different responses. Treating the wrong one wastes a season.
  - q: Do you cut every lawn at the same height?
    a: No. Mowing height is set for the turf species on your property. St. Augustine cut too short stresses and browns out, which is one of the most common causes of a commercial lawn failing under a new contractor.
