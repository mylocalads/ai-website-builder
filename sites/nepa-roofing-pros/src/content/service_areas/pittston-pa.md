Pittston is across the river from our shop — a five-minute drive — and between the city and the boroughs around it, it is some of the most frequent work on our schedule.

We tend to quote two quite different kinds of roof here in the same afternoon. Downtown, the older row-style housing means party-wall flashing, shared eaves and almost no room to stage, so the job is as much about access and control as about shingles. On the slopes above the city, the detached homes are steeper and more wind-exposed, which changes the fastening pattern and makes the SureNail reinforced nailing zone in the Owens Corning Duration Series shingles we install worth what it costs.

Whichever yours is, the process does not change: we get on the roof, tell you what we actually found, and leave a written itemised price the same day where we can.
