Kingston sits across the Susquehanna from Wilkes-Barre, about twenty minutes from our shop.

The thing homeowners here underestimate is the trees. Kingston's mature street canopy is one of the nicest things about the borough and it fills gutters relentlessly. Once a gutter is packed, water backs up over the front edge instead of running to the downspout — which is a nuisance in October and a real problem in January, because that standing water at the eave is exactly where the freeze starts. The ice dam that forms then pushes meltwater back up under the bottom courses of shingle until it finds the decking.

So on Kingston roofs we tend to look at the drainage as hard as the roof. Seamless gutter sized to the roof area actually draining into it, pitched so it runs, downspouts placed where the water can get clear of the foundation — backed by our five-year workmanship warranty — alongside the eave detailing and ventilation on the roof itself. Doing both together is cheaper than doing them a year apart, because you only pay for the access once.
