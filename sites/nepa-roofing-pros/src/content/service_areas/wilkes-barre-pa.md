Wilkes-Barre is fifteen minutes from our shop and one of the two cities we build most of our work around.

The thing that changes a roof replacement here is not the roof — it is the lot. Houses sit close together across the Wyoming Valley, so there is frequently nowhere convenient to drop tear-off, the neighbour's property line is a few feet from the base of the ladder, and everything coming off the roof has to be controlled on the way down rather than thrown. That is why we clear debris as we go instead of leaving it piled in a driveway overnight, and why the yard gets swept for nails before anyone packs a truck. Customers mention cleanup in our reviews more than anything else, and this is the kind of street where it earns the mention.

We handle residential shingle and metal work across Downtown, South Wilkes-Barre, Parsons, Miners Mills and the North End, and flat and low-slope commercial systems on the buildings in between. Estimates are free and usually same-day.
