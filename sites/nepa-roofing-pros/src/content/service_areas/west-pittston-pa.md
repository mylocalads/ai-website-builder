Our shop is at 36 Luzerne Avenue, so West Pittston is not a service area to us — it is where we are. If you are in the borough we can usually be on your roof the same day you call.

The borough sits right on the Susquehanna, and its older housing stock throws up more junctions per square foot than newer development does: porch roofs meeting walls, rear additions at a different pitch from the main roof, and low-slope extensions tacked on over the years. Every one of those is a flashing detail, and flashing details are where roofs leak. Add a winter of freeze-thaw working at them and you have the reason so many West Pittston ceilings stain in February while the roof looks fine from the street.

We install Owens Corning Duration Series shingles, standing seam and rib panel metal, and flat and EPDM systems on the commercial buildings around town. Estimates are free and written, and being five minutes away means we can get to a leak quickly.
