We install Owens Corning Duration Series shingles on most of the homes we work on. The SureNail Technology in them is not marketing: it is a reinforced fabric strip through the nailing zone, so the fastener grips an engineered layer instead of the shingle mat alone. That is the part that decides whether the roof is still on after a wind event.

What actually fails on a shingle roof in Northeastern Pennsylvania is rarely the middle of the roof. It is the eaves. Snow sits, meltwater runs down to a cold overhang and refreezes, and the ice dam that builds there pushes water back up under the bottom courses until it finds the decking and then your ceiling. Every roof we put on is detailed for that — the eave protection, the flashing at every valley and penetration, and the ventilation that keeps the deck cold enough that the cycle does not start in the first place.

Most residential tear-offs and replacements are finished in a single day. The estimate is free, written and itemised, and we can usually get out to you the same day you call.
