Not every roof needs replacing, and we will tell you when yours does not — even though the replacement is worth more to us.

Most of the leaks we get called to in Northeastern Pennsylvania are not worn-out shingles. They are details: a flashing that was reused when it should have been replaced, a valley that was never lapped correctly, a penetration around a chimney or skylight, or an ice dam at a cold eave pushing meltwater back up under the bottom courses in February. That last one is the reason so many local ceilings stain in winter while the roof looks perfectly sound from the driveway. Fixing it properly means dealing with the eave detailing and usually the ventilation as well, not patching where the stain appeared.

Low-slope and EPDM roofs fail differently again — at the seams and around drainage. We advise on the right material for the repair before we start, obtain the permits the work needs, and come back after it is done to check it is holding.
