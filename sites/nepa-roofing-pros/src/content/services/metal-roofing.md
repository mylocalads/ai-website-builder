Metal is what people choose when they would rather not think about the roof again. We install standing seam, concealed-fastener shingle-look panels, rib panel and stone-coated systems across residential, agricultural and light commercial buildings.

Panel-Loc Plus gives those projects superior durability with an attractive appearance. Our CF Shingle was designed specifically for shingle styling with concealed fastening, so you get the look without a field of exposed screws. Stone-coated systems sit somewhere between the two.

The argument for metal in Northeastern Pennsylvania is not just longevity. A metal surface sheds snow instead of holding a load against the roof all winter, which takes pressure off the whole assembly during exactly the months that break asphalt roofs around here. We still detail the eaves, flashing and ventilation properly — the material does not excuse the workmanship — but it starts the job from a better place.
