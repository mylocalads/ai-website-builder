Monitoring your roof's condition is essential for protecting your home. Recognizing the signs that it's time to contact a trusted roofing company can help prevent extensive damage to your home's structure. Here's what our team looks for on inspections across Northeast Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

## Visible damage

Cracked or missing shingles, sagging areas, or debris accumulation on the roof are all signs that something needs attention before it gets worse.

## Water leaks

Water stains on your ceiling or walls — or even a dripping sound during rain — usually mean water has already found a way in, even if the roof looks fine from the ground.

## An aging roof

If your roofing system is approaching the 20-year mark, it's worth having it inspected even if you haven't noticed any obvious problems yet.

## Granule loss

Finding granules from your shingles collecting in your gutters is a sign the shingles are wearing down and losing their protective coating.

## High energy bills

A poorly insulated or damaged roof can let conditioned air escape, driving up your heating and cooling costs.

## Moss or algae growth

Moss and algae growth indicates moisture retention on the roof surface, which can harm your shingles and the decking underneath over time.
