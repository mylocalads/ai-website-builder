Yes, you can buy each of our plans individually.

#### Is there a commitment required?

We have 3-month (minimum), 6-month, and 12-month commitment offers. Terms of service apply.

#### Are the leads shared?

Never! All of our leads are generated directly from direct response campaigns, showing your brand, and we do not share lead information with multiple contractors at the same time. This creates high-intent leads for your business, and a much higher chance to close more jobs.

#### How are these leads generated?

Every lead we generate for you is generated with direct response campaigns across search engines and social media platforms. That means, a) these leads see your brand and offer in the ad, and b) they have decided to inquire about your services.

We do not reactivate stale leads. We do not scrape & sell contact lists. We do not sell shared leads.

#### How will I receive new leads?

You will receive leads via email, and your leads will also be available in their CRM account if the access was purchased.

Yes, our leads are qualified before they reach your inbox. Phone numbers and Emails are also 'look-up' verified, to make sure your team is not bogged down by spam leads. Lastly, we have human(s) in the loop to quality check each lead generated to ensure quality.

Yes! All of our leads come with a TCPA compliant certificate and full session recording of their sign-up to make sure that you can safely follow up with these leads.

No! We only sell direct-response leads, meaning when our leads sign-up with you, they just converted from an ad online, and the lead wants to take action now! We do not re-sell or buy stale or old leads.

Since we do not scrape leads, or re-sell old leads, we need about 5-20 business days after our initial setup to generate a consistent flow of leads for you.
