Hilldale is the other Plains Township village on our list, strung along North Main Street north of
Wilkes-Barre. Like Hudson it gets skipped by services that only think in borough names. Lots here
tend to be a little deeper than the city average, which usually means a real back lawn worth putting
on a weekly cut rather than the front-strip-and-trim work we do closer in.
