Larksville climbs from the valley floor up onto Larksville Mountain, and the properties change
completely as you go up — flat and close-set at the bottom, then large sloping hillside lots with
woodland behind them. The high ground is steep enough that mowing direction and machine choice
actually matter for safety, and it drains fast, so those lawns brown off in a July dry spell well
before the ones down by the river.
