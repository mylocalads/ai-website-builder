Plains Township covers a lot of ground for a township this size, and the properties change character
as you move through it — mining-era homes on tight streets near the Wilkes-Barre line, then newer
subdivisions with real front lawns off the Route 315 corridor. We handle both, and the two need
different schedules: the older lots are trim-heavy and quick to mow, the newer ones are open turf
that wants a consistent weekly cut.
