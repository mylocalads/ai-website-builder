Hudson is a village inside Plains Township rather than a borough of its own, which is why it does
not always show up on a service area map even when a company covers it. We do cover it, and have
done for years. It shares the Plains pattern — older homes on modest lots with a scattering of newer
builds — and it runs on the same schedule as the rest of the township.
