Wilkes-Barre is the middle of our route and the city we work most. The housing stock runs old and
close-set, so lots are small, gates are narrow and there is rarely room to swing a wide deck — we
run walk-behinds here for a reason. The mature street trees that make blocks like the Heights and
South Wilkes-Barre pleasant also drop a serious leaf load every fall, which is why the autumn clean-
up matters more in this city than almost anywhere else on our list.
