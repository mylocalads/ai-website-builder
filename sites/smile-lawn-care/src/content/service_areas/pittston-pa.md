Pittston sits on the Susquehanna between Wilkes-Barre and Scranton, and it is a hill town — a lot of
the residential streets run steeply down toward the river. Slope is the thing that changes the work
here: it dictates which mower goes on the trailer, and it makes bed edges wash out faster, so mulch
and edging need refreshing more often than they do on the flat.
