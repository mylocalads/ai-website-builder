Forty Fort is one of the tidier boroughs on the west side and the standard on its residential
streets is noticeably high — neighbours notice an unmown lawn here within a few days. The properties
nearer the levee and the river flats hold moisture longer after rain, which means the grass keeps
growing when higher ground has stalled, and it means we sometimes shift a visit rather than rut a
soft lawn with a machine.
