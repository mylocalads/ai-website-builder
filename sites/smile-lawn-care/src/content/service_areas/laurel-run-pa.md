Laurel Run is a tiny borough on the mountain east of Wilkes-Barre, wooded and spread out along Route
115. Properties here are rural in character — big lots, long drives, trees on every boundary. That
makes fall clean-up the main event of the year rather than a tidy-up, and it makes snow clearing a
genuinely different job: a long mountain driveway holds more snow and glazes over harder than
anything on the valley floor.
