Laflin is one of the smallest boroughs we cover — a few hundred residents on the hillside between
Plains and Pittston. Lots are larger than the valley floor average and back onto woodland, which
brings the two jobs that come with a tree line: leaf volume in the fall, and growth pushing in from
the boundary every summer if nobody keeps cutting it back.
