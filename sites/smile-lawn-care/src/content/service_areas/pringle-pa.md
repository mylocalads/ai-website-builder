Pringle is among the smallest boroughs in Pennsylvania — a handful of streets between Luzerne and
Kingston, and you can walk the whole thing in a few minutes. Practically speaking that means we are
already there: Pringle falls inside the same morning as Luzerne and Kingston, so a small property
here does not carry the travel surcharge it would from a service based further out.
