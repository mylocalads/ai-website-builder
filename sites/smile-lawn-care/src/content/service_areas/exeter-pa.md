Exeter runs up the west bank past West Pittston, and the borough is mostly settled residential
streets with established plantings — hedges and foundation beds that have been in the ground for
decades. Overgrown shrubs are the common ask here rather than mowing alone. Cutting a mature hedge
back hard is fine if it is done at the right time of year, and we will tell you before we start if
what you want will leave bare wood showing for a season.
