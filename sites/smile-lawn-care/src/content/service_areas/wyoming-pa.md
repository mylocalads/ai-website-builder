Wyoming borough carries some of the oldest history in the valley — the Wyoming Monument commemorates
the 1778 battle, and the residential streets around it are proud, well-kept and visible. Kerb appeal
counts for a lot on these blocks, which is really a question of edges: a crisp bed line and a clean
trim along the walk read as looked-after even in a week when the grass has barely grown.
