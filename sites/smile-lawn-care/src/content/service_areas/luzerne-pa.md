Luzerne borough is small and compact, wedged between Kingston, Swoyersville and Larksville, with the
hillside rising behind it. Properties are modest and close together, which makes it efficient to
service several on the same street on the same morning. If you have neighbours who also want the
lawn cut, say so — a cluster on one block is cheaper for us to run and we price it that way.
