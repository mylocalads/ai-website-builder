Kingston is the largest borough on the west bank and the one with the heaviest tree canopy — the
streets around Nesbitt Park are shaded most of the day. Shade changes everything about lawn care:
turf grows thinner and slower, so it wants a higher cut and less feeding than a sunny lawn, and the
north-facing concrete greens up with algae, which is where the pressure washing work comes from.
