Shavertown sits up on the Back Mountain in Kingston Township, and it is the most different property
type we service. Lots are bigger, the ground is higher and cooler, and there is far more tree cover
than down in the valley — the growing season runs slightly behind Wilkes-Barre and the leaf load in
October is heavy. Larger open turf is a better fit for a wide deck and a proper mowing pattern, so a
Shavertown lawn often costs less per square foot to maintain than a cramped city lot.
