Moosic is the one town on our list in Lackawanna County rather than Luzerne, sitting up the valley
toward Scranton near PNC Field. It is the northern end of our route, so we schedule it in a block
with Pittston and Laflin rather than as a one-off trip — that keeps the drive time out of your
price, which is the honest reason some services quote higher the further out you are.
