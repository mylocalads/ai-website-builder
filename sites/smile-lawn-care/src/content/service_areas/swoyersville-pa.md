Swoyersville is a former coal patch town, and it still has the layout of one: a tight grid of long
narrow lots between Kingston and Luzerne. Access is the daily problem — back yards reached through a
gate rather than off the street. We size the equipment to get through, which sounds obvious until a
service turns up with a mower that does not fit and quotes you for the front lawn only.
