Edwardsville is a dense west-side borough of narrow lots packed tight along the grid off Main
Street. Most properties here are more trimming than mowing — a small patch of front lawn, a strip
down the side, a back yard with fence line on three sides. That is fiddly work, and it is the kind
of property a big crew rushes. Being five minutes away means we can do it properly and still keep
the price sensible.
