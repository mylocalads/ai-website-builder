A mown lawn with overgrown beds around it still reads as neglected. Landscape maintenance is the rest of the property: shrubs and hedges trimmed back into shape, bed edges re-cut so the line stays crisp, weeds pulled out of the beds and stone rather than sprayed and left standing, and dead growth cut out before it spreads.

We can fold this into a mowing schedule so it happens on a rotation, or come out for a one-off reset if the beds have got away from you.
