Two clean-ups carry a Wyoming Valley property through the year. The spring reset clears winter debris, cuts back dead growth and gets the beds and edges ready before the first mow.

The fall clean-up is the important one: the mature maples and oaks in these neighbourhoods drop a leaf load heavy enough to mat down and smother turf over winter, and a lawn that goes under leaves in November comes out thin and patchy in April.

We also take on one-off clearing — overgrown boundaries, properties that have sat empty, rentals between tenants — and everything we cut gets hauled away rather than piled at the kerb.
