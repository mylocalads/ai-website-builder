Mowing is where most of our customers start, and it is the service the schedule is built around. You get a set day each week or fortnight, a cut height chosen for the season rather than one setting all year, and trim lines cut around beds, walks and fence posts. Clippings come off the hard surfaces before we load up.

Blades are kept sharp — a torn grass blade browns at the tip and takes days longer to recover than a cut one, which is the real reason two lawns mowed the same week can look completely different by Thursday.
