Weed control is mostly a question of timing. Crabgrass has to be stopped before it germinates in spring, because once it is up in July nothing will remove it without taking the lawn with it. Broadleaf weeds like dandelion and clover treat far better in fall, when the plant is pulling nutrients down into the root. Spraying everything in midsummer — which is when most people get around to it — is the least effective option available.

Feeding matters just as much: a thick lawn crowds weeds out on its own, and thin turf is an invitation. In the beds we pull by hand rather than spray and leave the dead growth standing.
