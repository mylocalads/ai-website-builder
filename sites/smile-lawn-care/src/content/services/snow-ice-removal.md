Winter here does not arrive politely. We clear driveways, walks and steps by plow or snow blower depending on the property, then treat the surface so the melt does not refreeze into a sheet overnight — which is where most of the falls happen. Clearing right down to the surface matters for the same reason: a packed inch left behind glazes over and gets harder to shift with every storm.

We service the same properties we mow in the summer, which is the tidiest way to run it. Book before the first storm rather than during it — routes fill up, and once it is snowing we are working through a list that was set weeks earlier.
