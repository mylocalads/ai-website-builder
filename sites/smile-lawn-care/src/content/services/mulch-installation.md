  - q: "How deep should mulch go?"

  - q: "How often does it need redoing?"
    a: "Once a year for most beds, usually in spring. Colour fades first and the depth drops as it breaks down into the soil, which is the part doing your beds good."
  - q: "Do you pile mulch up against the tree trunk?"

Mulch is the fastest visual improvement available on most properties, and it does real work: it holds moisture through July, keeps soil temperature steady, and smothers the weed seed already sitting in the bed.

We edge and prep first, because mulch laid over a soft edge and standing weeds looks tidy for about three weeks. Then it goes down at a depth that actually suppresses growth without burying the crowns of your plants or piling up against tree trunks.
