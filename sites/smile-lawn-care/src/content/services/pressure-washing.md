Concrete and siding in this climate collect a green-black film of algae and mildew — the shaded north side of a house and anything under a tree line go first. Most of what people read as a stained driveway is that growth sitting on top of the concrete rather than damage to it.

We wash at a pressure matched to the surface: hard enough to strip the growth off concrete and pavers, gentle enough on siding, soft-washed on older wood so we are not tearing up the grain.

It pairs naturally with a spring clean-up — the walks and the driveway are usually the last thing left looking dirty once the beds are done.
