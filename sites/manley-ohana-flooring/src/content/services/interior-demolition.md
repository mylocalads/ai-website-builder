faqs:
  - q: Do you handle the permits?
    a: Interior, non-structural tear-out of the kind we do — floors, drywall, cabinets, trim — typically does not require a permit in Central Florida, but anything structural or anything touching mechanical, electrical or plumbing does, and that belongs with your general contractor. We will tell you plainly which side of that line your job sits on.
  - q: Can you demo just the kitchen while we live in the house?
    a: Yes, and it is most of what we do. The work area gets sealed, a clean route is kept for load-out, and we take it in stages so the rest of the house stays usable.
  - q: Who takes the debris?
    a: We do. Haul-off and disposal are part of the price unless you would rather supply your own dumpster, in which case we will load it.
