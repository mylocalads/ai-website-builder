faqs:
  - q: How long does it take to remove tile from a whole house?
    a: With a ride-on scraper, most single-storey Orlando homes come up in one to two days including haul-off, where a hand-demo crew would be there most of a week. The variable is what is under the tile — a mortar bed or a second layer of old tile underneath adds time, which is why we ask for a photo before quoting.
  - q: Will removing tile damage my baseboards or cabinets?
    a: It does not have to. The machine gives the operator far more control than a chisel and hammer does, and the edges and thresholds get worked by hand where the machine cannot reach cleanly. Hand-carved doors, cabinet toe-kicks and finished jambs are the parts we slow down for.
  - q: Do you remove the thinset too?
    a: Yes, and you should insist on it from anyone you hire. Ridges of leftover thinset will show straight through vinyl plank and laminate and will disqualify most manufacturers' warranties. Grinding the slab back flat and clean is part of the job, not an upsell.
