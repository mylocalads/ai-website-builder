faqs:
  - q: Can you work in an occupied office building?
    a: Yes — a good share of our commercial work is done around tenants who cannot close. We take corridors and suites in sections, keep a clean walkable path, and schedule the loudest passes around whatever hours you need protected.
  - q: Is old glue under vinyl a problem?
    a: It is the whole problem. Mastic and cutback adhesive left on a slab will bleed through and prevent a new floor from bonding, so it gets ground off as part of removal. If you are quoting this out elsewhere, ask specifically whether adhesive removal is included — often it is not.
  - q: Do you remove the tack strip and staples on carpet jobs?
    a: Yes. Tack strip, staples and pad all come out and go with the debris. Leaving a perimeter of tack strip for the next trade is not finishing the job.
