faqs:
  - q: Why can't my installer just lay the new floor over the residue?
    a: Some will, and it is the most common reason a new floor fails early. Adhesive residue stops glue-down products bonding, and any ridge or high spot under LVP or laminate shows through as a visible line and works the joints loose. Most manufacturers exclude that damage from warranty as an installation defect.
  - q: How do you keep grinding dust out of the house?
    a: The grinder runs shrouded and connected to a vacuum, so the dust is captured at the head rather than released into the room. We also seal the openings to areas we are not working in. It is not a zero-dust process, but it is a fundamentally different one from open grinding.
  - q: Can you do prep only, if another company pulled the floor?
    a: Yes. Prep-only calls are common — usually from a homeowner or an installer who has found the slab in worse condition than expected. Send a photo of what you are looking at and we will price the grind on its own.
