faqs:
  - q: Is hardwood removal less messy than tile?
    a: Less silica dust, yes — but glue-down wood produces adhesive residue and nail-down produces a lot of splintered debris and hundreds of fasteners. We still contain the work area and still haul everything out, because a floor full of loose staples is not a floor anyone should be walking on.
  - q: Can you tell what is under my wood floor before starting?
    a: Usually from a photo of an edge or a threshold, plus one lifted board if there is a spare closet. Knowing whether it is nailed, glued or floating is what makes the quote accurate, so it is worth the two minutes before we price it.
  - q: What if there is another floor underneath?
    a: It is common in older Central Florida homes — wood over vinyl, or wood over the original tile. We will tell you what we find and price the second layer separately rather than discovering it mid-job and handing you a change order.
