## Why Choose a Metal Roof?

Metal roofing is quickly becoming a popular roofing option for home and business owners alike. We offer metal roofing to fit any style or budget, fabricated in either standing seam or architectural shingle styles. Some of the benefits are that metal roofs are long lasting and require little maintenance.

Metal roofs provide some of the best aesthetics in Pennsylvania roofing to date, but there are some practical reasons for choosing it as your material as well.

- **Durability** – The life expectancy of your metal roof should be roughly as long as that of your home itself. Metal stands up exceptionally well to a variety of damages, weather, and sun, and is generally proven to last in excess of 50 years.
- **Structural soundness** – You may not think it at first, but metal is usually much lighter than many other roofing materials. This reduces the load on your home and alleviates structural stress.
- **Fire resistance** – Metal roofs boast the best fireproof rating amongst all roofing materials.
- **Simple and quick installation** – Metal roofs are generally quite easy to install and take very little time overall, ensuring the job is done efficiently.
- **Versatility** – With numerous styles and materials available, there’s a metal roof to suit any homeowner’s tastes.

### Metal Roof Types

# Rib Panel Metal Roofing

Panel metal gives residential, agricultural, and light commercial projects the protection of steel, with an attractive appearance. It features an extra striation on top of the rib to give it superior durability and protection against leaking. Panel metal is available in 3 qualities: Standard, Prime, and Ultra.

# Metal Roofing Pros and Cons

Metal roofing “pros,” the material’s long lifespan is why most homeowners make the switch in either a re-roofing or new construction.

Metal roofing “cons,” a metal roof promises come at a high cost. This material can run from $120 to $900 per 100 square feet (or one “square” of material)

# Standing Seam Metal Roofing

Standing seam metal roofing has metal panels that interlock at the edges to form a seal. It’s divided into two different categories, architectural and structural. Architectural standing seam roofing is the best type of metal roof for steep-sloped roofing. Depending on the design, the panels are either mechanically seamed or snapped together. And, unlike r-panel roofing, architectural roofing requires materials to be laid down beforehand.

# Shingle Look Metal Roofing

Metal Shingles, as beautiful as they are smart. Available in a variety of colors and textures with a look similar to architectural, wood shake, or ceramic shingles. This option is by far lighter than any available asphalt shingle, but they’re also tougher and easier to maintain, while saving you money and looking great.
