Both need someone who will look before quoting. We do slate as a first-class service line rather than an excuse to sell a replacement, and on storm calls we attend the adjuster appointment in person so the claim reflects the damage we documented. Lackawanna County winters are hard on gutters and flashing; those are usually the first things we find.
