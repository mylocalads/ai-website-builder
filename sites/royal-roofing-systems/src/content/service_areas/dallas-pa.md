  The Back Mountain is deck country. Bigger lots, more trees, and houses positioned to make use of the yard, which is why so much of our Dallas work is TREX composite and cedar rather than roofing alone. It is also exposed country: wind comes across these ridges harder than it does down in the valley, and the roofs and gutters up here take it. We build decks to sit steady under snow load, we install roofs specified for that exposure, and we come back to fix the boards a bad winter lifted rather than telling you to live with it.
