faqs:
  - q: Why seamless gutters instead of sectional?
    a: Sectional gutters come with multiple seams, and seams are where leaks and debris buildup start. Seamless aluminum is custom-formed to fit your property in continuous runs, so there is nothing to separate over a Northeastern Pennsylvania winter.
  - q: Which siding material should I choose?
    a: It depends on the look you want and how much maintenance you want to do. Vinyl is the most cost-effective, fiber cement is the most durable, and wood and metal each have their place. We walk through the pros and cons of each on site rather than pushing one.
  - q: Can siding and gutters be financed?
    a: Yes, including 0% options on qualifying projects. Military, first responders and seniors also get 5% off any full gutter installation or repair over $1,500.
