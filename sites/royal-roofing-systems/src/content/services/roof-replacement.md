faqs:
  - q: How do I know whether I need a replacement or just a repair?
    a: A free inspection answers it. Roofs usually need replacing for one of three reasons — age, damage across too much of the surface to patch, or an original installation that was done wrong. If yours is repairable we will tell you, because a repair we do today is a replacement we do for you in five years.
  - q: How long does a roof replacement take?
    a: Most residential roofs are a one to two day job once the materials are on site. Pitch, complexity and weather can extend that, and we tell you up front when we expect it to.
  - q: Will insurance pay for it?
    a: Often, minus your deductible, when wind, hail or a tree caused damage across enough of the roof. We attend your adjuster appointment to make sure they see the same damage we identified, and we follow up with the carrier so you get a decision quickly.
