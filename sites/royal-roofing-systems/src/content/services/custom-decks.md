faqs:
  - q: TREX composite or cedar?
    a: TREX if you would rather not spend a weekend every summer sealing and staining — it cleans easily and does not fade like wood. Cedar if you want the natural look and smell and a surface that stays comfortable underfoot. Both hold up to NEPA rain, snow and summer heat; the difference is upkeep versus character.
  - q: Can you repair the deck I already have?
    a: Usually. We have seen just about everything decks do around here — boards pulling up after a bad winter, railings that wobble, stairs that are no longer safe. We will look at it and tell you honestly whether it is a repair or a rebuild.
  - q: Do you offer financing on decks?
