## Our Remediation & Emergency Services

We provide a broad range of remediation and emergency response services for both residential and commercial properties throughout the Orlando area and surrounding communities, including Winter Park, Dr. Phillips, College Park, Baldwin Park, Lake Nona, and beyond.

### Water Damage Mitigation & Drying

Whether from a storm surge, a burst pipe, a failed appliance, or roof penetration, water damage demands an immediate response. Our team works quickly to assess the extent of the intrusion, extract standing water, and deploy drying and dehumidification equipment to prevent further damage and the mold growth.

### Mold Remediation

Orlando's warm, humid climate creates some of the most favorable conditions for mold in the country. Mold can develop within 24 to 48 hours of moisture exposure, and once it takes hold, it moves fast. Wolfpack Construction provides thorough mold inspection, containment, removal, and treatment, addressing both the visible growth and the underlying moisture source causing it.

### Storm & Wind Damage Response

Central Florida knows storms. Wind and rain can strip roofing, compromise structural integrity, and leave a property wide open to further damage. We respond to storm events with emergency board-up and tarping services to secure the structure, followed by a full damage assessment and a clear plan for restoration and reconstruction.

### Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration

After a fire is extinguished, the damage is far from over. Smoke and soot penetrate walls, ceilings, and HVAC systems. Water from firefighting efforts creates its own set of problems. Wolfpack Construction provides cleanup, smoke removal, odor treatment, and the reconstruction work needed to bring your property back to a safe and livable condition.

### Emergency Board-Up & Structural Securing

When a property is compromised, every hour it sits exposed is an hour of additional risk. We provide emergency board-up services, temporary weather protection, and structural securing to prevent the situation from getting worse while a full remediation plan is developed.

### Reconstruction After Remediation

As a full-service, licensed general contractor in the State of Florida (License #CGC1531295), we don't stop at cleanup. We handle the rebuild. That means one company, one point of contact, and a seamless path from emergency response all the way to a fully restored property. You won't be handed off to a third party when the hard part is over.
