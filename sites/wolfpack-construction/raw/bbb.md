# About

## About This Business

Wolfpack Construction provides construction, roofing, and remodeling services, including roof repair, replacement, and installation for asphalt, metal, flat, and tile roofs. The company assists clients with residential and commercial projects, such as kitchen and bathroom remodeling, renovations, additions, gutter installation, and solar panel installation.

**BBB Accredited Since:** 3/20/2024

**Years in Business:** 5
