faqs:
  - q: How do I know whether I need a repair or a full replacement?
    a: Age, the extent of the damage, and the condition of the decking underneath. We inspect all three before recommending anything — and if a repair will genuinely get you several more years, we will say so. That conversation is free.
  - q: Which roofing materials do you install?
    a: Asphalt shingle, metal, tile and flat systems, for both residential and commercial properties. Which one is right depends on your structure, your roofline, your budget and how long you plan to hold the property.
  - q: Will my insurance cover storm damage to my roof?
    a: It depends on your policy and on whether the damage reads as storm-related rather than wear and tear. We document the damage properly and provide the assessment and scope your carrier needs — and because we hold the general contractor's licence, we can carry the job through the full rebuild either way.
  - q: How long does a roof replacement take?
    a: Most residential re-roofs are a matter of days once materials are on site, though pitch, access, roof size and what we find on the decking all move that. We give you a schedule up front and tell you when it changes.
