faqs:
  - q: How quickly do I need to act after water gets in?
    a: Fast. Orlando's warm, humid climate creates some of the most favourable conditions for mold in the country, and it can develop within 24 to 48 hours of moisture exposure. We respond 24/7 for exactly that reason.
  - q: Do you do the rebuild too, or just the cleanup?
    a: Both. That is the point of calling a licensed general contractor rather than a mitigation-only company. We hold Florida licence CGC1531295 and carry the job from emergency response through final reconstruction — you are not sent to find a second contractor at the worst possible moment.
  - q: What do you do first after a storm?
    a: Secure the structure. Emergency board-up, tarping and temporary weather protection stop the situation getting worse, then we complete a full damage assessment and give you a clear plan for restoration and reconstruction.
  - q: Do you work with insurance?
    a: We document the damage thoroughly and provide the assessment and scope your carrier needs. We keep the paperwork moving so you are not the one on the phone fighting for coverage while your property sits open.
