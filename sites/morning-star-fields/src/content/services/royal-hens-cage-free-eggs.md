faqs:
  - q: What makes Certified Humane different from plain cage-free?
    a: A cage-free label does not specify how much space a hen gets, does not require outdoor access, and is not enforced by inspection. Certified Humane is audited by third-party veterinarians and animal-welfare scientists against published standards. We hold both.
  - q: How fresh are the eggs when they reach me?
    a: Laid, packed and shipped within 48 hours. Because we deliver inside roughly 250 miles of Vero Beach rather than trucking across the country, cases arrive days fresher than a national blend.
  - q: Why are the yolks so orange?
    a: Diet. Our hens eat marigolds and leafy greens grown on the farm, and that carotenoid content is what deepens the yolk colour. It happens naturally rather than through an additive.
