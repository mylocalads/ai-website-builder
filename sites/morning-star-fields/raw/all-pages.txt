About Us
Good eggs do not travel far. They start right here in Florida.
At Morning Star Fields, we keep things close to home, with eggs that are laid, collected, and packaged on our family-owned farm. Established in 2022, we have grown into one of Florida’s most certified local farms by focusing on a system that works in harmony with the land and our hens.
Through our BioCircular approach, resources are reused, waste is minimized, and every part of the farm has a purpose. It is a thoughtful, closed-loop way of producing eggs you can feel good about, from our farm to your table.

Why biocircular?
A Better Way to Farm Starts With a Better System
At Morning Star Fields, BioCircular farming is the foundation of how we operate. We designed our farm as a closed-loop system where energy, water, nutrition, and natural resources work together to reduce waste and create a more responsible approach to food production.
A Closed-Loop Farming System
Our BioCircular model focuses on recapturing, reusing, and returning resources back into the farm:
Renewable Energy: Integrating solar power to support responsible energy use.
Water Stewardship: Capturing and reclaiming water to reduce waste and protect resources.
Natural Fertilizer: Transforming hen manure into valuable fertilizer pellets.
Integrated Nutrition: Reusing egg wash water to support hydroponic growing systems.
Every part of our farm is connected, creating a system built around efficiency, sustainability, and stewardship.
Caring for Our Land, Hens, and Future
BioCircular farming is about more than production. It is about building a system that supports long-term environmental responsibility, animal care, and the future of agriculture.
As a Certified Humane, family-owned farm, Morning Star Fields prioritizes hen welfare through thoughtful barn design, responsible practices, and a safe environment where our hens can thrive.
Located in Vero Beach, Florida, we are committed to creating a more responsible food system through innovation, transparency, and respect for the resources that make farming possible.

Frequently Asked Questions
Please reach us at
MSFMarketing@MorningStarFields.com
if you cannot find an answer to your question.
What is Morning Star Fields?
We are a biocircular farm in Vero Beach. We grow food the way nature does it best, with care, reuse, and respect for the land.
What do you produce?
Fresh eggs are our main focus today, but we're expanding to include 100% organic chicken manure fertilizer, hydroponic flowers, herbs, and seasonal farm products. Every part of our farm works together in a biocircular system designed to make the most of every resource.
What does biocircular mean?
It means nothing goes to waste if we can help it. What the farm produces helps the farm right back.
Do you use chemicals or antibiotics?
Nope. We keep things natural and simple. The old fashioned way works just fine.
Why are your eggs different?
Our eggs come from hens raised in state of the art facilities with certified humane care. They get fresh air, space to move, and cage free living. Happy hens, better eggs, that is the simple truth.
Where do your eggs go?
We are based in Vero Beach. Our eggs are laid, packed, and shipped within 48 hours. Right now we deliver B2B within about 250 miles across Florida, keeping it local so everything stays fresh and close to home.
Can I visit the farm?
We are not open to the public at this time. We are focused on keeping the farm running smoothly and producing the best quality eggs and products for our partners.
Is it only a chicken farm?
Not at all. We’ve got cows, goats, pigs, two dogs, a cat, and even a roaming alligator that is not part of the family but likes to hang around outside.
