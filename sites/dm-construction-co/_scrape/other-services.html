We Offer Other Professional Services In Cape Coral, Florida & Surrounding Areas in Southwest Florida

In addition to our core remodeling and construction work, we offer a wide range of complementary services designed to enhance, maintain, and transform your property. From drywall installation and repairs to flooring upgrades, interior and exterior painting, siding, and stucco, our team delivers consistent quality and attention to detail across every project—no matter the size.

What types of lending options does Acorn Finance offer?

PERSONAL LOANS: A personal loan is a financial product that allows a customer to borrow money now, and pay it back over time by making fixed monthly payments. Personal loans have fixed rates of interest and typically have terms ranging from 2 to 12 years. They also may be unsecured (no collateral) or secured (collateral required) depending on the specifics of the offer.

PERSONAL LINE OF CREDIT: A line of credit is a financial product that customers can borrow from at any time based on a preset limit. As long as the customer's account is open and their credit remains in good standing, they can borrow up to the maximum amount and pay interest only on the amount you actually borrow. As it is paid off over time, that credit may once again become available for the customer to borrow from. 

How much can you borrow with Acorn Finance?

 Acorn Finance's network of lenders extends loans from $2,000 up to $100,000 for qualified consumers.  

How long does the loan approval process take?

Qualified applicants can be approved quickly during business hours and may receive their funds as soon as within 1 business day.
