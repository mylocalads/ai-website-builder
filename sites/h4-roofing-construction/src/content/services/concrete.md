faqs:
  - q: Why does so much concrete around here crack?
    a: Freeze-thaw. Water gets into the base or the slab, freezes, expands, and lifts. Proper base prep, drainage away from the pour, and control joints cut at the right spacing are what separate flatwork that lasts from flatwork that does not.
  - q: Can you replace an existing driveway?
    a: Yes. Tear-out and haul-away of the old slab is part of the job, and it is also the moment to correct any grading problem that has been sending water toward the house.
  - q: Do you do concrete work alongside roofing projects?
    a: We do. Being one company across the roof, the exterior and the flatwork means the drainage is thought about as a whole — where the gutters discharge and where the driveway slopes are the same conversation.
