faqs:
  - q: The stain on my ceiling is small. Does the whole sheet need replacing?
    a: Not always, but the visible stain is usually smaller than the wet area. We check the extent of the moisture rather than the size of the mark, and cut back to sound board — patching over saturated drywall is how a stain comes back through the paint months later.
  - q: Can you match my existing ceiling texture?
    a: In most cases yes. Texture matching is the difference between a repair you notice every time you walk in the room and one you forget about.
  - q: Should I fix the drywall before or after the roof?
    a: After, always. Repairing the interior while the leak is still live means paying for the same ceiling twice.
