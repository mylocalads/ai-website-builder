faqs:
  - q: How long does a roof replacement take?
    a: Most Dayton-area homes are a one to two day job once materials are on site. Steep pitches, multiple existing layers, or decking that needs replacing can add a day. We tell you before we start if we expect it to run long.
  - q: Will you roof over my existing shingles?
    a: No. Roofing over old shingles hides whatever is happening to the decking underneath, and most manufacturer warranties are worth very little on a layover. We tear off to the deck every time.
  - q: What happens if you find rotted decking?
    a: We show you. Soft or rotted decking gets photographed and replaced before the new roof goes on, and the cost of that is discussed with you rather than quietly added at the end.
