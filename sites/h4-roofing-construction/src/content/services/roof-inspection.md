faqs:
  - q: Do you charge for an inspection?
    a: No. Inspections and written estimates are free throughout the Miami Valley.
  - q: How do I know whether hail actually damaged my roof?
    a: Often you cannot tell from the ground, which is exactly what door-knocking storm chasers rely on. Hail bruising shows as soft spots and granule loss that are visible up close. We photograph what we find so you are looking at evidence rather than taking someone's word for it.
  - q: Will you help with my insurance claim?
    a: We document the damage thoroughly so you have what you need to file. Most homeowners policies require a claim within one to two years of the storm, so it is worth getting it looked at rather than waiting.
  - q: Someone told me my roof needs replacing. Should I get a second opinion?
    a: Always. Get it in writing from both, and compare. If we look at your roof and disagree with the first assessment, we will tell you that plainly — we would rather lose a job than sell you a roof you do not need.
