  - q: Can you do siding and roofing at the same time?
    a: Yes, and it is usually the better way to do it. The roof-to-wall transitions are where a lot of leaks start, and having one crew responsible for both sides of that joint removes the argument about whose work is letting water in.

    a: It does, particularly on the elevation the storm came from. Cracks and punctures in vinyl are easy to miss from the ground and are worth checking whenever your roof is being inspected.
