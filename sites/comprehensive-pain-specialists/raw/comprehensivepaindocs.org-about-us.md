# About Comprehensive Pain Specialists in Wilkes-Barre, PA

## At Comprehensive Pain Specialists Clinic, our skilled professionals focus on identifying the root causes of pain and creating personalized treatment plans. Our committed team works to enhance patients’ quality of life, alleviate discomfort, and improve mobility, enabling individuals to better manage pain and regain control of their daily activities. We provide a wide range of services such as neuropathy pain, arthritis, herniated disc, whiplash treatment, facet joint injections, fibromyalgia treatment, sciatica pain treatment, and much more. For more information, please **[contact us](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/contact)** or book an **[appointment online](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/appointments)**. We are conveniently located in **[1177 PA-315, Dolphin Plaza, Wilkes-Barre, PA 18702](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/get-directions)**.
