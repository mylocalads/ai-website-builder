# Meet Our Providers at Comprehensive Pain Specialists in Wilkes-Barre, PA

## At Comprehensive Pain Specialists, our providers are dedicated to delivering compassionate, individualized care. With extensive experience in pain management, they work closely with each patient to create personalized treatment plans aimed at improving quality of life. Our team is committed to helping patients find effective relief and regain their optimal functioning. We are conveniently located in **[1177 PA-315, Dolphin Plaza, Wilkes-Barre, PA 18702](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/get-directions)**. For more information, please **[contact us](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/contact)** or schedule an **[appointment online](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/appointments)**.
