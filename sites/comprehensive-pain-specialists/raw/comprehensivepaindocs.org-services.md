# Pain Management Services at Comprehensive Pain Specialists in Wilkes-Barre, PA

## At Comprehensive Pain Specialists, our pain management services are focused on offering holistic and effective solutions to alleviate chronic pain. Using a range of advanced techniques like targeted injections, non-invasive therapies, and rehabilitation programs, we aim to reduce discomfort and enhance your daily functioning. Each treatment plan is customized to meet your specific needs, ensuring a more personalized approach to healing and improved quality of life. Our services include treatment for back pain, joint pain, neck pain, shoulder pain, hip pain, knee pain, arthritis, osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, sciatica, nerve pain, chronic pain, spinal stenosis, whiplash, concussion, and much more! We are conveniently located in **[1177 PA-315, Dolphin Plaza, Wilkes-Barre, PA 18702](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/get-directions)**. For more information, please **[contact us](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/contact)** or book an **[appointment online](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/appointments)**.
