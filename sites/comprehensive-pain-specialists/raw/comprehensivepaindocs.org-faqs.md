**What days/hours is Comprehensive Pain Specialists open?**

[We are open 5 days a week: Monday – Friday: 8:00AM – 4:00PM, Saturday – Sunday: Closed.](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/hours)

**Where is Comprehensive Pain Specialists located?**

It is located at [1177 PA-315, Dolphin Plaza, Wilkes-Barre, PA 18702](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/get-directions).

**Does Comprehensive Pain Specialists accept insurance?**

We accept multiple insurance companies. **[Contact us](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/contact)** to find out more!

**Does Comprehensive Pain Specialists accept same-day appointments?**

Yes, please **[contact us](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/contact)** or book an **[appointment online](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/appointments)** and we would be happy to help you.

**Do you have a board certified doctor at Comprehensive Pain Specialists?**

Yes. [Dr. Youssef Josephson, DO](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/youssef-josephson) is a board‑certified pain management physician (Pain Medicine/Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation).

**What conditions does Dr. Youssef Josephson, DO treat?**

He treats chronic and acute pain like [back pain](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/back-pain), [neck pain](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/neck-pain), [joint pain](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/joint-pain), nerve pain, [arthritis](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/arthritis), fibromyalgia, CRPS, and post‑surgical pain.

**What types of treatments are offered at Comprehensive Pain Specialists?**

Treatments include [spinal cord stimulation](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/spinal-cord-stimulation), [facet joint injections](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/facet-joint-injections), epidural steroid injections, nerve blocks, prolotherapy, [trigger point injections](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/trigger-point-injections), and other personalized pain management procedures.

**What should I bring to my first appointment?**

Bring completed new patient forms, your medical history, and any relevant imaging/tests — you can fill forms beforehand to save time.

**What type of insurance do you take?**

Most major insurance plans are accepted, but you should verify with your insurance for your specific coverage.

**Do you offer self-pay options for uninsured patients?**

For self-pay options for uninsured patients, it’s recommended to contact the clinic directly for specific pricing and payment arrangements.

**Is Comprehensive Pain Specialists accepting new patients?**

Yes, they are currently [accepting new patients](https://comprehensivepaindocs.org/new-patients) and developing personalized pain care plans.
