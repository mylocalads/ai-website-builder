We treat CRPS, fibromyalgia, myofascial pain syndrome, post-surgical pain, and other complex conditions with sympathetic blocks, spinal cord stimulation, multimodal medication plans, and coordinated rehabilitation. Early intervention changes outcomes substantially in CRPS, so getting these patients evaluated quickly is a priority rather than an afterthought.

faqs:
  - q: Several doctors have told me nothing is wrong. Is it worth another appointment?
    a: Yes. Conditions like CRPS, fibromyalgia, and myofascial pain syndrome do not show up on routine imaging, which is exactly why they get missed. They are diagnosed clinically, and that is what this evaluation is for.
  - q: How quickly should CRPS be treated?
    a: As early as possible. Outcomes in complex regional pain syndrome are meaningfully better with early intervention, which is why we prioritise scheduling suspected CRPS cases.
  - q: Do you treat fibromyalgia?
    a: Yes, as its own diagnosis with its own plan — typically a combination of medication management, trigger point injections where indicated, and coordination with rehabilitation rather than a single intervention.
