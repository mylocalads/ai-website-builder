faqs:
  - q: My headaches start at the base of my skull. Is that a neck problem?
    a: It often is. Cervicogenic headache — pain referred from the upper cervical joints or nerves — is a common and treatable cause of chronic headache, and it responds to targeted blocks rather than to headache medication alone.
  - q: I was in a car accident and my neck still hurts months later. Is it too late?
    a: No. Whiplash pain that persists past the expected recovery window usually has an identifiable source, and that is exactly what the evaluation is for.
  - q: Does Botox for migraine actually work?
    a: For chronic migraine it is an established treatment with a defined protocol, and it is one of the options we offer. Whether it is right for you depends on your headache pattern, which is part of what the consultation establishes.
