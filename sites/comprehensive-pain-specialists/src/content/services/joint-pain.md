For patients who are not surgical candidates, or who want to avoid an operation, interventional pain management often restores meaningful function without ever entering an operating room.

faqs:
  - q: I have been told I need a joint replacement. Is there anything else?
    a: Sometimes. Interventional options can meaningfully reduce pain and delay or avoid surgery for some patients, though not all. The evaluation is where that is determined honestly.
  - q: How many injections will I need?
    a: That depends on the joint, the diagnosis, and how you respond to the first one. You will be told the expected course before anything is scheduled, not after.
  - q: Is the injection guided by imaging?
    a: Image guidance is used where it matters for accuracy. Placement is what makes a joint injection work, and blind injections into the wrong space are a common reason patients report that injections "did nothing."
