faqs:
  - q: What is a spinal cord stimulator and would I need surgery for it?
    a: It is a small implanted device that interrupts pain signals before they reach the brain. It is always trialled first with a temporary lead, so you know whether it works for you before anything permanent is placed.
  - q: My feet burn at night from diabetes. Is that treatable?
    a: Diabetic neuropathy is one of the conditions we treat regularly. Treatment ranges from targeted medication management to peripheral nerve stimulation depending on severity and what has already been tried.
  - q: Is sciatica a back problem or a nerve problem?
    a: Usually both — it is nerve pain caused by something compressing or irritating the nerve root in the spine. Identifying which structure is doing the compressing is what determines the treatment.
