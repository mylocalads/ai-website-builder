faqs:
  - q: Should I repair my AC or replace it?
    a: That is what the diagnosis is for. We test the system, identify the specific failed component, and give you the repair-or-replace recommendation with the reasoning attached. If the unit has life left in it we will tell you, because a repair now and a replacement when it is genuinely due costs you less than replacing early.
  - q: My house is cold but still humid. Why?
    a: Almost always because the system is oversized or the duct work was never balanced. It reaches the set temperature quickly and shuts off before removing enough moisture, leaving you around 55 to 60 percent indoor humidity. Testing and balancing corrects it — that is a different job from swapping the unit.
  - q: Do you pull permits for AC replacements?
    a: Yes. Miami-Dade and Broward require a mechanical permit for HVAC replacement, and unpermitted work voids equipment warranties. We hold the licenses to pull our own.
