faqs:
  - q: Do you pull the permits?
    a: Yes. Permitting is part of the planning phase, not something we hand back to you. Because we hold construction, mechanical, electrical and plumbing licenses, we can pull the permits the project needs rather than waiting on subcontractors to do it.
  - q: Can you handle the mechanical and electrical scope too?
    a: Yes, and that is the main reason clients bring us in. HVAC, plumbing and electrical are in-house, so there is no gap in the schedule where two trades are waiting on each other and no ambiguity about who owns a problem.
  - q: How disruptive is a remodel?
    a: Less than it could be, if it is planned properly. We map the flow of materials, finished goods, sanitation and waste management before starting specifically to keep the disruption contained and predictable.
