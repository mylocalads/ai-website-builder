about_body: Avon is mostly newer subdivision housing, which means the concrete we are asked to look at tends to be twenty years old or less and failing early rather than fairly. Nine times out of ten the cause is drainage — downspouts discharging beside the slab, or a grade that sends water back under the driveway instead of away from it. We fix the cause at the same time as the concrete, because a perfect new slab poured over the same water problem gives you the same result again in a few years.
