about_body: Indianapolis housing stock runs from century-old bungalows on the near-north side to subdivisions built in the last decade, and the concrete tells you which is which. Older drives here are usually thin, unreinforced and sitting on fill that has been moving for fifty winters. Newer ones fail for a different reason — a base that was skimmed rather than compacted before the builder's subcontractor poured over it. Either way the fix starts underneath. If your driveway ties into a city street, Indianapolis requires a driveway permit, and that is ours to file rather than yours.
