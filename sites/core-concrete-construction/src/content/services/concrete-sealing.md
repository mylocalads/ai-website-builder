faqs:
  - q: How often should concrete be resealed?
    a: Every few years for most driveways and patios in this climate, sooner on surfaces that take heavy salt over winter. We will look at yours and tell you where it is in that cycle.
  - q: Can new concrete be sealed straight away?
    a: No. Fresh concrete needs to cure first — normally around 28 days — before a sealer goes on. Sealing too early traps moisture in the slab.
  - q: Will sealing hide existing cracks?
    a: It will not. Sealing protects a sound surface; it is not a repair. If the slab is cracked or spalling we will tell you what it actually needs before quoting a seal.
  - q: Does sealer make concrete slippery?
    a: It can on a smooth slab, which is why the sealer is specified to suit the finish and the location. Tell us if it is a pool surround or a sloped walk.
