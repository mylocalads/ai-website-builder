faqs:
  - q: Can you pour a driveway over an existing gravel drive?
    a: Yes, and it is a common job for us. The work is in the subgrade — the gravel has to be graded, filled where needed and compacted before anything is poured, or the slab will settle unevenly.
  - q: How thick will my driveway be?
    a: Residential drives are typically four inches, going thicker where heavy vehicles will be parking or turning. Your estimator will specify the thickness and any reinforcement on the written estimate.
  - q: How long before I can park on it?
    a: Keep off it on foot for 24 hours and keep vehicles off for about seven days. Concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days, so avoid heavy loads near the edges during the first month.
  - q: Do you take the old concrete away?
    a: Yes. Demolition and haul-away of the existing slab are part of the quote, and the site is cleared and swept before we leave.
