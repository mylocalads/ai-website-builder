faqs:
  - q: Should I choose asphalt or concrete?
    a: Concrete generally lasts longer and takes decorative finishes; asphalt costs less per square foot up front and suits long runs and large areas. We install both, so the recommendation you get from us is not driven by what we happen to sell.
  - q: How soon can I drive on new asphalt?
    a: Usually within a day or two, but keep heavy vehicles off for longer and avoid turning the wheels of a parked car on it in hot weather while it is still fresh.
  - q: Do you also handle drainage?
    a: Yes. We install drainage and replace culverts, and on a paving job the drainage is part of the design rather than an afterthought.
