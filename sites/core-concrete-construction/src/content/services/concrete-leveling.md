faqs:
  - q: Is leveling cheaper than replacing the slab?
    a: Usually, yes — when the concrete itself is still sound and only the ground under it has moved. Once a slab is badly cracked or spalling, replacement is the better use of the money and we will say so.
  - q: How long does leveling take?
    a: Most residential leveling is done in a day, and the surface can normally be walked on shortly afterwards. Your estimator will confirm timing for your job.
  - q: Why did my concrete sink in the first place?
    a: Nearly always water. Downspouts discharging next to the slab, a grade sloping back toward the house, or fill that was never properly compacted. If we do not deal with the cause, the slab will sink again.
  - q: Can you fix a cracked garage floor?
    a: Yes — we have repaired plenty. What the right fix is depends on whether the crack is cosmetic or structural, which is something we need to look at on site.
