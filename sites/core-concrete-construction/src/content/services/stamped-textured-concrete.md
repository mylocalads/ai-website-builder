faqs:
  - q: Is stamped concrete slippery?
    a: The texture is what gives it grip, so a stamped surface is generally no more slippery than a broom finish. If it is going around a pool, tell your estimator and we will specify the sealer accordingly.
  - q: How much more does stamped cost than a broom finish?
    a: More, because the pattern work, colour and release are extra labour and material on top of the same base slab. Your estimate breaks out the flatwork and the finish so you can see the difference.
  - q: Does the colour fade?
    a: Integral colour runs through the concrete rather than sitting on top of it, so it holds up far better than a surface stain. Resealing every few years keeps the colour looking rich and protects the surface.
  - q: Can you match an existing patio or walkway?
    a: Often, yes. Bring us a photo or let us see it on the site visit and we will tell you honestly how close we can get.
