faqs:
  - q: Is exposed aggregate rough on bare feet?
    a: It has more texture than a broom finish, which is the point around a pool. The size of the aggregate makes the difference, so tell your estimator if it is somewhere people will be barefoot.
  - q: Does it need sealing?
    a: We recommend it. Sealing locks the exposed stone in place, keeps water and salt out, and deepens the colour of the aggregate.
  - q: Can I have aggregate as a border rather than the whole slab?
    a: Yes. An aggregate band around a broom-finish driveway is a common and cost-effective way to get the look without pricing the whole area as decorative.
