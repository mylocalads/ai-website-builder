Wilkes-Barre is the city end of our route and the properties show it — older housing stock on tight, close-set lots where there is nowhere to stage a trailer and no room to be careless with a mower.

Small front yards need edging that actually holds a line, back yards run narrow and shaded, and clean-up matters because whatever we leave behind ends up on the neighbour's walk.
