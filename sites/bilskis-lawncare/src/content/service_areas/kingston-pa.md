Kingston sits across the Susquehanna from the city and the lawns here are the kind people notice — flat, well-kept borough lots on a tight street grid where a scruffy edge stands out against the neighbours.

Being across the river also means the low-lying blocks take on water in a heavy spring, so bed grading and downspout runs come up as often as mowing does.
