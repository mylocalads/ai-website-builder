Pittston sits between us and Scranton, which makes it an easy stop on the route north. The borough runs down to the Susquehanna, so the riverside blocks deal with real water pressure in spring — the drainage and erosion work we do here is often as much of the job as the lawn itself.

Uphill the lots open out and the mowing gets straightforward again.
