Scranton is the one market we serve outside Luzerne County, about twenty minutes up the valley. It is the biggest city on our route, and the work skews commercial — parking lots and walkways that need plowing and salting through the winter.

Alongside that we run residential maintenance in the Hill Section and Green Ridge, where the older properties come with mature trees and the leaf volume to match.
