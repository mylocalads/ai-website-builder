Plains is home — our yard is on North Main Street in Hilldale, so this is the shortest drive we make and usually the fastest we can get to a property.

The mix here runs from tight township lots to larger parcels backing onto the hillside, which means both weekly maintenance routes and the bigger grading and retaining-wall work that sloping ground tends to need.
