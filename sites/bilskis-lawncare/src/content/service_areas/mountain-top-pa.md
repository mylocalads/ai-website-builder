Mountain Top runs a few hundred feet above the valley floor and it behaves like it — the growing season starts later, the snow arrives earlier and lies longer, and the lots are larger with more tree cover than anything down in the boroughs.

That combination means bigger mowing areas, serious autumn leaf volume, and hardscape work on ground that is rarely flat.
