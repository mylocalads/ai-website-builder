Landscaping covers everything between the mowing and the hardscape. Mulch installs with the beds properly edged and re-cut first, so the line stays sharp instead of blurring into the lawn by July. Shrub and perennial planting chosen for what actually survives a Northeastern Pennsylvania winter. Full leaf clean-ups in autumn and the reset in spring.

A good share of our landscaping work is really water work — underground downspout extensions, drains and re-grading to stop the erosion that keeps washing out the same corner of a yard every spring. Fixing where the water goes is usually cheaper than re-seeding the damage year after year.
