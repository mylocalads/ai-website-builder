Lawn maintenance is where Bilski's started and it is still the backbone of what we do. You get a set day of the week, a crew that turns up on it, and a property that looks maintained rather than merely cut.

Every visit includes the mow, the trim around beds, walls and posts, crisp edging where the grass meets hard surfaces, and a blow-down of the drive, walks and patio before we load up. Clippings and debris leave with us.

We also handle hedge trimming and the two clean-ups that matter most around here — the spring reset after winter, and the autumn leaf work that gets away from most homeowners by mid-October.
