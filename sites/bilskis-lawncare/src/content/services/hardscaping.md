Hardscaping is the part of a property where shortcuts show up two winters later. A paver patio laid on a thin, poorly compacted base heaves and separates the first time the ground freezes hard. A retaining wall built without drainage behind it leans out as soon as the soil saturates.

So that is where our work goes. We excavate and compact a proper base, install the drainage stone and pipe behind walls, and set pavers and block to line before backfilling. What you see is the patio, the walkway, the steps or the wall — what decides whether it is still straight in ten years is underneath it.
