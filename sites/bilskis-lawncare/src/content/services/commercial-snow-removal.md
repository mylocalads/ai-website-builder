A commercial property that cannot be parked on or walked into is closed, whatever the sign on the door says. Our snow work is built around that: we plow lots and clear sidewalks overnight and early morning so your site is open and safe to walk before the first customer or employee turns up.

We hold seasonal contracts for commercial parking lots and walkways across Luzerne County, including salting and ice control on the freeze-thaw cycles that make the mornings after a storm more dangerous than the storm itself. Coverage is arranged before the season starts, so there is no scramble once the first storm is already forecast.
