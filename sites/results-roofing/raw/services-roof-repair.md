#### Tips For Healthy Roof Maintenance

The general rule of thumb in the industry is to take a good look at your roof every spring and assess any damage that may require a roof repair. The roof provides your home with more protection than any other part of your house. Keep an eye out for the following while you’re inspecting:

- branches too close to the roofline
- faulty caulking around the chimney or vent pipes (to prevent water leaks)
- moss and mold growing under shingles
- soft spots on shingles

#### OTHER TIPS FOR YOUR ROOF HEALTH

You can also give some TLC to your attic! Without proper insulation, snow and ice on your roof can start to melt at the lowest layer and let water refreeze around your shingles. Insulation in your attic protects that because it keeps the heat trapped in your home and away from ice on your roof! It also keeps your air conditioning and heating systems more efficient, which directly benefits your bottom line.
