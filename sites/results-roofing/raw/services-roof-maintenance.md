#### Why You Need Routine Roof Maintenance

Seasonal storms and scorching summers make it so that regular roof maintenance in the Dallas area is essential. However, there’s way more to a healthy roof than checking after a storm. These are some of the other areas that need to be inspected and maintained:

- Debris like leaves and branches need to be removed from your roof regularly. Otherwise, they’ll start to gather moisture, which can compromise the integrity of your roof.
- Moss and mold growth need to be inspected and handled appropriately. The more mold is allowed to grow, the farther it will spread, eating whatever gets in its way.
- Chimneys, skylights and vent openings should be cleared of all debris. Otherwise, it will keep piling up until they end up falling right through.
- Blockages and miscellaneous items in gutters, soffits, downspouts and fascia need to be removed. Leaving them there causes them to build up, which can cause issues with pests, moisture and more.
