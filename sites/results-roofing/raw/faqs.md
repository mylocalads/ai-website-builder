# FAQs

## What are the potential consequences of neglecting hail damage repairs?

Neglecting hail damage repairs can lead to compromised structural integrity, leaks, and further deterioration of your roof, potentially increasing repair costs in the long run.

## What are the common signs of storm damage that homeowners should look out for?

Common signs of storm damage include cracked or missing shingles, dents on metal surfaces, water stains on ceilings, and granule buildup in gutters.

## What does a typical roof inspection involve?

A routine roof inspection includes checking for shingle condition, flashing integrity, and gutter functionality and identifying visible signs of damage or wear.

## How can I determine if my roof needs to be replaced?

Consult a professional roofer to assess the extent of damage, but factors such as age, significant shingle deterioration, and recurrent leaks may indicate the need for a roof replacement.

## How can regular maintenance prolong the lifespan of my roof?

Routine maintenance involves cleaning gutters, trimming overhanging branches, inspecting for shingle damage, and addressing minor issues promptly to extend your roof’s lifespan.
