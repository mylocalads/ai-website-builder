#### Signs Your Roof May Need Replacing

The last thing you want to do is replace your roof and then find out it wasn’t necessary. Work with professional roofing contractors who inspect your roof to make sure a roof replacement is right for you. Here are some red flags it’s time to call in the pros:

- You are seeing visual signs of deterioration.
- Your neighbors who moved in around the same time you did are getting their roofs replaced.
- Repairing your roof costs more than replacing it would.
- Your roof is over 25 years old.
