faqs:
  - q: How does regular maintenance prolong the life of my roof?
    a: Cleaning gutters, trimming overhanging branches, inspecting for shingle damage and addressing minor issues promptly all extend a roof's lifespan. The general rule of thumb in the industry is a good look every spring, plus a check after any significant storm.
  - q: How often should I have the roof inspected?
    a: Once a year is the baseline, and again after any storm with hail or high wind in your area. Homeowners who keep a regular inspection record are also in a far stronger position if they ever need to file a claim.
  - q: Why does my attic matter to my roof?
    a: A great deal. Without proper insulation and ventilation, attic heat drives ice melt and refreeze in winter and bakes shingles from underneath in summer. It also makes your HVAC work harder, which shows up directly on your bill.
  - q: Do you offer maintenance for roofs you did not install?
    a: Yes. We maintain roofs regardless of who put them on, and we will tell you honestly what condition the existing installation is in.
