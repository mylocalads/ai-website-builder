faqs:
  - q: What does a typical roof inspection involve?
    a: Checking shingle condition, flashing integrity and gutter function, and identifying any visible signs of damage or wear. We photograph what we find and email you the report, often before we leave the property.
  - q: My ceiling is stained but I cannot see damage from the ground. What now?
    a: Water travels. The stain is rarely directly beneath the entry point, which is why leak diagnosis matters more than patching what looks obvious. We trace it from the attic side as well as the roof, so the repair addresses the actual source rather than the symptom.
  - q: What should I be checking for myself each spring?
    a: Branches too close to the roofline, faulty caulking around chimneys and vent pipes, moss or mold growing under shingles, and soft spots underfoot. Any of those are worth a professional look before they turn into a leak.
  - q: Is the repair covered by a warranty?
    a: Yes — our workmanship warranty covers the repair itself. If the same spot leaks again and the cause is our work, we come back and make it right.
