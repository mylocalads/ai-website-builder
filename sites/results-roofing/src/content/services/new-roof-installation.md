faqs:
  - q: What should I look for in a new roof installation team?
    a: Licensed crews, a free written estimate, inspections both before and after the installation, and a warranty you can actually make a claim against. Ask how long the company has been in business relative to the warranty length — a twenty-year warranty from a two-year-old company is worth exactly as much as the company's survival.
  - q: Does a better roof actually lower my energy bills?
    a: It can, meaningfully. Ventilation and decking choices affect how much heat your attic holds, which drives how hard your air conditioning works through a Texas summer. We specify for the climate rather than to the minimum code.
  - q: Shingles or metal?
    a: Depends on budget and how long you plan to stay. Metal typically costs more up front and lasts considerably longer, and homeowners in this region consistently report it running cooler. We will price both against your actual roof rather than a generic comparison.
  - q: Can you coordinate with my builder's schedule?
    a: Yes. New construction has dependencies in both directions, and we schedule around framing and inspection milestones so the dry-in happens when it needs to.
