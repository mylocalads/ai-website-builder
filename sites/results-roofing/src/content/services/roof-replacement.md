faqs:
  - q: How can I tell if I need a replacement rather than a repair?
    a: Visible deterioration across the roof, neighbors on the same build cycle replacing theirs, repair costs approaching replacement cost, or a roof over 25 years old are all red flags. Get it inspected before you commit — the last thing you want is to replace a roof that did not need it, and we will tell you if that is the case.
  - q: How many layers of shingles can my roof have?
    a: Texas code caps a roof at two layers. If you already have two, a full tear-off is the only legal option — there is no third overlay. We confirm your layer count during the inspection so the scope is right before anyone quotes it.
  - q: Are Class 4 impact-resistant shingles worth the upgrade?
    a: Often, in hail country. They cost more up front and many Texas carriers offer a premium discount for them, so the payback depends on your policy. Ask your agent what the discount would be on your specific policy and we will price both options.
  - q: How long does a replacement take?
    a: Most residential roofs are a one to two day job once materials are on site. Weather, roof complexity and decking condition can extend that, and we tell you up front if we expect it to.
