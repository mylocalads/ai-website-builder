faqs:
  - q: Should the house be empty when you clean it?
    a: Empty is ideal, because it lets us reach inside drawers, shelves, and cabinets — the areas that are unreachable once a home is furnished. If the move is staged and some rooms are still full, tell us at the estimate and we will plan the visit around it.
  - q: Do you clean vacation rentals between guests?
    a: Yes. Vacation rental turnovers are part of our residential service, and the same one-cleaner rule applies — you get someone who knows the property rather than a rotating crew.
  - q: Can you handle post-construction dust?
    a: Yes, we take post-construction projects. Fine construction dust settles for days, so we will be straight with you at the estimate about what a single visit can realistically achieve versus what needs a second pass.
