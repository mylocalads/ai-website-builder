faqs:
  - q: How is this different from a General Cleaning?
    a: "A General Cleaning is a maintenance clean — it keeps an already-clean home at standard. The Deluxe is done by hand, with a deliberate focus on the build-up a maintenance clean does not reach: cabinet fronts, door frames, switch plates, blinds, baseboards, and double-scrubbed shower tile."
  - q: Do I have to start with the Deluxe?
    a: Not if you have had a professional cleaning within the last four weeks — in that case a General Cleaning is the right fit. Otherwise we strongly recommend starting here, because it sets the baseline that makes every recurring visit afterwards efficient and consistent.
  - q: Is there anything the Deluxe does not cover?
    a: We do not climb higher than a two-step ladder, so most chandeliers and vaulted-ceiling fans are dusted with an extension duster rather than hand cleaned. We cannot move furniture, and heavily built-up mini-blinds may need ultrasonic cleaning, which we can arrange on request. Expect some dust resettlement after we leave — we limit it, but we cannot prevent it entirely.
