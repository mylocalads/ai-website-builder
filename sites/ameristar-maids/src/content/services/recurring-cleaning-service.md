faqs:
  - q: Do I get the same cleaner every time?
    a: Yes — that is the whole point of how we run recurring service. One maid, same maid, every time. Your home is assigned to a cleaner who learns it and returns to it, rather than being handed to whoever is available that week.
  - q: Why do I need a deep clean before starting?
    a: An initial Top-to-Bottom Deluxe Cleaning brings the home up to a defined standard. Once it is there, maintaining it on a schedule is efficient and consistent. Skipping that step means every recurring visit is spent fighting build-up instead of maintaining a clean home.
  - q: How do I choose between weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly?
    a: It depends on household size, pets, and how much traffic the home sees. We will walk it with you at the free in-home estimate and give you an honest recommendation — plenty of our clients do fine on bi-weekly.
  - q: What happens if I need to skip a visit?
    a: Call us and we will move it. Recurring service is a schedule, not a lock-in — you can adjust the cadence or pause it as your year changes.
