Residential Cleaning Services
Residential House Cleaning and Maid Service. Home is where the heart is!
It’s also where everyday dirt and grime lie. House cleaning is an unavoidable part of homeownership and one that is often hard to fit into a busy schedule. Maintaining a clean home shouldn’t be sacrificed due to a busy schedule. AmeriStar Maids is here to help. We are a total-home cleaning solution that combines reliable and dedicated service with the most advanced cleaning tools and technologies for a cleaning unlike any your home has experienced.
We provide residential cleaning for:
Homes
Apartments
Move-ins and move-outs
Vacation rentals
Post-construction projects

Customized Home Cleaning Plans
Our house cleaning service starts before any dusting or vacuuming takes place. It begins with our free in-home estimate. We care about what’s important to you- that’s why we take the time to visit your home and create a custom cleaning plan to fit your needs and your home. We never take a one-size-fits-all approach.
If we need to avoid an area- like the guest room- we will. If you want special attention paid to another- like a thorough deep clean of the kid’s playroom- we can do that, too. We will address all your concerns and expectations before we lift a finger, so we know what you want- and you understand what we provide.
Residential Cleaning to Fit Your Schedule
We clean your home on your schedule, not the other way around. Whether you need an occasional one-time deep clean or regular recurring service, our home cleaning professionals bring the same level of service to every project. Our one-time cleanings are perfect before or after a big event, while our recurring cleanings are optimal for busy families who can’t find the time for routine house chores. Whatever your needs, we can schedule a service that will leave you with a clean home and a satisfied smile.
