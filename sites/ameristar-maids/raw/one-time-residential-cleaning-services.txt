One Time Cleaning Services
We offer two types of one time cleans at AmeriStar Maids our signature Top to Bottom Deluxe Clean and a General Clean.
Its our job to help you choose the right level of service for your needs.
Choosing the right type of service we recommend that all recurring customers start with our signature Top to Bottom Deluxe Cleaning bringing your home to the level where we can easily and efficiently maintain our standard of cleanliness. Our Top to Bottom Deluxe Cleaning is also great option when getting ready for a special occasion, spring cleaning or when you just haven’t had the time to keep up.
General Cleaning Items include the following:
Ceiling fans & light fixtures (dusted)
Cobwebs removed
Blinds, windowsills, & lock ledges (dusted)
Moldings & woodwork (dusted)
Baseboards (dusted)
Lamps & lampshades (dusted)
All sinks (scrubbed/disinfected)
Tub/shower & tiles (scrubbed)
Wastebaskets (emptied/washed/relined)
Stairs vacuumed &/or mopped
Microwave exterior & interior cleaned
Pictures & knick-knacks (dusted)
Furniture (dusted & polished)
Top of refrigerator (dusted)
Outside of appliances cleaned
Stovetop & drip pans scrubbed
Counter tops & backsplashes (washed)
Mirrors cleaned
All bathroom counters & fixtures (sanitized)
Beds made,1 set of sheets changed
Floors (vacuumed &/or mopped)
Top to Bottom Deluxe Cleaning list items (shown below) cleaned by hand with a detailed emphasis and focus on build up, plus the following:
All Cabinet fronts (hand washed or wiped)
Vacant homes: drawers, empty shelves, inside cabinets (hand washed or wiped)
All Doors and door frames (hand washed or wiped)
Light switch plates (hand washed)
Counter tops & backsplashes (hand washed)
Mirrors (cleaned)
Tub/shower & tiles (double scrubbed for buildup)
All bathroom counters & fixtures (sanitized)
Beds made,1 set of sheets changed
Stairs vacuumed &/or mopped
Floors (vacuumed &/or mopped)
Uncluttered shelves (hand wiped)
Furniture cleaned & polished
Cobwebs removed
Light fixtures within 2-step ladder reach (hand washed in place/not removed)
Blinds (hand washed or wiped)
Pictures & knick-knacks (hand wiped)
Top of refrigerator (hand washed or wiped)
Outside of appliances cleaned / inside microwave
Stovetop, grates or drip pans scrubbed
Moldings & woodwork, windowsills, & lock ledges (hand washed or wiped)
Baseboards (hand washed or wiped, reachable w/out moving furniture)
Ceiling fan blades within 2-step ladder reach (hand washed or wiped)
Wastebaskets (emptied/washed/relined)
It is presumed that the home will be in order and somewhat picked up to facilitate deep cleaning. We’ll focus on your house while you focus on your free time, allowing you to spend more time with activities you enjoy, and time with loved ones, family and friends.
