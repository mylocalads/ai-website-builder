Recurring Services
A recurring clean is the best way to keep your home always looking its best.
Choose from Weekly (once a week), Bi-weekly (once every two weeks) or Monthly (once every 4 weeks).
We can help you decide which would best fit your family’s needs. To establish recurring service, an initial one-time deep cleaning is performed to get your home to the level where we can easily and efficiently maintain our standard of cleanliness. After the initial cleaning, we will continue to regularly clean your home for as long as you like. AmeriStar Maids’ attention to detail is what homeowners love about our service.
