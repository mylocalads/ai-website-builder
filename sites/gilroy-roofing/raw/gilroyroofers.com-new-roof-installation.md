What Materials Do You Use For New Roof Installations?

At Gilroy Construction, we provide both asphalt and rubber roof installations for new roof constructions. Asphalt shingles are a popular choice for new roofs because they are durable, cost-effective, and easy to maintain. They are also available in a variety of colors and styles to match the aesthetic of your home or business. Rubber roofing, also known as EPDM (ethylene propylene diene monomer) roofing, is a cost-effective and energy-efficient option that is particularly suitable for flat or low-sloped roofs. It is a flexible and durable material that is resistant to extreme temperatures, UV radiation, and water.

Our team of experienced professionals will work with you to determine the best material for your new roof construction based on your specific needs and budget. Call (570) 938-5324 for a free estimate for your new roof installation needs today.

How Much Does It Cost To Install A New Roof?

The cost of a new roof construction and new roof installation for asphalt roofs can vary widely depending on a number of factors, such as the size and pitch of the roof, the materials being used, and the location of the property. On average, a new asphalt roof construction and installation can cost between $5,000 and $10,000 for a standard-sized residential property.

The cost of a new roof construction and new roof installation for rubber roofs can also vary widely depending on a number of factors, such as the size and pitch of the roof, the materials being used, and the location of the property. On average, a new rubber roof construction and installation can cost between $7,000 and $15,000 for a standard-sized residential property. It is important to note that these estimates are only rough estimates and actual costs may vary based on specific project requirements.
