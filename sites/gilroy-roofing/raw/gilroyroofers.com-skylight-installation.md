How Much Does It Cost To Install Or Repair Skylights?

The cost of a skylight installation or repair can vary widely depending on several factors, including the size and number of skylights being installed or repaired, the type of skylight material you choose, and the extent of the installation or repair work needed. In general, skylight installation costs can range from a few hundred dollars for a simple repair or installation project to several thousand dollars for a more extensive installation. Skylight repair costs can also vary depending on the extent of the damage and the type of repair work needed. Here are some factors that can impact the cost of a skylight installation or repair:

Type of skylight material: Different types of skylight materials, such as glass, acrylic, or plastic, can have different price points.

Size and number of skylights: The size and number of skylights being installed or repaired will impact the amount of material needed, which can affect the overall cost.

Extent of the installation or repair work: The amount of installation or repair work needed will also impact the cost. For example, installing new skylights on an entire roof will typically cost more than installing skylights on a small addition.

Any additional features: If you choose to add any additional features, such as electric skylights or skylights with built-in blinds, this will also impact the overall cost of the installation or repair.
