How Can I Tell If My Roof Needs To Be Replaced, Or If It Just Needs Repairs?

There are a few key signs that your roof may need to be replaced rather than repaired. These include:

Age: If your roof is more than 20-25 years old and is showing signs of wear and tear, it may be time to consider replacement.

Damage: If your roof has suffered significant damage from a storm or other event, it may be more cost-effective to replace the entire roof rather than making repairs.

Leaks: If you have persistent leaks or water damage, it may be a sign that your roof is no longer able to protect your home or business.

Sagging: If you notice sagging or unevenness in your roof, it could be a sign of structural damage that requires replacement.

What Are The Benefits Of A Rubber Roof Installation?

Rubber roofing, also known as EPDM (ethylene propylene diene monomer) roofing, is a popular choice for flat or low-sloped roofs. Some of the key benefits of a rubber roof installation include:

Durability: Rubber roofing is highly resistant to extreme weather conditions, including heat, cold, and UV radiation. It can last for 30-50 years or more with proper maintenance.

Versatility: Rubber roofing can be used on a wide range of roof types, including flat, low-sloped, and pitched roofs. It is also available in a variety of colors and styles to suit different aesthetic preferences.

Watertight: Rubber roofing is highly waterproof and can be sealed tightly to prevent leaks and water damage.

Easy installation: Rubber roofing is lightweight and easy to install, making it a cost-effective option for many homeowners.

Energy-efficient: Rubber roofing can reflect sunlight and reduce heat absorption, helping to lower energy costs and improve indoor comfort.
