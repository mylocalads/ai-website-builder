## Landscaping

# The trusted name of landscaping companies in Mountain Top, Pennsylvania

Like most things, the landscapes surrounding Northeast, PA homes and businesses look their best and naturally thrive when regularly cared for and maintained. At Stubbs Landscaping, we understand you would probably prefer enjoying time with family and friends than undertake the many landscape maintenance chores that need to be done.
Our landscape maintenance programs start with an assessment of your yard, trees, plants, flower beds, and lawn to determine the right approach to get your landscape looking its best.

## Landscape Maintenance Services

Our landscape maintenance services are extensive and include:

- Mowing, trimming, edging and blowing
- Landscape design plans
- Landscape beds preparation
- Installation of plants, trees & shrubs
- Mulch beds
- Sod installation
- Autumn and Spring seasonal clean-up

- Lawn seeding, fertilizer, mowing, and aeration
- Pruning and care of shrubs and tree care
- Sprinkler and irrigation system audits, maintenance, startups, and blowouts
- Fertilization
- Outdoor lighting installation and repair
- Drainage solutions
- Winter maintenance, snow removal, and ice management

Our maintenance services are tailored to each client and for every landscape. Whether you are a homeowner, property owner or business owner, we can help determine the sufficient amount of landscape maintenance services that are needed. Regular landscape maintenance both keeps your landscape looking its best but can also help it to become healthier and more lush over time.
