Also referred to as Soake pools, Plunge pools are shallower and smaller than a traditional swimming pool. ##### PLUNGE POOL CONSIDERATIONS

What is a Plunge Pool?

A Plunge Pool is a small deep pool, also known as a plunge pool (or a cocktail pool, a small pool or a spool). A Soake Pool is a hybrid between a traditional pool and a hot tub because it can be used year round and heated to hot tub temps in cooler weather.

What are Plunge Pools made of?

Plunge Pools are highly engineered precast concrete pools. They are fully tiled on the interior upon arrival.

What is the largest size available?

Our 7’×13’ is our largest pool. The outside dimensions of the 7’×13’ is 7’10 ” x 13’10”. The structural interior depth is 5’1” and the water depth is between 55”-57”.

Your Plunge Pool will be a great addition to your backyard!

##### EQUIPMENT NEEDS

What equipment comes with my pool?

Our package includes a variable speed pump, a cartridge filter, a salt chlorine generator, an automation panel with an app for pool control, and an LED color changing light.

Many customers choose to add either a gas heater in colder climates like New England, or heat pumps in warmer clients for an additional cost. For cold plunge pools, an AOP sanitation system and a chiller are standard and used in place of a salt water system.

The equipment can be configured in different ways, but in general, a 4’x6′ area is ample space to contain the required equipment.

What size is the propane or natural gas heater?

We use a 260,000 BTU heater.



What are the power requirements for the equipment?

Your licensed electrician will determine this, but typically 50 amps are run to the automation panel. This panel has its own electrical subpanel within it.

Heat pumps require more power. Please contact us for additional details!

How are Plunge Pools bonded?

Plunge Pools have an ETL listing for the bonding system. Each pool comes with the ETL label, and the ATM certificate and report are available upon request. Bonding lugs and copper wires are installed in NH prior to each shell being poured, and pools are tested for conductivity to ensure they pass.
