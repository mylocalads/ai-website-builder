## About

## Welcome to Stubbs Landscaping, LLC

Stubbs Landscaping, LLC is a full-service landscape design, installation, and maintenance company founded by Justin Rinehimer in 2010. When he was 16 years old, Justin started doing landscaping jobs to earn spending money. After considering a career in chemical engineering, he decided to pursue his passion for landscaping/horticulture and earn a degree from Penn College. Today he’s a proud business owner with nine full-time employees.

Our team of experienced professionals will work diligently to make sure your vision becomes a reality. From landscaping, hardscaping, and outdoor living, we are your one-stop-shop to transform your backyard into a picturesque outdoor space.

We offer full 3D renderings and videos. It’s hard to make design decisions in the dark. Our 3D rendering service lets you envision your space BEFORE the work starts. Get a feel for the look and flow of your design so you can be sure that everything is just as you’ve pictured in your head.

## Why Choose Us?

## 1\. Expert Team

Our team comprises experienced landscape architects, horticulturists, and skilled craftsmen who bring a wealth of knowledge to each project. We understand the intricacies of plant life, soil composition, and climate to create designs that thrive.



## 2\. Personalized Approach

No two landscapes are alike, and we embrace the uniqueness of each project. Our approach is tailored to your specific needs, preferences, and the natural characteristics of your outdoor space.



## 3\. Quality Materials

We believe in the lasting beauty of quality materials. From premium stones for hardscape features to the finest plants for your garden, we source and use materials that stand up to the elements and enhance the aesthetics of your property.
