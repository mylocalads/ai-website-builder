  - q: "When is the best time to plant in Northeast PA?"
    a: "Spring and early fall are the strongest windows here. Fall planting in particular gives roots time to establish while the soil is still warm and the plant is not fighting summer heat. That said, with proper watering we install through most of the growing season — the timing question matters more for some species than others."
