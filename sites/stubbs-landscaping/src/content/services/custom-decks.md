faqs:
  - q: "Composite or wood — which should I choose?"
    a: "Composite costs more up front and asks for far less of you afterwards. It needs no sealing or staining, does not splinter, resists mould better, and washes down easily, and because the boards are manufactured to a uniform colour, a replacement board years later still matches. Wood costs less initially and rewards you for maintaining it. We will walk you through both against your budget and how much upkeep you actually want."
  - q: "Do you handle the permits?"
    a: "Yes. We secure the proper permits on your behalf and hand you documentation on every deck component and product for your records. You should not be the one figuring out what your township requires."
  - q: "What warranty comes with the deck?"
