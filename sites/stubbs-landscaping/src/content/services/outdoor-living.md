faqs:
  - q: "Can I build the outdoor living area in phases?"
    a: "Yes, and it is often the smarter way to do it. We design the full space up front so the grades, drainage, utility runs, and footings are all correct from day one, then build it in stages that fit your budget. The mistake to avoid is building phase one without a plan for phase two — that is how people end up tearing out good work."
  - q: "Do outdoor kitchens need utilities run?"
    a: "It depends on the build. A built-in grill with storage may need nothing more than a gas line, while a full kitchen with a sink and fridge needs water, drainage, and electrical. We identify what your design requires during the 3D stage so the trenching happens once, before the pavers go down, rather than being cut back in later."
  - q: "Will a fire pit or fireplace hold up through the winter here?"
    a: "Built properly, yes. That means the right footing below frost depth, materials rated for exterior use and freeze-thaw, and a build that sheds water rather than trapping it. Most failed fire features we are asked to repair were built on an inadequate footing."
