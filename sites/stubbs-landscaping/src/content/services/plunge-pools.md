faqs:
  - q: "Will a plunge pool fit my yard?"
    a: "Usually, and that is the main reason people choose one. Sizes start at a 4'x4' cold plunge and the most popular full plunge is 13'x7', so they fit small lots, tight setbacks, and yards where you would rather not give up the whole lawn. We measure access as well as the footprint — the shell arrives as a single precast unit and has to be craned into place."
  - q: "How is installation different from a traditional pool?"
    a: "The shell is precast off site, so instead of months of on-site construction the pool arrives finished and is set in place. That saves a large part of the schedule and a large part of the disruption to your yard. The site work — excavation, base, utilities, and the surround — is still ours to do properly."
  - q: "Can I use it as a cold plunge?"
    a: "Yes. The 4'x4' model is purpose-built for cold plunging and its health benefits, and some clients install two side by side to run one hot and one cold for contrast hydrotherapy. The larger plunge pools are designed to stay cool in summer and warm in winter."
