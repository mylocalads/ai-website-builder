faqs:
  - q: "Why is my existing patio sinking?"
    a: "Almost always the base rather than the pavers. The usual causes are a sub-base that was too shallow for this climate, fill that was never compacted in lifts, no pitch built in so water sits under the field, or missing edge restraints that let the pavers creep outward each freeze-thaw cycle. We can often lift, re-base, and reset an existing patio rather than replacing the material outright."
  - q: "Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?"
    a: "It depends on the height and on your township. Walls above a certain height generally require a permit and sometimes an engineered design, and requirements differ across Luzerne County municipalities. We tell you what your township needs before work starts rather than after."
  - q: "How long does a patio project take?"
    a: "A straightforward patio on an accessible, reasonably flat site is usually a matter of days. Add a retaining wall, a stair run, drainage correction, or difficult machine access and it grows from there. We give you a schedule with the estimate, and if something on site changes it you hear about it from us first."
