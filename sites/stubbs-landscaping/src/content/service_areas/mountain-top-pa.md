You are up at elevation above the Wyoming Valley on shale-derived ground that runs shallow over rock in places, and very few of these lots are flat. That means most patios up here are really a retaining wall and a stair run with pavers on top, and the grade change has to be solved before the surface is even discussed. It also means the freeze-thaw season is harder than it is down in the valley, so a base that would survive in Wilkes-Barre will not necessarily survive here. We have been working this ground for sixteen years and we design for it rather than around it."
