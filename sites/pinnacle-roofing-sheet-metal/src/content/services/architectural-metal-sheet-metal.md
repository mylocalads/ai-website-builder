We install architectural metal roofing and composite aluminum panel systems, and we fabricate the copings, counterflashings, gutters, scuppers and custom trim that make a commercial roof watertight where the membrane ends, rather than subbing that work out.

faqs:
  - q: Do you fabricate the sheet metal yourselves?
    a: Yes. Copings, counterflashings, custom trim and gutter work are fabricated in-house. That is why the company is called Pinnacle Roofing & Sheet Metal rather than just Pinnacle Roofing.
  - q: Is a metal roof worth it on a commercial building here?
    a: On the right building, yes — architectural metal has a long service life and handles snow load well. On a large flat roof with a lot of rooftop equipment, a single-ply system is usually the better answer. We will tell you which one your building actually wants.
  - q: Can you do the metal work on a roof somebody else is membraning?
    a: Yes, we regularly take on the sheet metal scope for general contractors on projects where another trade is handling the field.
