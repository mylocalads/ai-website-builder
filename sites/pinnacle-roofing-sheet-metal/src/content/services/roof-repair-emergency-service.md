faqs:
  - q: How do I reach you on an active leak?
    a: Call whichever location is closer — (570) 735-7751 in Nanticoke or (570) 343-7153 in Dickson City. Tell us where the water is showing up inside and what is underneath it, so we know whether we are protecting product, equipment or people first.
  - q: Do you repair roofs you did not install?
    a: Yes. A good share of our service work is on roofs put on by somebody else. We will tell you what we find, including if the original installation is the problem.
  - q: Why do leaks show up in the middle of winter here?
    a: Freeze-thaw cycling works at seams and terminations all winter, and ice damming backs water up under flashings that were never detailed for it. That is why NEPA roofs fail at the parapet and the curb far more often than in the middle of the field.
