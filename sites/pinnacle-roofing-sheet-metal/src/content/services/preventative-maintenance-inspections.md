faqs:
  - q: How often should a commercial roof be inspected?
    a: Twice a year is the standard for this climate — once after winter and once before it — plus after any significant storm. Buildings with a lot of rooftop equipment traffic usually need more.
  - q: Will a maintenance program really protect the warranty?
    a: It is often the deciding factor. Extended warranties generally require that the roof was maintained, and a documented inspection history is what proves it. Without one, an otherwise valid claim can fail on paperwork.
  - q: Can you take over maintenance on a roof another contractor installed?
    a: Yes. We will inspect it first, tell you honestly what condition it is in and what the existing warranty appears to cover, and then quote the program from there.
