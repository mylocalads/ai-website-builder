faqs:
  - q: How disruptive is a re-roof to the people in the building?
    a: Less than most owners expect, if it is sequenced properly. We plan the phasing around your operations — which sections come off when, where the crane and the dumpsters sit, and how the building stays dry overnight — and we put that plan in the scope before you sign.
  - q: How long will it take?
    a: It scales with square footage, deck condition and weather. We give you a schedule in writing with the scope, and we tell you up front where the risk of a delay actually sits — usually the deck once it is exposed.
  - q: Can you work on new construction as the roofing subcontractor?
    a: Yes. A large share of our work is new construction for general contractors and owners across Luzerne, Lackawanna and Monroe counties.
