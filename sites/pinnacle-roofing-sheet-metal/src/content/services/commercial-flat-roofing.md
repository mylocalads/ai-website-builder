faqs:
  - q: Which membrane do you install?
    a: All types of single-ply. Which one belongs on your building depends on the deck, the occupancy, the rooftop equipment and the exposure — we spec it to the roof in front of us rather than selling one product to everybody.
  - q: Can you re-roof without a full tear-off?
    a: Sometimes. It depends entirely on the condition of the insulation and deck underneath and on what the code allows for the existing assembly. We will tell you honestly after we have opened it up and looked.
  - q: Does a white reflective roof really save money here?
    a: On a building with a large roof area, yes. We are part of the Energy Star Savings Program, and on a re-roof it is usually the cheapest efficiency upgrade available — the roof is already open.
