faqs:
  - q: Is a metal roof worth the extra cost over shingle?
    a: On a house you intend to keep, usually yes. Metal outlasts asphalt by decades, sheds snow instead of holding it, reflects summer heat rather than absorbing it, and does not lose granules. The trade-off is a higher number on day one.
  - q: What is Tilcor and why do you install it?
    a: Tilcor is stone-coated steel — the durability of metal with the appearance of tile or shake, in a range of colours and profiles. It is rated for hail, hurricane-force wind and fire, and each roof is backed by a 50-year manufacturer warranty.
  - q: Do you do agricultural and post-frame buildings?
    a: Yes. Master Rib, corrugated, 5V and the R-panel series are all standard for barns, pole buildings and outbuildings, and we supply wall panels alongside the roofing.
  - q: Is a metal roof loud in the rain?
    a: Not when it is installed over solid decking and underlayment the way we install it. The drumming people remember comes from panels fastened straight to open purlins on an old barn.
