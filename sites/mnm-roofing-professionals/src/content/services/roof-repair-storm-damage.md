faqs:
  - q: My roof is leaking right now — how fast can you get here?
    a: Call (570) 793-5715. We respond quickly to active leaks and can tarp the roof to stop water entering while we schedule the permanent repair.
  - q: Will you deal with my insurance company?
    a: Yes. We document the damage to the standard adjusters expect, provide the expert report most insurers require, and deal with them directly so you are not the one chasing the claim.
  - q: How do I know if a storm actually damaged my roof?
    a: Often you cannot from the ground. Lifted or creased shingles, granule loss in the gutters and damaged flashing all read as normal wear from a driveway. A free inspection is the only reliable way to find out, and it costs you nothing.
  - q: What causes ice dams and can you fix them?
    a: Warm air escaping into the attic melts snow that refreezes at the cold eave, backing water up under the shingles. Fixing it is a matter of ventilation and proper ice-and-water membrane at the eaves, both of which we address during a replacement.
