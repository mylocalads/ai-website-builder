faqs:
  - q: How long does a roof replacement take?
    a: Most residential replacements are finished in one to two days once materials are on site. Larger or more cut-up roofs — multiple dormers, valleys and skylights — take longer, and we tell you up front if we expect yours to.
  - q: Do I need a full replacement or will a repair do?
    a: Sometimes a repair genuinely is the right call, and we will say so. Miguel inspects the roof, shows you what he finds, and quotes repair alongside replacement wherever both are real options so you can decide with the numbers in front of you.
  - q: What happens if you find rotten decking?
    a: We replace it. Bad sheathing is only visible once the old roof is off, so it is priced per sheet and added transparently rather than buried in the original quote. Shingling over soft decking is how a new roof fails early.
  - q: Who actually does the work?
    a: Our own installers. MNM does not subcontract any part of a roof — the crew that shows up is on our payroll and was trained by Miguel, who is on site with them.
