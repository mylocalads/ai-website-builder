faqs:
  - q: Do I really need leaf guards in the Poconos?
    a: Under mature trees, yes — pine needles and leaf litter pack a gutter solid within a season, and a blocked gutter in this climate becomes an ice dam by January. Book a full roof and gutters together and we include the leaf guards free.
  - q: What is a seamless gutter?
    a: Gutter formed in one continuous run on site rather than assembled from sectional lengths. Fewer joints means fewer places to leak and fewer places for debris to catch.
  - q: Can you replace just the gutters?
    a: Yes. Gutters, downspouts and leaf guards are available on their own — you do not need to be booking a roof.
  - q: How do I know my gutters are the problem?
    a: Streaking on the siding, erosion or pooling at the foundation, water in the basement after a storm, or gutters visibly sagging away from the fascia. All of it is worth a look before it becomes a foundation problem.
