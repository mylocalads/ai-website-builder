## GoodLeap FAQ’s

The following is some general information about our financing program through GoodLeap.

### Who is GoodLeap?

GoodLeap is a loan servicer we have partnered with to offer our customers financing on their new roof and/or gutters.

### How Do I Apply?

To receive an application, or to make an appointment to apply in person, please call Samantha at (570) 793-5715. Our main showroom and office is located at 106 Pfeuffer Ct, Greentown, PA 18426. We also serve clients by appointment at our Nanticoke office (136 W Main St, Nanticoke, PA 18634) and Whitehall Township location (1133 3rd St, Whitehall Township, PA 18052).

### When Do I Receive a Decision?

Instantly. The application process should take under 10 minutes to complete. Once you submit your application a decision will be given.

### Can I Make Changes to My Loan?

Yes. Your loan decision should include an “up to” amount. If there are additional costs on your job, we can include them in your loan. Your credit will not be re-run. You will only need to resign the documents with the new amount.

### What Are Their Interest Rates?

Interest rates vary. The standard loan with GoodLeap is currently at 12.99%\* for a term of 5, 7, 10, 12 or 15 years. The current promotional loan is 0%\* for 12 months. There is also a 2nd look counteroffer program if you do not qualify for the standard loan. 2nd look loans have an interest rate of 15.49%.

### What Is the Minimum and/or Maximum I Can Apply For?

You can apply for a loan from $1,000 up to $55,000. 2nd look loans are up to $24,750.

### When is My First Payment Due?

Your first payment due date is 60 days after funding and indicated on your Closing Certificate. To have us resend your Closing Certificate, please email us at [closingcertificates@goodleap.com](http://closingcertificates@goodleap.com/). Or you can review all your loan details on your Portal.

### How Do I Make a Payment on My Loan?

If you are paying by check, please include your Loan Agreement Number on your check and mail it to: GoodLeap, PO BOX 850717 Richardson, TX 75085.

If you have set up your payments to be made by Autopay, your account will be withdrawn monthly for the exact amount of your loan payment. To view your current Autopay settings, please visit the [online Portal](https://www.billerpayments.com/app/cust/login.do?bsn=goodleapmain) from the [goodleap.com homepage](https://goodleap.com/).

### How Do I Enroll in AutoPay?

To enroll in Autopay, please enter your Portal and follow the instructions on the Autopay tab.

### Where Do I Mail Payments?

If you are paying by check, please include your Loan Agreement Number on your check and mail it to GoodLeap, PO BOX 4387, Portland, OR 97208.

### Will I Be Charged Any Prepayment Penalty?

No, GoodLeap never assesses any fee or penalty for prepayments.

### When Does Interest Begin to Accrue on My Loan?

For most of our loans, interest begins accruing from the date that GoodLeap funds your loan. Please reference your loan documents for specific dates.

### Once Approved for Credit, How Long Is It Valid?

Our credit approval is valid for 180 days from when we initially run your credit. Your loan must fund within 180 days of running your credit, or we will simply need to revalidate your credit.

\*rates subject to change. Can vary based on credit score. Promotional loan may be removed at any time.
