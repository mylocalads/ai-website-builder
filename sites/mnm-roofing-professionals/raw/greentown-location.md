# Greentown

For your roofing projects in Greentown, you can depend on MNM Roofing Professionals to deliver outstanding craftsmanship and reliable service. Located at 106 Pfeuffer Ct, Greentown, PA 18426, this main hub serves as the core of their operations for the Poconos and surrounding communities. The local team is fully equipped to handle everything from urgent leak repairs to complete new roof installations, ensuring your home or business is well-protected against the elements. They are open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

To help visualize your upcoming project, we highly encourage clients to visit our fully equipped roofing showroom. It features a comprehensive display of premium shingles, metal roofing options, gutter systems, and color samples, allowing you to see and feel the quality of our materials firsthand before making a decision. Whether you prefer an in-person consultation at our showroom or a direct evaluation of your address, our team is dedicated to providing a seamless, satisfactory experience for your home or business.
