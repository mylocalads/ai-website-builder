faqs:
  - q: How often should gutters be cleaned in North Georgia?
    a: Twice a year is the usual answer, and more often under heavy tree cover. Autumn is the one that matters most.
  - q: Can you repair gutters rather than replace them?
    a: Often, yes. Re-pitching a run, resealing joints or replacing a single section is common and much cheaper than a full replacement.
  - q: Do clogged gutters really affect the roof?
    a: Yes. Water backs up under the shingle edge and rots the decking and fascia, and standing debris at the roofline is a common starting point for the algae that streaks the shingles.
