faqs:
  - q: Will my insurance actually cover it?
    a: It depends on your policy and on how the damage is documented. The most common denial reason in Georgia is an adjuster classifying storm damage as ordinary wear and tear, which happens most often when the damage is subtle or the roof already has some age on it. We photograph and measure everything before they arrive so there is a record to point at.
  - q: A contractor offered to cover my deductible. Is that normal?
    a: No. It is illegal in Georgia under HB 423 and it exposes you as well as the contractor. It is used as a closing tactic, so treat it as a warning about how that company operates.
  - q: What is the difference between ACV and RCV on my policy?
    a: An actual cash value policy pays the depreciated value of your roof, so the settlement can land well below what replacement actually costs. A replacement cost value policy pays current replacement cost minus your deductible. It is worth checking which you have before a storm rather than after.
  - q: Can you tarp my roof right away?
    a: Yes. Emergency tarping stops further water damage and protects the claim while the rest of the process runs.
