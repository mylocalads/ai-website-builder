faqs:
  - q: Will pressure washing damage my siding?
    a: It can, which is why siding gets a low-pressure soft wash rather than a pressure wand. Driveways and concrete are different — those take genuine pressure and come up well.
  - q: Do you pressure wash roofs?
    a: Never. Pressure strips the granules off asphalt shingles and shortens the roof's life. Roofs get a soft wash, which treats the algae itself.
  - q: Will the cleaning solution kill my plants?
    a: Not the way we work. Surrounding plants get wet down before and after, which is exactly the step the crews people complain about tend to skip.
