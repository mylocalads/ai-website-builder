faqs:
  - q: What are the black streaks on my roof?
    a: Gloeocapsa magma — a blue-green algae. It feeds on the limestone in asphalt shingles, which is why it causes granule loss rather than just looking bad. In North Georgia it shows up first on the north and east slopes, where the sun is softer and moisture lingers.
  - q: Why not just pressure wash it?
    a: Pressure strips the granules that protect the shingle and shortens the roof's life, and because it removes the staining rather than the organism, the algae is back within weeks. A soft wash treats the algae itself.
  - q: How long does it stay clean?
    a: Typically three to six years after a proper soft wash, depending on tree cover and how shaded the slope is.
  - q: Will it void my shingle warranty?
    a: Soft washing is the method shingle manufacturers accept. High-pressure washing is what puts a warranty at risk.
