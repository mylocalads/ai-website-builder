faqs:
  - q: Should I replace my roof before installing solar?
    a: If the roof is near the end of its life, yes. Panels last decades. Removing and reinstalling an array so the roof underneath can be replaced is an avoidable cost, and it is the main reason we assess the roof before quoting a system.
  - q: Will mounting panels cause leaks?
    a: Not when the penetrations are flashed properly, which is roofing work rather than electrical work. That is the advantage of having a roofer do the mounting.
  - q: Can you tell me if my roof is suitable?
    a: Yes — that assessment is part of the free inspection. We will tell you the roof's remaining life and whether the pitch and orientation make sense for an array.
