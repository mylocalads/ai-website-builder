faqs:
  - q: How is volumetric different from a regular ready-mix truck?
    a: A regular ready-mix truck leaves the plant with a fixed batch — you pay for the full load whether you use it or not, and the clock is ticking on set time from the moment it's mixed. A volumetric truck carries the raw materials separately and mixes on-site, so you get fresh concrete every time, only pay for what you use, and can adjust the mix mid-pour.
  - q: Is there a minimum yardage?
    a: No hard minimum — that's the whole point. If you need one yard for a footing, we'll bring it. If you need a full day of continuous pours, we can do that too.
  - q: Can I hire your truck for my own project?
    a: Yes. We supply outside contractors, businesses, and homeowners with volumetric concrete throughout South Florida. Call us with the location, mix spec, and yardage estimate and we'll get you scheduled.
