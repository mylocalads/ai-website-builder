Dupont is where Advanced Electrical and Communications is based — our shop is on Simpson St., so if you're calling from the borough, we're close by.

Being local means we're a short drive from almost anywhere in town, for both scheduled work and the kind of electrical problem that can't wait for a normal appointment. Residential, commercial and low voltage work all run out of the same shop, so whatever the job is, it's handled by the same licensed team.
