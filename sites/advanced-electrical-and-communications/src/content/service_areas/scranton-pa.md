Scranton is one of the larger cities in our Northeastern Pennsylvania service area, and its older housing stock keeps our residential inspections busy.

A lot of Scranton homes predate modern electrical code, and it's common for us to find outdated panels, ungrounded outlets, or wiring that was fine for a much lighter electrical load than most households run today. We're trained to flag those issues as part of any service call, not just the specific problem that prompted it — and to give you a straight answer on what it would take to bring things up to a safer standard.
