Clarks Summit and the surrounding Abingtons sit at the edge of our Northeastern Pennsylvania service area, a bit further up the valley from our Dupont shop.

The area's mix of long-established homes and newer construction means the work varies — safety inspections and updates on older wiring in some neighborhoods, and standard installations and low voltage wiring in newer builds. We make the drive for both.
