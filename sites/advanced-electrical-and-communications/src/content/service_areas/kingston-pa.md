Kingston sits just across the Susquehanna from Wilkes-Barre, a reasonable drive from our Dupont shop.

Between the borough's residential streets and its commercial stretches along the main roads, we see a mix of work here — panel upgrades and repairs in homes, and smaller commercial electrical jobs for the businesses along Wyoming Avenue and the surrounding blocks. One licensed team handles both.
