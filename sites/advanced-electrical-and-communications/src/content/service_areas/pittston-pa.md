Pittston is just a few minutes from our Dupont shop, across the river, and it's one of the towns we're out in most often.

The borough's mix of older row-style housing downtown and detached homes on the slopes above it means we see two fairly different jobs depending on the block — older wiring and panels that were installed under a different code downtown, and homes with more room to work but their own share of aging electrical systems on the hillside. Either way, our licensed electricians look at what's actually there before quoting the work.
