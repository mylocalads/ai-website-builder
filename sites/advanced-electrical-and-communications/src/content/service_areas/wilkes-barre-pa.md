Wilkes-Barre is a short drive from our Dupont shop and one of the larger cities we regularly work in.

The city's mix of dense residential blocks, commercial storefronts and older industrial buildings means the work varies block to block — a panel upgrade or rewire in a residential neighborhood, code compliance and equipment hookups in a commercial space, and everything in between. We handle all three service lines — residential, commercial/industrial, and low voltage — out of the same shop, so a Wilkes-Barre job doesn't have to involve more than one contractor.
