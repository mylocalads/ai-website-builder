Electrical work for commercial buildings and industrial facilities across Dupont and the greater Northeastern Pennsylvania area.

From a single retail space to a full industrial facility, commercial electrical work has to hold up to more use and more scrutiny than a home installation — and a mistake costs more in downtime, not just repair. We handle new circuits, panel and equipment installation, lighting retrofits and ongoing maintenance, with licensed electricians trained to flag code and safety issues as part of every job, not as an afterthought.
