Structured cabling, security wiring and other low voltage systems for homes and businesses across Northeastern Pennsylvania.

As homes and businesses add more systems that run on low voltage — security cameras, access control, structured data cabling, phone and network wiring — that work increasingly sits alongside standard electrical service rather than apart from it. We handle both, which means one licensed team for the whole job instead of coordinating between separate contractors.
