Licensed residential electricians for repairs, upgrades and full rewiring across Dupont and Northeastern Pennsylvania.

Most residential calls start with something small — a flickering light, an outlet that doesn't work, a breaker that trips more than it should. We treat every one of those as worth a real look, because a minor symptom is sometimes the first sign of a wiring problem that gets more expensive, and more dangerous, the longer it sits.

Whether the job is a single repair or a full panel upgrade and rewire, our electricians are trained to point out safety hazards beyond the immediate problem, explain what they found in plain terms, and get the work done with quick, on-time service.
