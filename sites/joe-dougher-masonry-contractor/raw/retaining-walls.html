Retaining Wall Installation in Scranton/Clarks Summit/Dunmore, PA area


Retaining walls are usually thought of as utilitarian to holding back soil. But, they can also be used for aesthetic reasons, like adding interest to a landscape, or creating/separating outdoor living spaces. From structural, load bearing walls to intricate terraced walls to the simple planter box for decoration, all need to be built correctly in order to last.  That's where we come in! A retaining wall serves an important purpose, but should also look good!
