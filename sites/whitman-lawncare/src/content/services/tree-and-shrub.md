faqs:
  - q: "What is deep root feeding?"
    a: "Fertilizer is injected under pressure directly into the root zone rather than spread on the surface. Established trees and shrubs compete with turf for surface nutrients, so getting the feed below the grass roots is what actually reaches them."
  - q: "Does deer proofing actually work?"
    a: "Repellent applications reduce browsing considerably, particularly on the arborvitae, yews, and hostas deer target first. It needs reapplication through the season, especially after heavy rain and going into winter when other food is scarce."
