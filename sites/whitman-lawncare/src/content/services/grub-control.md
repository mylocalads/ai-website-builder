long_description: "White grubs are a common threat to healthy lawns, feeding on grass roots and causing significant damage if left unchecked. They are elusive pests, living and feeding beneath the soil and often going unnoticed until brown patches start to appear. If you suspect an infestation, pull back a section of turf — if the grass lifts easily, like new sod, you will usually find white grubs within the top inch of soil. Early detection matters, because untreated grubs severely weaken a lawn into dead patches and attract secondary pests like skunks and moles that dig for them. We assess the property, confirm the infestation, and apply treatment timed to the life cycle rather than to the calendar."
