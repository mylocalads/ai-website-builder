Unlike synthetic products, organic varieties release nutrients slowly into the soil, providing a steady supply of nourishment to grass and plants. They are nutrient-rich, carrying the nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium crucial for healthy growth along with beneficial microorganisms. The slow release reduces nutrient runoff and minimizes the chance of over-fertilization, which can burn a lawn outright. Over time these products improve soil structure and promote microbial activity, leading to healthier soil rather than just a temporarily greener surface. The one honest trade-off: organic fertilizers take longer to show visible results than chemical products, so we set expectations before starting a program."
