Down on the valley floor along the Susquehanna, Wilkes-Barre lawns deal with heavier, wetter soil than the hillside towns above them. Compaction and poor drainage show up as moss and thin turf long before anyone suspects a nutrient problem, and no amount of fertilizer fixes a lawn that cannot breathe.

Core aeration is the highest-value service on most properties here. Opening up the soil lets water move through instead of sitting, and gives lime and fertilizer a path to the root zone rather than leaving them on a sealed surface. Paired with a pH correction where the soil test calls for it, that combination turns around more Wilkes-Barre lawns than any product we apply.
