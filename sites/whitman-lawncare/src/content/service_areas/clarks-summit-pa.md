The Abingtons sit higher and colder than the valley floor, so the growing season starts later and ends earlier than it does in Scranton. Timing matters more here than anywhere else we work: crabgrass pre-emergent applied on the schedule that suits a Scranton lawn can be a week or two early for Clarks Summit, and going down before the soil warms wastes the application.

The trade-off is that these are some of the best-conditioned properties in the region — larger lots, better original topsoil, and homeowners who maintain them. Deer pressure is the recurring complaint. Tree and shrub programs here lean heavily on deer proofing for the arborvitae and yews that get browsed to the stem every winter.
