about_body: "Grass growing under mature hardwood behaves nothing like grass in the open. Less light means less growth, so these lawns need lighter, better-timed feeding rather than more of it, and thin shaded turf is where moss and weeds move in first. Heavy leaf drop each fall and the compaction that comes with it make core aeration the highest-value service on most properties out here. The woods also mean the heaviest tick pressure we treat anywhere in our service area, and a perimeter barrier along the tree line does more for a family than treating the open turf."
