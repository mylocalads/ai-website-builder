The upper Lackawanna Valley is the coldest ground we treat. The growing season is the shortest in our service area and winter arrives early, which shifts the weight of the program toward fall — there is less spring available for a lawn to recover in, so it needs to go into winter already strong.

That means fall core aeration, lime where the soil test calls for it, and the heaviest feeding of the year going down in autumn to build roots through the cold months. Get that sequence right in Carbondale and the lawn greens up weeks ahead of its neighbors in spring. Skip it and you spend the whole short season catching up.
