Dickson City properties tend toward smaller, denser lots, with residential lawns sitting close to the commercial frontage along the Boulevard. Compact lawns show weed pressure faster than large ones because there is simply less healthy turf to crowd invaders out — one patch of crabgrass takes up a far bigger share of a small front yard.

That makes the pre-emergent timing and a consistent feeding schedule the priority here. A dense, well-fed stand of grass is the most effective weed control there is, and on lots this size the difference between a lawn on a program and one without is visible from the street.
