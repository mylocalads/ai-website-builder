Moosic runs from the valley floor up the side of Montage Mountain, so soil and drainage change considerably within a single mile. Properties down near the river hold water and compact easily. Hillside lots sit on shallow ground over rock and dry out fast in a July stretch without rain.

Those two conditions call for different programs, which is the argument against buying a lawn treatment plan off a menu. We soil test each property and set the feeding schedule and watering guidance to what the ground there actually does, rather than assuming a Moosic address means one thing.

Wooded edges through this area also mean steady tick pressure. Perimeter barrier treatment along the tree line does more for a family's exposure than treating the open turf ever will.
