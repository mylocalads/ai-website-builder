faqs:
  - q: "What is a Miami-Dade NOA and why does it matter?"
    a: "A Notice of Acceptance is Miami-Dade's product approval, confirming a window or door has been tested to the county's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone standard. Inspectors verify the installed product against its NOA. We install only NOA-approved units and hand over the documentation."
  - q: "Do impact windows help with insurance?"
    a: "Florida insurers commonly offer wind-mitigation credits for approved opening protection. The size of the credit depends on your carrier and your whole-home mitigation report, so ask them directly — but the inspection documentation we provide is what they will want to see."
  - q: "Can you replace the doors at the same time?"
    a: "Yes — entry doors, French doors and sliding glass doors are part of the same package, and doing them together means one permit and one installation schedule."
