faqs:
  - q: How long does a deck take to build?
    a: Most residential decks run one to three weeks on site depending on size, footing work and whether there is a tear-off first. Weather is the biggest variable in this valley, and we tell you when it moves the schedule rather than letting you wonder.
  - q: Pressure-treated wood or composite?
    a: Both work here. Pressure-treated costs less up front and can be stained to whatever you want; composite costs more and gives you back the weekend you would have spent sealing it. We will walk you through the real cost difference over ten years rather than just pushing the more expensive option.
  - q: Do you do stamped concrete patios?
    a: Yes — stamped and decorative concrete patios, sidewalks and flatwork. It is one of the services people most often find us through, and it pairs well with a deck or covered porch on the same project.
  - q: Do I need a permit for a deck?
    a: Usually yes, depending on the municipality and the height. We handle permitting as part of the project, and we work it out during the planning stage rather than after the posts are in.
