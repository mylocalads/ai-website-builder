faqs:
  - q: What is pre-construction, in plain terms?
    a: It is the work of deciding exactly what is being built, out of what, for how much, and on what schedule — before anyone starts building it. Changes on paper are cheap. Changes once the walls are open are not.
  - q: How is this different from just getting a bid?
    a: A bid is a number attached to an assumption. Pre-construction removes the assumption. By the time you get our formal estimate, you have heard your own needs reflected back against your budget, you understand the scope and the workmanship standards, and you know what the process from breaking ground to completion actually looks like.
  - q: Do I need this for a small project?
    a: Not usually. It earns its keep on additions, whole-home remodels and new construction, where the number of decisions is large enough that making them in order actually saves money.
  - q: Does pre-construction lead to a firm price?
    a: It leads to a formal written estimate from us, with the scope and material selections already settled. That is what makes the number meaningful instead of a placeholder.
