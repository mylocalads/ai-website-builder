faqs:
  - q: What does your refinishing process involve?
    a: Sanding through three progressively finer grit patterns, hand scraping the edges and corners, a water-pop to open the grain so stain penetrates evenly, an oil-based stain application, and then most often three to four coats of an extremely high-quality water-based top coat. The result is glass-smooth and extremely durable. Oil-based finish is available if you prefer it.
  - q: Can my floors be refinished, or do they need replacing?
    a: Most of the time they can be refinished. It depends on how much wear layer is left above the tongue, whether there is water damage, and how many times they have been sanded before. We can tell you at the consultation.
  - q: There's carpet over my floors — is there hardwood underneath?
    a: In this area, very often yes. Coal-era homes around Nanticoke and Wilkes-Barre were commonly built with Southern Yellow Pine or oak that got carpeted over in the seventies. Pull up a corner in a closet and call us with what you find.
  - q: What finish sheens can I choose from?
    a: Multiple sheens are available, from matte through satin to semi-gloss. We will show you samples on your own wood before committing, because the same finish reads differently on red oak than it does on heart pine.
  - q: How long before I can walk on them?
    a: With the water-based top coats we use, light foot traffic is usually fine within 24 hours and furniture can go back after a few days. We will give you the exact schedule for your job before we start so you can plan around it.
