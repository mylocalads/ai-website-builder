faqs:
  - q: Do you take small remodeling jobs?
    a: Yes. Crawl space doors, window wells, a set of stair treads, an entry door — a lot of our best customers started with a job another contractor would not return a call about. Custom finish work is the business; job size is not the filter.
  - q: Can you work on older homes?
    a: That is most of what we do. The housing stock in the Wyoming Valley is old, and pre-war construction has its own rules — balloon framing, plaster, undersized joists, additions that were never flashed. We plan for what we are likely to find behind the wall instead of discovering it and stopping.
  - q: Will I get a written scope before work starts?
    a: Always. You see what is included, what is not, and what would change the number, before you commit to anything. Surprise line items are the single most common complaint homeowners have about contractors and they are entirely avoidable.
  - q: Do you handle permits and inspections?
    a: Yes, where the municipality requires them. It is part of our pre-construction work, which is exactly the stage where a permitting problem is cheap rather than expensive.
