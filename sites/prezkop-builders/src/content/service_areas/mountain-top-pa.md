about_body: Mountain Top sits several hundred feet above the valley floor, and it shows up in the work. Deeper frost, more snow load on covered structures, and a shorter outdoor build season than Wilkes-Barre gets. Footings, ledger flashing and roof framing on pavilions all get specified for that rather than for the valley. The newer housing stock here also means more full-scale additions and finished outdoor rooms than restoration work.
