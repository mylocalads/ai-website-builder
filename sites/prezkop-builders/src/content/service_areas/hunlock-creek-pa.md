Footings go into ground that moves, decks get built off grades rather than off flat yards, and access for materials is a real part of the schedule rather than an afterthought. Properties out here also tend to be older farmhouses and cabins where the interior finish work — floors, stairs, trim — is worth restoring rather than replacing.
