faqs:
  - q: How long does a custom concrete pool take to build?
    a: It depends on the design, the ground and the weather, which is why we give you a schedule after the site visit rather than before it. What we commit to is that the crew stays on the job — excavation, shell, plumbing, equipment, masonry and finish run as one continuous schedule with one point of contact.
  - q: Can you build on a sloped or rocky lot?
    a: Yes, and it is routine here. Gunite lets us shape the shell to the lot instead of forcing the lot to fit a shell. We have built pools into hillsides in Dallas Township and onto rocky ledge near Bear Creek — the dig plan just changes with the ground.
  - q: Can a spa be integrated with the pool?
    a: Yes. Spas can be stand-alone or built into the pool with a spillover. Integrated spas extend the backyard into the cold months, which in this climate is most of the year.
