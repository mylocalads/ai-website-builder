faqs:
  - q: Do I have to demolish the plaster to change the colour?
    a: Usually not. The plaster options we use can be brought back to good-as-new colour without the demolition most pool plaster requires, which is a significant part of why renovation costs less than people expect.
  - q: What does a typical renovation include?
    a: Most start with the interior surface and waterline tile, then coping, then the deck. From there it is whatever else you want changed — depth, a spa, a tanning ledge, water features, automation, lighting, or swapping cracked concrete for maintenance-free travertine pavers.
  - q: Will you renovate a pool another company built?
    a: Yes. Most of our renovation work is on pools we did not build. We assess it honestly and quote it in writing after we have looked at it.
