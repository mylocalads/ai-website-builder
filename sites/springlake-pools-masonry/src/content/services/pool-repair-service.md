faqs:
  - q: How do I know if I have a leak?
    a: Losing more water than evaporation explains is the usual first sign, along with wet spots around the deck or equipment pad. Finding it is the hard part — we use electronic listening equipment and pressure testing rather than guessing, because chasing the wrong theory costs more than diagnosing it properly.
  - q: When does a pool need resurfacing?
    a: Typically after ten to fifteen years in Pennsylvania weather. Freeze-thaw cracks plaster and leaves the surface rough underfoot. Replastering restores a smooth finish and closes small leaks at the same time.
  - q: Do you handle emergencies?
    a: Yes. A pump failure before a party or a leak draining the pool is not something that waits for a scheduling window. Call the office and we will get to you.
