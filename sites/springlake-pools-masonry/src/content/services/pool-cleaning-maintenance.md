faqs:
  - q: When should I open and close the pool?
    a: Openings generally start in April, moving with the weather. Closings should happen before a hard freeze — the whole point of winterization is getting water out of the lines before it can expand in them.
  - q: Why does chemistry need weekly attention?
    a: Water here is moderately hard, with mineral content near Harvey's Lake that causes scale and well water in Back Mountain that shifts chemical balance. pH, alkalinity and sanitizer all drift, and a pool that drifts long enough turns green fast. We test weekly and adjust as needed.
  - q: Can you inspect a pool before I buy a house?
    a: Yes. We check equipment function, safety features and code compliance, and tell you plainly what we find. Many of the issues that turn up are simple fixes when they are caught early — which is exactly what you want to know before closing.
