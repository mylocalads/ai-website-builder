faqs:
  - q: Do you subcontract the masonry?
    a: No. Coping, decking, walls, fire pits, water features and outdoor kitchens are our own crew. That is the difference between hardscape that meets the pool cleanly and hardscape that was fitted around someone else's work afterwards.
  - q: Can you do masonry work on a pool you did not build?
    a: Yes. Deck replacement, new coping, retaining walls, fire pits and outdoor kitchens are all common on existing pools, and they are often the single biggest visual change you can make without touching the shell.
  - q: What holds up best against freeze-thaw?
    a: Properly set travertine pavers and correctly bedded stone handle Pennsylvania winters far better than poured slabs that crack and heave. On renovations we frequently remove old concrete and replace it with maintenance-free pavers for exactly that reason.
