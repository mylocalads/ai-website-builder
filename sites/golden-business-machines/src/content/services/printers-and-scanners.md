faqs:
  - q: Should we buy printers outright or include them in a service agreement?
    a: It depends on volume. Low-volume devices are often cheaper to own; anything running real page counts usually costs less under a managed print agreement once toner, maintenance and downtime are counted. We will show you both numbers before you decide.
  - q: What scanner speed do we actually need?
    a: That comes down to how much backfile you are converting and how much arrives daily. A departmental desktop unit handles routine daily scanning; centralized production scanners at up to 130 ppm are for records conversion projects and high-volume intake.
  - q: Can scanned documents be searched later?
    a: Yes. Paired with Laserfiche document management, scanned files are indexed for full-text search and retrieval, so you find a document by a phrase inside it rather than by remembering where it was filed.
