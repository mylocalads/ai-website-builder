faqs:
  - q: How is IT support priced?
    a: Two ways. Pay-as-you-go suits businesses with occasional needs. Pre-paid plans are discounted, tiered by the number of hours purchased, and the hours never expire — when you run out we renew you at the same level or move you up or down as you see fit.
  - q: What happens if we get hit with ransomware?
    a: Recovery comes down to whether a clean copy of your data exists. We build a backup strategy that protects data wherever it lives — physical machines, virtual servers, offsite and cloud — so recovery means eliminating the malware, deleting the encrypted files and restoring the good copy rather than negotiating.
  - q: Can you support staff working from home?
    a: Yes. We provide secure remote access through Microsoft networking products, VPN solutions for mobile workers, and the same monitoring and patching on remote machines as on the ones in your building.
  - q: Do you replace our IT person or work alongside them?
    a: Either. Some clients use GBM as their whole IT department; others use us to supplement an internal person for projects, after-hours coverage, security or specific expertise.
