faqs:
  - q: What does managed print actually include?
    a: Metering and reporting on every device, automatic toner supply, preventative maintenance, help desk support, on-site repair service, and device optimization as your volumes change. The point is that supply ordering and equipment upkeep stop being anyone in your office's job.
  - q: How much can we expect to save?
    a: It depends entirely on where you are starting from, which is why the first step is measurement rather than a quote. We meter your existing fleet, report the real cost per page by device, and show you where volume can shift to cheaper devices or be cut without affecting the work.
  - q: Is document management worth it for a small organization?
    a: If you are storing, retrieving and approving paper regularly, yes. The value is in instant retrieval by full-text search, controlled access to sensitive records, and routing approvals automatically instead of walking a folder down the hall. It scales down as well as up.
  - q: Can this help with HIPAA or records compliance?
    a: Granular document security lets you restrict access at the folder, document, field and annotation level, with a full audit trail. Healthcare document management is one of the areas we work in most.
