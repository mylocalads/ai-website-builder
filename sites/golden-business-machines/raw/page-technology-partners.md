**Canon U.S.A., Inc.,** headquartered in Lake Success, New York, is a leader in professional business, consumer and industrial imaging equipment and information systems. With around $45.6 billion in global revenue, its parent company, Canon Inc., ranks third overall in U.S. patents registered in 2011 and is one of Fortune Magazine’s World’s Most Admired Companies in 2012. Canon develops innovative digital solutions, technologies and products that make it easy to create, manage and share images and information better, faster and more efficiently. They are leaders in rising innovative high quality business solutions, including a broad range of digital and network compatible imaging products.

Some of Canon’s products include Printers & Multifunction, Scanners, Digital Presses, Large Format Printers, Multifunction Printers/Copiers, Facsimile Machines and Printers, and Office Software.
