# Wilkes-Barre Proven IT Service Provider

Golden Business Machines (GBM) delivers Wilkes-Barre area businesses  the manpower of an entire IT Department without the need or cost of an in-house IT Staff. IT Services from GBM covers a wide array of areas in network support.

![](https://goldenbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/computer-4.png)

#### Networking Made Easy

In today’s connected environment, you need an IT support company that understands the needs of small and medium sized businesses. GBM can provide you with all of your technical needs from workstations, and servers to printers and connected multi-function devices. We offer call-to-repair services along with support contracts and IT project management.

![](https://goldenbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/secure-data-1.png)

#### Security In A Connected World

We can leverage the power of Microsoft networking products to provide you with secure remote access services, so you can access your data from virtually anywhere. We can also provide you with secure VPN solutions for mobile workers, CIPA compliant firewalls, Enterprise Anti-Virus, and Anti-Spam flittering.

#### Custom Application Development

GBM can provide complete turnkey solutions specific to your business needs, or use our expertise at the level where it’s needed. Whether your needs are to supplement current development or to have GBM develop an entire database package, we can help with your overall database development plans.

![](https://goldenbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/best-price-1.png)

#### Flexible Pricing

We offer various plans that can accommodate your IT support budget. Some customers prefer a pay-as-you-go model. Others prefer the pre-paid support plans. Pre-paid plans offer discounts over pay-as-you-go and even offer tiered pricing based on the number of hours you purchase. The best part is that hours never expire. When you run out of hours we can renew you at the current level or move you up or down as you see fit.
