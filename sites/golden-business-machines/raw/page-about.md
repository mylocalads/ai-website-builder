# About Us

Golden Business Machines, Inc. (GBM) is the local leader in providing businesses of all sizes with office copiers, printers, software solutions, managed IT services and top-notch services throughout Northeastern and Central Pennsylvania for over 50 years.

Founded by Joseph R. Bradley in 1969, GBM is a locally owned business headquartered in Kingston, Pa. with Branch sales and service offices in Edwardsville, Scranton, Hazleton and Williamsport. This allows us to give you the attention you cannot receive from larger corporations, especially if their headquarters are located elsewhere.

GBM has always been a leader, providing customers with trusted solutions to help their organizations prosper. We became one of Canon’s first Authorized Dealers for office equipment in the nation, and were among the area’s first Microsoft Solutions Provider. Our partnership with Hewlett Packard began with printers and expanded to include computers and servers following their acquisition of Compaq. Understanding the marketplace has been the key to over 5 decades of growth, success and industry leadership. GBM is comprised of over 45 dedicated Sales and Service professionals with the expertise to provide our clients with the document solutions and products required in today’s complex business environment.
